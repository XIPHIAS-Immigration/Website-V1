# XIPHIAS SEO and Audience Recovery Playbook

## Search ownership

| Page | Primary query | Supporting queries | Intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | immigration consultants in India | best immigration consultants in India | Commercial |
| `/skilled/canada` | Canada immigration consultants in India | Canada PR consultants, Express Entry consultants India | Commercial |
| `/residency/usa` | EB-5 visa consultants in India | US immigration consultants, EB-5 investor visa | Commercial |
| `/citizenship` | citizenship by investment consultants | second citizenship consultants | Commercial |
| `/residency` | investment migration consultants in India | Golden Visa consultants India | Commercial |
| `/corporate` | corporate immigration consultants in India | business immigration services India | Commercial |
| `/skilled` | skilled immigration consultants in India | work permit consultants India | Commercial |

Do not create another indexable page for one of these primary queries without first
deciding whether it replaces the owner above. Supporting articles must link to the
owner with descriptive, natural anchor text.

## Publishing standard

Before a page is published:

1. Assign one primary query, one search intent and one topic cluster.
2. Confirm that no existing page already owns the same intent.
3. Use a descriptive title, one H1 and a useful opening answer.
4. Link to the relevant commercial page and two closely related articles.
5. Cite at least one current government or primary source for programme claims.
6. Record the author, reviewer and last-reviewed date.
7. Avoid approval guarantees, unverifiable rankings and artificial keyword repetition.
8. Update or redirect an older overlapping page instead of adding another version.

## Organic social cadence

One reviewed website article should supply the source material for the week:

| Day | LinkedIn | Instagram / Facebook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Expert point of view with article link | 30-45 second explainer |
| Tuesday | Document carousel | Story poll and question box |
| Wednesday | Case-study lesson | Five-slide checklist carousel |
| Thursday | Policy interpretation | 30-45 second myth-versus-fact video |
| Friday | Founder or advisor post | Consultation CTA and retargeting creative |

Run a live question-and-answer session every two weeks. Use client stories only with
written consent and never imply that a previous outcome guarantees another result.

## Campaign links

Every external link must use a consistent UTM structure:

```text
https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/skilled/canada?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=canada_pr_q3&utm_content=express_entry_carousel
```

Allowed source values: `linkedin`, `facebook`, `instagram`, `email`, `partner`.

Suggested medium values: `organic_social`, `paid_social`, `email`, `referral`.

Campaign names should use lowercase words separated by underscores. Content names
should identify the actual creative, not a person or audience attribute.

## Paid media sequence

1. Begin with organic publishing and website visitor retargeting.
2. Use Meta for lower-cost video engagement and retargeting.
3. Use LinkedIn organic content for professionals, founders and HR leaders.
4. Test paid LinkedIn only after a landing page converts and a qualified-lead value is known.
5. Scale a campaign only when qualified consultation cost is sustainable.

Never target or exclude people using sensitive personal attributes. Ads and landing
pages must not promise visas, jobs, residency, citizenship or investment returns.

## Weekly scorecard

Track:

- Google Search Console clicks, impressions, average position and CTR by query owner.
- Organic landing sessions and engaged sessions.
- `generate_lead` events and completed consultations.
- Qualified leads by source, campaign and programme.
- Landing-page conversion rate.
- Cost per qualified lead and cost per booked consultation.
- Lead-to-consultation and consultation-to-client conversion.

Use a 28-day comparison for operational decisions and a 90-day comparison for SEO
direction. Annotate deployments, major content updates and campaigns so ranking and
lead changes can be interpreted correctly.
