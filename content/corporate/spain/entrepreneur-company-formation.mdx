## Overview

Spain gives non-EU founders two connected steps: forming a Spanish company, usually a **Sociedad Limitada (S.L.)**, and obtaining **residence as an entrepreneur** under **Ley 14/2013**. The entrepreneur route sets **no minimum investment and no minimum job creation**. What it requires is a business plan that is innovative and/or of special economic interest for Spain, backed by a **favourable report from ENISA**.

## Forming the S.L.

- **Minimum share capital: €1**, since Ley 18/2022. €3,000 is now a threshold rather than a minimum: below it, 20% of profit must go to the legal reserve, and on liquidation the shareholders are jointly liable for any shortfall.
- **NIE** (foreigner identity number) for each foreign shareholder or director.
- **Company name certificate** from the Central Mercantile Registry, issued within 24 working hours and valid for three months, renewable.
- **Incorporation through CIRCE**, the electronic company-formation system: an S.L. can be incorporated in **1 to 10 days**.

## Official costs

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NIE (tasa 790/012) | €9.84 | National Police fee |
| Notary + Mercantile Registry: telematic S.L. with standard articles (*estatutos tipo*), capital up to €3,100 | €100 | €60 notary + €40 registry, regulated tariff |
| Notary + Mercantile Registry: other telematic S.L. | €250 | €150 notary + €100 registry, regulated tariff |
| Notary + Registry outside these regimes | Scales with capital | Regulated tariff |
| Minimum share capital | €1 | Paid into the company |

Municipal activity licences depend on the city and the activity, and translations and apostilles depend on your documents; XIPHIAS quotes them per file.

## Residence as an entrepreneur

| Stage | Duration |
|---|---|
| Entrepreneur visa, issued by the consulate | 1 year |
| Residence authorisation granted in Spain (UGE-CE) | 3 years, renewable for 3 years |

ENISA issues its report on the business plan within **10 working days**. The authorisation gives residence **in Spain**; it allows visa-free travel in the rest of the Schengen area for up to **90 days in any 180**, but it is not a right to live or work elsewhere in the EU. Your spouse or partner and children can apply to accompany you.

## Tax and social security

| Company | 2026 corporate tax rate |
|---|---|
| General rate | 25% |
| Newly created companies (first two profitable tax periods) | 15% |
| Micro-enterprises (turnover under €1 million) | 19% on the first €50,000 of profit, 21% on the rest |
| Reduced-dimension companies (*empresas de reducida dimensión*) | 23% |

A director or controlling shareholder must register with Social Security as an *autónomo societario* (RETA). The 2026 minimum contribution base, applied on annual regularisation, is **€1,424.40** a month at a rate of **31.40%**, about €447 a month at that base. It is a recurring personal cost to budget for from the start.

## Timeline

- Name certificate: within 24 working hours.
- Company incorporation through CIRCE: 1 to 10 days.
- ENISA report: within 10 working days.
- Visa or residence authorisation: consular and UGE-CE processing follow, and this is the longest stage.

## The Golden Visa is closed

Spain's residence visa for investors (the Golden Visa) was abolished by Ley Orgánica 1/2025 with effect from **3 April 2025**. Applications made after that date are inadmissible, including new family applications arising from marriages or births after 3 April 2025. Permits already granted remain valid for their term and can be renewed. For a non-EU founder, the entrepreneur route is the business-based alternative.

## Long-term residence and citizenship

After five years of continuous legal residence you can apply for long-term residence. **Spanish citizenship by residence requires ten years for Indian nationals** under article 22 of the Civil Code; the two-year period for Ibero-American and certain other nationalities does not extend to India. India does not permit dual citizenship: acquiring Spanish citizenship would end Indian citizenship under Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955. Spanish residence does not.

## Risks to plan for

- ENISA's report is the gate: a plan that is neither innovative nor of special economic interest for Spain will not receive a favourable report.
- Residence is in Spain only; Schengen travel is limited to 90 days in any 180.
- Share capital below €3,000 brings the legal-reserve rule and shareholder liability on liquidation.
- RETA contributions are a personal monthly cost for directors and controlling shareholders.
- Renewal depends on the business remaining active.
