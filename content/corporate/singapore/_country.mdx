overview: |
  Singapore lets a foreign founder own 100% of a private limited company and work in it on an Employment Pass (EP) issued by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM). The company is registered with the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA), employs you as its director or senior manager, and applies for your pass. This is the route widely called the Self-Employed Employment Pass.

  MOM assesses a founder like any other EP candidate: a qualifying salary from S$5,600 a month (S$6,000 for new applications from 1 January 2027) and at least 40 points under COMPASS. Corporate income tax is 17%, with a start-up exemption for qualifying new companies. XIPHIAS Immigration helps founders incorporate, open a bank account, file the EP and bring their family.

faq:
  - q: "Can a foreigner own a Singapore company outright?"
    a: "Yes. A foreigner can hold 100% of the shares of a Singapore private limited company. ACRA requires foreigners to register through a corporate service provider, and at least one director must be locally resident."
  - q: "Does owning a Singapore company give me the right to live there?"
    a: "No. Incorporation alone gives no right to live or work in Singapore. You need a work pass, usually an Employment Pass applied for by your company."
  - q: "What salary do I need for an Employment Pass?"
    a: "At least S$5,600 a month, or S$6,200 in financial services, rising with age to S$10,700 and S$11,800 at 45 and above. For new applications from 1 January 2027 the starting figures are S$6,000 and S$6,600."
  - q: "Can I bring my family?"
    a: "Yes. With a fixed monthly salary of at least S$6,000 you can sponsor Dependant's Passes for your legally married spouse and unmarried children under 21. Parents need a Long-Term Visit Pass, which requires a salary of at least S$12,000."
  - q: "What is the corporate tax rate in Singapore?"
    a: "17%. Qualifying new companies get a 75% exemption on the first S$100,000 and 50% on the next S$100,000 of normal chargeable income in each of their first three consecutive years of assessment."
  - q: "Can an Employment Pass lead to permanent residence?"
    a: "Employment Pass holders are eligible to apply to the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) for permanent residence. ICA publishes no minimum period or points test, and each application is decided on its merits."
