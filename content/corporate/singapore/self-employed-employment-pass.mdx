## Overview

Singapore has no pass called a "self-employed visa". What the market calls the **Self-Employed Employment Pass** is the standard **Employment Pass (EP)** issued by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM), where the employer is a Singapore company that you own. You incorporate a private limited company, the company employs you as its director or senior manager, and the company applies for your EP. You hold the shares and run the business, and MOM assesses you like any other EP candidate.

The route suits founders, business owners and senior managers who want to run a real trading, consulting, services or investment business from Singapore and live there with their family. No investment amount buys the pass, and renewal depends on the company being genuinely active. Established business owners seeking permanent residence through a qualifying investment should look at the [Singapore Global Investor Programme](/residency/singapore) instead.

## How the route works

1. **Company.** A private limited company is registered with the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA). You can hold 100% of the shares. ACRA requires foreigners to register through a corporate service provider.
2. **Resident director.** Every company needs at least one locally resident director. Until your own pass is issued, a locally resident nominee director fills that seat. A valid Employment Pass holder meets ACRA's local residency rules, so you can take the seat yourself once your EP is in hand.
3. **Secretary and address.** A company secretary must be appointed within six months of registration, and the company needs a registered office address in Singapore.
4. **Bank account.** The corporate account is opened once the bank completes its due diligence on the company and its owners.
5. **Employment Pass.** The company applies for your EP through MOM's online service, declaring your role, salary and qualifications.
6. **Arrival.** The in-principle approval gives you six months to enter Singapore and have the pass issued. Fingerprints and a photograph are registered if required, and the card follows within five working days.

## Two ways to structure the company

| | Operating business | Investment company |
|---|---|---|
| What the company does | Trades or provides services, for example wholesale and international trade, consulting, logistics or technology | Holds and manages your own capital, with you employed to manage it |
| Investment amount | No fixed amount | Our programme works with investable funds from S$2.5 million |
| Your role | Director or chief executive of the business | Director or investment manager of the company |
| Start-up tax exemption | Available if the IRAS conditions are met | Not available to investment holding companies |

The S$2.5 million figure is our working minimum for the investment company structure. It is not an MOM threshold, and it is separate from the S$10 million Global Investor Programme.

## What MOM assesses

MOM uses a two-stage test for every Employment Pass application.

**Stage 1: qualifying salary.** Your fixed monthly salary must be at least the qualifying salary for your age and sector.

| | Now | New applications from 1 January 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| All sectors except financial services | S$5,600, rising with age to S$10,700 at 45 and above | S$6,000, rising to S$11,500 |
| Financial services | S$6,200, rising with age to S$11,800 at 45 and above | S$6,600, rising to S$12,700 |

The 2027 figures apply to renewals of passes expiring from 1 January 2028.

**Stage 2: COMPASS.** The application must score at least **40 points**.

| Criterion | 20 points | 10 points | 0 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Salary, against local professionals in the sector | 90th percentile or above | 65th to under 90th percentile | Under 65th percentile |
| C2 Qualifications | Top-tier institution | Degree-equivalent qualification | No degree-equivalent qualification |
| C3 Diversity, your nationality's share of the firm's professionals | Under 5% | 5% to under 25% | 25% or more |
| C4 Support for local employment | 50th percentile or above | 20th to under 50th percentile | Under 20th percentile |

Two bonus criteria can add points: up to 20 for a job on the Shortage Occupation List, and 10 for firms that meet the Strategic Economic Priorities criteria.

**What this means for a founder.** A firm with fewer than 25 professional, managerial, executive and technical employees scores 10 points on C3 and 10 on C4 by default. A new founder-owned company therefore starts with 20 points and needs the other 20 from salary and qualifications: 10 and 10, or 20 from either one. Setting the salary is the main decision in the application, because it drives both the Stage 1 test and the C1 score.

Applicants with a fixed monthly salary of at least S$22,500 are exempt from COMPASS. MOM requires verification proof for the qualifications declared in the application, and says most verification checks are completed within 14 working days.

## The profile we look for

- Experience as a business owner, senior manager or investor that matches the role you will hold in the company.
- Around **S$300,000** of available funds to capitalise the company and cover your salary and first-year running costs. This is our planning figure for a credible application, not an official MOM minimum.
- A business plan for genuine activity through the Singapore company: customers, suppliers, contracts and a reason to be in Singapore.
- Clear source-of-funds documents for the bank and the corporate service provider.

## Family

| Family member | Pass | Your fixed monthly salary |
|---|---|---|
| Legally married spouse | Dependant's Pass | At least S$6,000 |
| Unmarried children under 21, including legally adopted children | Dependant's Pass | At least S$6,000 |
| Common-law spouse, unmarried stepchildren under 21, unmarried handicapped children aged 21 and above | Long-Term Visit Pass | At least S$6,000 |
| Parents | Long-Term Visit Pass | At least S$12,000 |

The test is your own salary, not household income. A Dependant's Pass is tied to the validity of your Employment Pass. A Dependant's Pass holder who wants to take a job in Singapore needs a work pass of their own.

## Keeping the pass

MOM publishes no turnover or headcount threshold for renewal. A renewal is assessed under the salary and COMPASS rules in force at that time, and MOM asks the employer to keep the company's turnover information up to date. In our experience a company that is not trading, or not paying the declared salary, is unlikely to be renewed. As a planning benchmark, aim for annual revenue of around S$150,000 and two local employees by the first renewal. Hiring locally also matters as the firm grows, because C3 and C4 are scored on your actual workforce once the firm has 25 or more professionals.

Budget for the running costs from the first year: accounting and annual filing, the registered address and secretary, and from the second year an office and local salaries.

## Tax in brief

- **Corporate income tax: 17%.**
- **Start-up exemption:** in each of its first three consecutive years of assessment, a qualifying new company is exempt on 75% of the first S$100,000 and 50% of the next S$100,000 of normal chargeable income. The company must be incorporated and tax resident in Singapore, with no more than 20 shareholders, all of them individuals or at least one an individual holding 10% or more. Investment holding and property development companies cannot claim it.
- **Dividends** paid by a Singapore resident company are exempt from tax in the hands of shareholders.
- **Capital gains:** Singapore does not tax gains from the disposal of assets that are capital in nature. A limited exception applies to certain foreign-sourced disposal gains of group entities without economic substance in Singapore.
- **GST is 9%.** Singapore is a low-tax jurisdiction, not a zero-tax one.
- Your Employment Pass salary is taxable income in Singapore.

Moving to Singapore does not by itself end your tax residence in your home country. Take tax advice at home before you relocate, and before you remit funds to capitalise the company.

## Permanent residence and citizenship

Employment Pass holders are eligible to apply to the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) for permanent residence. ICA weighs family ties to Singaporeans, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile and length of residency. It publishes no minimum period on a pass and no points test, though its document list asks for your last six months of payslips. ICA processes complete applications within six months, some take longer, and the application fee is S$100 per applicant.

A permanent resident aged 21 or over may apply for citizenship after at least two years. Singapore does not allow adult citizens to hold dual citizenship.

## Self-Employed EP, EntrePass or Global Investor Programme

| | Self-Employed EP | EntrePass | Global Investor Programme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who it is for | Owners running an ordinary trading, services or investment business | Founders of venture-backed or innovative-technology businesses | Established business owners and family office principals |
| Main test | Qualifying salary and 40 COMPASS points | Funding, incubator, intellectual property or track-record criteria | Investment of S$10 million, S$25 million, or a S$200 million family office |
| What you receive | Employment Pass, up to 2 years, then up to 3 | EntrePass, up to 1 year for the first pass and the first renewal, then up to 2 years | Permanent residence directly |

## Risks to plan for

- Approval is MOM's decision on each application. No adviser can promise an outcome or a processing time.
- The salary you declare must actually be paid every month, and it is taxed in Singapore.
- A nominee director is a full legal director of your company. Use a registered corporate service provider and agree the terms in writing.
- A company with no real activity is unlikely to be renewed.
- Qualifying salaries rise on 1 January 2027 for new applications.
- Bank account opening depends on each bank's own due diligence.
