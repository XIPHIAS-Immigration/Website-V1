## Overview

A **Dubai free zone company** is licensed by a free zone authority rather than by Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism. It can be wholly foreign-owned, and once it holds an establishment card it can sponsor residence permits for its owners, its employees and their families.

Three questions decide whether a free zone suits you: what the set-up will actually cost, which residence route you qualify for, and whether your income can benefit from the free zone corporate tax regime. This page answers each one.

## What it costs: three separate layers

Set-up costs in the UAE fall into three layers, and it pays to keep them apart:

1. **Government immigration fees**, charged by GDRFA Dubai and the federal ICP. These are small and published.
2. **Free zone charges**, set by each free zone authority for licensing, registration and its own services.
3. **Professional and service fees**: company formation support, PRO services and advice. These are not government fees.

### Government fees (GDRFA Dubai and ICP)

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Residence permit, private sector (GDRFA) | AED 200 |
| Knowledge and Innovation fees | AED 10 each (AED 20) |
| Inside-country fee, if the applicant is already in the UAE | AED 500 |
| Delivery | AED 20 |
| Entry permit for residence (GDRFA) | AED 200 plus 5% VAT |
| Status change inside the UAE (GDRFA) | AED 500, plus AED 10 + AED 10 |
| Establishment card (ICP) | AED 100 application + AED 100 per year + AED 100 smart services (AED 300) |
| ICP e-system subscription | AED 2,000 |

Medical fitness and Emirates ID fees are charged separately; XIPHIAS confirms them for your file.

### Free zone charges: DMCC's published schedule

DMCC is the only Dubai free zone that publishes its full schedule of charges, which makes it the clearest benchmark:

| DMCC charge | Amount |
|---|---|
| Application | AED 1,015 |
| New company registration | AED 9,000 |
| Articles of association | AED 2,020 |
| Standard trading or service licence | AED 20,265 per year |
| General trading licence | AED 50,265 per year |
| DMCC establishment card | AED 1,825 per year |
| Employment residence permit package (2 years, new, applicant outside the UAE) | AED 2,972.50 |
| Emirates ID typing | AED 351.40 (1 year) or AED 451.40 (2 years) |
| Visa quota increase | AED 500 per visa, plus AED 250 per request |

On those published figures, a DMCC company with a standard licence pays **AED 32,300** in DMCC charges in its first year, before office costs and residence permits.

Meydan Free Zone publishes "from" pricing, with licences starting at **AED 12,500**. IFZA, DAFZA, Dubai Silicon Oasis and Dubai South do not publish public fee schedules; their charges are quoted company by company, and XIPHIAS obtains a written quote before you commit.

Many free zones issue the establishment card through their own portal (DMCC charges AED 1,825 a year) rather than through a federal ICP file (AED 300 plus the AED 2,000 e-system subscription). We confirm which applies to your company.

## Residence: who gets what

- **Employees** sponsored by the company receive a **2-year** residence permit, renewable. DMCC also offers 1-year permits.
- **Owners and partners** whose contribution to the company is at least **AED 1,000,000** in cash, or its equivalent in foreign currency, can qualify for the **5-year Green Residence** as an investor or partner, renewable on the same terms.
- Owners below that threshold are usually sponsored through the company's establishment card. XIPHIAS confirms the right category and validity for your licence before filing.
- Residence validity is set by federal and GDRFA rules, not by the free zone. What differs between free zones is price and visa quota.
- GDRFA processes residence permits in **48 hours**.

## Family sponsorship

To sponsor family, the resident needs a salary of at least **AED 4,000 a month, or AED 3,000 plus accommodation**. This is a single test for the sponsor. It covers a spouse, unmarried daughters, sons under 25 and children with special needs, with no separate threshold for a wife or for children. Family members over 18 must pass a medical fitness test. A partner who sponsors family as a partner must also hold a share of at least **AED 48,000** under the partnership agreement (GDRFA).

## Corporate tax: what 0% really means

- UAE corporate tax is **0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% above**, under Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, for financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023.
- A free zone company pays 0% only if it is a **Qualifying Free Zone Person**, and then only on **Qualifying Income**. Everything else is taxed at 9%. The conditions are cumulative: adequate substance in the free zone, Qualifying Income, no election to be taxed at the standard rates, transfer-pricing compliance, and the **de minimis test**, under which non-qualifying revenue must not exceed **5% of total revenue or AED 5,000,000, whichever is lower**.
- **Qualifying activities** include manufacturing and processing of goods, trading of qualifying commodities, holding shares and securities for investment, ship ownership and operation, reinsurance, fund and wealth management, headquarter and treasury services to related parties, aircraft financing and leasing, distribution in or from a Designated Zone, and logistics.
- **Excluded activities** include any transactions with natural persons (with narrow exceptions), banking, insurance other than reinsurance, most finance and leasing, and the ownership or exploitation of immovable property.
- A typical free zone consultancy or trading company selling to individuals or to mainland UAE customers will therefore not have Qualifying Income.
- Breaching the conditions costs the regime for the current tax period and the following four.
- **Small Business Relief**, for revenue up to AED 3,000,000, applies only to tax periods ending on or before **31 December 2026**, and is not available to Qualifying Free Zone Persons.
- The UAE levies **no personal income tax**. VAT is **5%**.

## Indian tax residence: an important caution

A UAE residence visa is an immigration status. It does **not** by itself end Indian tax residence. Under **Section 6 of the Income-tax Act, 1961**, residence turns on the days you spend in India: 182 days or more in the financial year, or 60 days in the year together with 365 days over the preceding four years, with longer limits for Indian citizens who leave India for employment or who are visiting. An Indian citizen whose Indian income exceeds **Rs. 15 lakh** and who is not liable to tax in any other country is treated as resident regardless of days spent in India. This deemed-residence rule matters precisely because the UAE levies no personal income tax.

Take Indian tax advice before you relocate. Indian residents who invest in a foreign company also have Indian exchange-control and foreign-asset reporting obligations to plan for.

## Risks

- **Package prices.** Set-up "packages" combine government fees, free zone charges and service margin. Ask for each layer separately.
- **0% is not automatic.** Qualifying Free Zone Person status depends on your activities, your customers and your substance, and is lost for five tax periods if the conditions are breached.
- **Residence depends on the company.** Licence, establishment card and residence permits must all be renewed on time.
- **Grace periods.** If a residence permit is cancelled, ICP allows a grace period of 30 days as standard, 60 days where there is a guarantor or host, 90 days for skilled workers at levels 1 to 3 and for property owners, and 180 days for Golden and Green residence holders and their families. Overstaying costs **AED 50 per day**.
