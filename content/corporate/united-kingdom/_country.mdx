overview: |
  The United Kingdom gives overseas businesses two practical ways to put people on the ground. An overseas business that is not yet trading in the UK can send a senior manager or specialist on the Expansion Worker visa to set up its first branch or subsidiary. A UK company that holds a sponsor licence can employ skilled staff, including in some cases its owner or director, under the Skilled Worker route.
  Both routes depend on a Home Office sponsor licence and a genuine role. XIPHIAS assesses viability against the Home Office's tests before any fee is paid, then manages the licence, the Certificate of Sponsorship and the visa.
