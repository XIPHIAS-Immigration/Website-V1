## Overview

A Cyprus **private company limited by shares** gives an international founder an EU-incorporated company under the Companies Law, Cap. 113, with **100% foreign ownership** and **no minimum share capital**. The 2026 tax reform has reshaped the numbers: corporate tax is **15% from 1 January 2026**, stamp duty is abolished from the same date, and the annual company levy was abolished from 2024.

Incorporation is a business decision rather than an immigration one: a Cyprus company **does not give its owner a right to live in Cyprus**.

## What you need

- **Directors and a company secretary.** The company secretary is mandatory.
- A **registered office** address in Cyprus.
- Passport, proof of address and KYC and source-of-funds documents for each shareholder and director.
- A clear business purpose. Banks and the tax authorities look for real activity behind the company.

## Tax: the 2026 position

- **Corporate tax: 15%** from 1 January 2026, under the Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 as amended by the reform package published in the Official Gazette on 31 December 2025.
- **Company tax residence:** a company incorporated in Cyprus is now tax resident in Cyprus by default, even when it is managed from abroad, unless it is tax resident elsewhere. Incorporation alone still does not create the substance that a treaty partner, or your home country, will test.
- **Personal income tax bands from 2026**, relevant if you relocate: 0% up to €22,000; 20% up to €32,000; 25% up to €42,000; 30% up to €72,000; 35% above €72,000.
- **Non-domiciled individuals:** domicile is deemed acquired after residence in 17 of the last 20 years. A new election allows a flat payment of €50,000 a year for five years (€250,000 in total) to keep non-domiciled treatment.
- **Special defence contribution on dividends: 5%** for Cyprus-resident, domiciled individuals.
- The **notional interest deduction** on new equity remains available.

## Timeline

Incorporation runs through the Registrar of Companies, which offers an accelerated service for an additional €100. Overall timing depends on the Registrar's workload and on how quickly the KYC documents are complete; XIPHIAS gives you a timetable once your file is ready.

## Residence and travel

Owning or directing a Cyprus company confers **no residence right**. Residence in Cyprus is a separate application; for investors, see [Cyprus permanent residence by investment](/residency/cyprus).

Cyprus is an EU member state but **not part of the Schengen area**: controls at its internal borders have not been lifted, and no accession date has been officially set.

## Risks to plan for

- A company with no real activity in Cyprus can still be taxed there, and may fail substance tests elsewhere.
- Bank account opening depends on each bank's own due diligence.
- Annual filings are mandatory: the HE32 return, financial statements and tax returns.
- Incorporation gives no right to live in Cyprus, and Cyprus is outside the Schengen area.
