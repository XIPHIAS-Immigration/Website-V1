**Times Now** has published an opinion piece by **Varun Singh**, Founder and Managing Director of **XIPHIAS Immigration**. Titled *The American Dream On Hold*, it appeared in the Times Now Opinion section on **9 October 2026**.

## The Development Behind the Piece

On 8 October 2026, the US Department of Labor paused PERM labour certification, the first step in most employer-sponsored green cards, for eight large employers: Microsoft, Adobe, Infosys, Tata Consultancy Services, Wipro, HCLTech, Cognizant and Capgemini. Many Indian professionals in the US work for these companies.

## Key Points From the Article

- Years of work and life in the US still do not guarantee a clear path to staying, and that uncertainty weighs heavily on families.
- The pause can delay new and pending PERM cases at these employers, but it does not automatically cancel existing H-1B visas or approved green cards.
- Enforcement against employers is fair, but law-abiding workers should not pay the price for it.
- Professionals should review their H-1B validity, PERM status, I-140 approval, priority date and extension options before making any decision.
- Those with exceptional achievements may consider EB-1A or the EB-2 National Interest Waiver, and many should build a plan that includes Canada, Australia, the UAE or Europe.
