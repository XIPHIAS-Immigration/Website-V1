## XIPHIAS MD Varun Singh Speaks on Firstpost Spotlight

**Varun Singh**, Managing Director of **XIPHIAS Immigration**, appeared on **Firstpost Spotlight** with anchor **Daniela Laurel** to discuss Saudi Arabia's newly launched **One-Stop Package Visa Pilot** — a landmark initiative that allows travellers from six countries, including India, to book flights, accommodation, tourist visas, experiences, and activities through a single authorised travel provider.

The feature explored how this simplified approach to travel and visa access fits into Saudi Arabia's **Vision 2030** strategy to transform tourism into a major economic pillar beyond oil and religious travel.

## What Is the One-Stop Package Visa Pilot?

Saudi Arabia's One-Stop Package Visa is a significant shift in how international travel to the Kingdom is structured. Instead of navigating separate processes for visas, flights, hotels, and activities, travellers can now complete everything through a single authorised travel provider.

Key features of the pilot:

- **Six countries included** in the pilot programme — India is among them
- **Single-window booking** for flights, accommodation, tourist visa, and experiences
- **Less paperwork** and a simpler, faster travel experience
- **Authorised travel providers** manage the full package end to end
- **Designed to scale** — if successful, the model could be expanded globally

---

## Why This Matters for Indian Travellers

India is one of the six countries included in the pilot, making this directly relevant for Indian nationals considering travel to Saudi Arabia for tourism, leisure, or cultural experiences.

India and Saudi Arabia share strong historical and economic ties. With approximately 2.4 million Indians living and working in the Kingdom, and a large community travelling for Umrah, business, and family visits, a simplified visa process could significantly increase travel volumes.

For Indians considering Saudi Arabia as a tourism destination — whether for the Edge of the World, cultural landmarks, or major global events — the One-Stop model removes traditional friction points in the travel planning process.

---

## Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 Tourism Push

The One-Stop Package Visa Pilot is a direct product of **Vision 2030**, Saudi Arabia's long-term economic diversification strategy led by Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.

Key Vision 2030 tourism goals:
- **150 million tourist visits** annually by 2030
- Reduce economic dependence on oil revenue
- Open up the entertainment, sports, culture, and hospitality sectors
- Develop iconic destinations such as NEOM, AlUla, and the Edge of the World
- Host major global events including sporting competitions and entertainment festivals

The Edge of the World — a dramatic natural escarpment near Riyadh — has become a symbol of Saudi Arabia's new tourism identity, attracting international visitors who previously would not have considered the Kingdom as a leisure destination.

---

## New Visa Reforms Reshaping Global Access

Beyond the One-Stop Pilot, Saudi Arabia has been steadily reforming its visa framework:

- **e-Visa** for nationals of eligible countries
- **Tourist visa on arrival** for select nationalities
- **Business visitor visa** reform to support international trade
- **Premium Residency** for high-net-worth individuals and exceptional talent

These reforms, combined with the One-Stop Package Visa, signal that Saudi Arabia is positioning itself as a competitive and accessible global destination — not just for religious tourism but for a much broader international audience.
