## Overview
Employing **50 people** in Türkiye, certified by the Ministry of Labour and Social Security,
qualifies you, your spouse and your children under 18 or dependent for Turkish citizenship.

There is **no capital threshold** on this route. The requirement is the payroll itself, which makes
it a route for someone genuinely operating a Turkish business rather than placing money.

## Before anything else: what this means for an Indian passport

**India does not allow dual citizenship.** Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955 says that any
citizen of India who voluntarily acquires the citizenship of another country **shall cease to be a
citizen of India**.

Acquiring Turkish citizenship is voluntary. So your Indian citizenship ends **automatically, by
operation of law, on the day you acquire Turkish citizenship**. There is no Indian decision to
appeal, no discretion to exercise, and no way to defer it. You must surrender your Indian passport
and obtain a Surrender Certificate.

You can then apply for **Overseas Citizen of India** status. The Ministry of External Affairs is
explicit that *OCI is not to be misconstrued as dual citizenship* and confers no political rights.
As an OCI you cannot vote, cannot hold constitutional posts, do not have equality in public
employment, and **cannot acquire agricultural land, farmhouses or plantation property in India**.

This is a **replacement** of your Indian citizenship, not an addition to it. Any consultant who
tells you otherwise is either mistaken or not telling you the truth. Turkey itself permits dual
citizenship and requires no renunciation — the restriction is entirely on the Indian side.

For many families this trade is still worth it, particularly for the US E-2 route. But it is a
decision to take with your eyes open, and we would rather you heard it from us on the first page
than discovered it at the end.
