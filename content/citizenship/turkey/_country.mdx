The holding period is **three years**, not five. Our pages previously said five on several routes;
Article 20 of the regulation says three, and we have corrected them.

No route has been added or removed since December 2023.

## Before anything else: what this means for an Indian passport

**India does not allow dual citizenship.** Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955 says that any
citizen of India who voluntarily acquires the citizenship of another country **shall cease to be a
citizen of India**.

Acquiring Turkish citizenship is voluntary. So your Indian citizenship ends **automatically, by
operation of law, on the day you acquire Turkish citizenship**. There is no Indian decision to
appeal, no discretion to exercise, and no way to defer it. You must surrender your Indian passport
and obtain a Surrender Certificate.

You can then apply for **Overseas Citizen of India** status. The Ministry of External Affairs is
explicit that *OCI is not to be misconstrued as dual citizenship* and confers no political rights.
As an OCI you cannot vote, cannot hold constitutional posts, do not have equality in public
employment, and **cannot acquire agricultural land, farmhouses or plantation property in India**.

This is a **replacement** of your Indian citizenship, not an addition to it. Any consultant who
tells you otherwise is either mistaken or not telling you the truth. Turkey itself permits dual
citizenship and requires no renunciation — the restriction is entirely on the Indian side.

For many families this trade is still worth it, particularly for the US E-2 route. But it is a
decision to take with your eyes open, and we would rather you heard it from us on the first page
than discovered it at the end.

## The real reason Indian families look at Türkiye

**Türkiye is a US E-2 treaty country**, in force since 18 May 1990. India is not, and never has
been. An Indian passport cannot access the US E-2 treaty investor visa at all. A Turkish passport
can.

That is usually the whole business case. If the United States is not your goal, compare Türkiye
carefully against the [Caribbean programmes](/citizenship), several of which do not cost you your
Indian citizenship in the same way — because they are not the point of comparison people assume.

## What changed in December 2023

Property must now have **kat mülkiyeti** (condominium ownership) or **kat irtifakı** (construction
servitude) established, or be **arsa**-classified land with a building on it. Bare land and
agricultural land no longer qualify. If you were shown a plot, check this first.

## A note on two official PDFs

Two Turkish government PDFs still show the old **USD 250,000** property figure — a Migration
Management brochure and an Investment Office guide. They are out of date. The binding figure is
USD 400,000, set by Presidential Decision 5554 in Official Gazette No. 31834 and in force since
13 June 2022. If someone shows you one of those PDFs as evidence the threshold is lower, they are
reading a stale document.
