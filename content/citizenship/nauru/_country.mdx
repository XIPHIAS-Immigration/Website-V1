overview: |
  Nauru's Economic and Climate Resilience Citizenship Programme was established by the Economic and
  Climate Resilience Citizenship Act 2024 and grants a distinct statutory class of citizenship for a
  single contribution. There is no residence requirement and no property route. An interview is
  mandatory for every principal applicant, though it may be held online. The programme's own FAQ puts
  travel access at over 85 destinations, naming Hong Kong SAR, Singapore and the UAE. The United Kingdom
  withdrew visa-free access on 9 December 2025, citing the programme itself, and there is no Schengen
  access because the EU visa waiver agreement contemplated by Annex II has never been concluded.

  - q: "What about the Schengen area?"
    a: "No. Nauru appears in Annex II of Regulation (EU) 2018/1806, but the exemption applies only once an EU–Nauru visa waiver agreement enters into force, and no such agreement exists."

    a: "No. There is no minimum stay and the process can be completed without travelling to Nauru. An interview is mandatory for the principal applicant, but it may be held virtually."
