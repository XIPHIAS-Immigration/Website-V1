  Egypt grants citizenship by investment under Law No. 140 of 2019, implemented by Prime Ministerial
  Decree 3099/2019 as amended by PM Decree 876/2023 and PM Decree 3562/2023. Four routes are open: a
  USD 250,000 non-refundable contribution to the state treasury, USD 300,000 in real estate, USD 450,000
  in an investment project (USD 350,000 in the project plus USD 100,000 non-refundable), or a USD 500,000
  three-year interest-free deposit at the Central Bank. The Cabinet publishes an overall standard of six
  to twelve months. A government fee of USD 10,000 applies per application.

faq:
  - q: "How long does the Egypt Citizenship by Investment process take?"
    a: "The Egyptian Cabinet publishes a staged standard: three to six months for a preliminary decision, six months of temporary residence to execute the investment, then a final decision within three months. Its own FAQ gives the overall timeframe as six to twelve months."
  - q: "Can I include my family in the application?"
    a: "Minor children acquire the nationality with you. A spouse does not: under Articles 6 and 7 of Law 26/1975 a spouse follows a separate route that requires two years from notifying the Minister of Interior. Parents, grandparents and siblings cannot be included."
  - q: "How many countries can Egyptian citizens visit without a visa?"
    a: "Approximately 50 destinations visa-free or visa-on-arrival, per the Henley Passport Index 2026. There is no EU or UK visa waiver. Rankings are commercial indices, not government data, and they change."
  - q: "Do I need to live in Egypt to qualify for citizenship?"
    a: "There is no residence requirement to obtain or keep the citizenship. On preliminary approval you are granted a right of temporary residence in Egypt for six months to execute the investment, so the process is not entirely remote."
  - q: "What does it cost in government fees?"
    a: "A non-refundable USD 10,000 government fee, paid by bank transfer from abroad to a designated Central Bank account. It is stated per application rather than per person. Egypt does not publish a separate per-person passport issuance fee."
  - q: "Are there any restrictions on the citizenship?"
    a: "Yes. Article 9 of Law 26/1975 defers political rights for five years and parliamentary eligibility for ten years. The citizenship can be withdrawn within ten years for fraud or false statements, and within five years on conviction for a felony or an honour crime."
