### Overview
Italy’s **Digital Nomad Visa** invites freelancers and remote professionals to **live and work in Italy** while serving their clients or employer, which may be based outside Italy.  
With its unique mix of history, lifestyle, and modern connectivity, Italy offers one of Europe’s most desirable setups for digital professionals.  
**Xiphias Immigration** supports applicants with document preparation, eligibility verification, and end-to-end visa guidance.

### Why Choose Italy
- Enjoy world-renowned culture, food, and scenic living  
- Work remotely while residing legally in Italy  
- No need for a local employment contract  
- Family reunification allowed  
- One-year permit, renewable annually, with long-term residence expected after five years
