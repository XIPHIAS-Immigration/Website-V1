overview: |
  Germany is one of Europe's leading destinations for skilled professionals, with strong demand across engineering, IT and healthcare.

  Since 1 June 2024 the route for professionals without a job offer is the Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte), which replaced the previous job seeker visa. It gives up to one year in Germany to find work, allows part-time work of up to 20 hours a week and trial employment of up to two weeks per employer, and is open either to holders of a qualification fully recognised in Germany or to applicants who reach 6 points.

  Once you hold a qualifying offer, the EU Blue Card is the main destination permit: in 2026 it requires a gross annual salary of €50,700, or €45,934.20 in shortage occupations and for graduates within three years of their degree.

### Overview
Germany welcomes skilled professionals from across the world to contribute to its innovation-driven economy. Whether through the **Opportunity Card** (*Chancenkarte*) to search for work, or the **EU Blue Card** and other work-based permits once you hold an offer, qualified professionals can access a wide range of opportunities.

### Why Germany?
- Europe’s largest economy with high demand for qualified professionals  
- Clear pathways from temporary visa to **permanent residence (PR)**  
- Focus on innovation, technology, and industrial excellence  
- High quality of life and strong social benefits
