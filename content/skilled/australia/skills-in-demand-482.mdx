### Overview
The **Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482)** is Australia's main employer-sponsored work visa. An
approved Australian employer sponsors you for a position they cannot fill locally, you stay for up
to **four years**, and you transition to permanent residency from there.

**It replaced the Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) visa on 7 December 2024.** TSS applications closed
on 6 December 2024 and the TSS streams are now on Home Affairs' repealed-visa list. The subclass
number stayed the same, which is why so much out-of-date material still calls it "TSS 482".

### The three streams

| Stream | Who it fits | Speed (50% / 90% of applications) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Specialist Skills** | Any ANZSCO 2022 occupation except Major Groups 3, 7 and 8, with a salary above the Specialist Skills Income Threshold | **37 days / 78 days** |
| **Core Skills** | Occupation on the Core Skills Occupation List, salary above the Core Skills Income Threshold | 4 months / 9 months |
| **Labour Agreement** | Employers with a labour agreement in place with the government | 5 months / 9 months |

The **Specialist Skills stream is the fastest skilled visa Australia offers** — faster than any
permanent route, and faster than the points-tested visas by an order of magnitude.

### What changed for the better
The experience requirement dropped to **one year**, a full year less than under the TSS visa. That
single change opened the visa to a much larger group of mid-career applicants.

### What to watch
Three applications have to succeed, not one: your employer's **sponsorship**, the **nomination** of
your specific role, and your **visa**. Sponsorship is quick — half are decided in 11 days — but
nomination is the bottleneck, at 64 days for half of cases and up to 9 months for 90%.

And the salary thresholds matter. Both the **Core Skills Income Threshold** and the **Specialist
Skills Income Threshold** were indexed on **1 July 2026**. Any figure you have seen quoted online is
probably from the December 2024 announcement and is now out of date. Confirm the current amounts
before you negotiate a salary — the stream you qualify for depends on it.
