### Overview
The **Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (RSMS) visa – Subclass 187** was a permanent residency
visa for skilled workers nominated by employers in regional Australia. It is **closed to new
applications**, and **[subclass 494](/skilled/australia/skilled-employer-sponsored-regional-494)**
replaced it for regional employer sponsorship.

### This visa is closed to new applications

The **Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (subclass 187)** stopped accepting new applications on
**16 November 2019**. Home Affairs states it plainly on its own page: *"This visa is closed to new
applications."* The cost is listed as N/A.

The closure is written into law. The *Migration Amendment (New Skilled Regional Visas) Regulations
2019* inserted a rule into the Migration Regulations 1994 requiring that an application "must be
made before 16 November 2019", with a matching bar on employer nominations.

### The one exception

The **Temporary Residence Transition stream** is still open, but only to a narrow group. You must:

- be a **transitional subclass 457 worker or transitional subclass 482 worker** at the time you apply
- hold a substantive visa, or a Bridging visa A, B or C
- usually have worked for your employer **in regional Australia for at least 2 years full time**
- hold an **employer nomination approved in the 6 months** before you apply

No occupation list applies to this stream — eligibility follows the occupation on your most
recently held temporary skilled visa. For those eligible, the Temporary Residence Transition
stream visa application charge is **AUD 6,140**.

Home Affairs publishes **no processing time** for subclass 187. It does not appear in the
processing-times tool at all.

### What replaced it

The same 2019 regulations that closed the 187 created its replacements:

- **[Subclass 494 — Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional)](/skilled/australia/skilled-employer-sponsored-regional-494)** — the direct replacement. A regional employer nominates you; 5-year visa.
- **[Subclass 491 — Skilled Work Regional (Provisional)](/skilled/australia/skilled-work-regional-491)** — the points-tested regional route, nominated by a state or sponsored by family.
- **[Subclass 191 — Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional)](/skilled/australia/permanent-residence-skilled-regional-191)** — the permanent visa that both of the above lead to, after 3 years.
