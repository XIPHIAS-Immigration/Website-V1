### Overview
The **Global Talent Visa (Subclass 858)** provides a **direct pathway to Australian permanent residency** for **highly skilled professionals** with **international recognition** in fields such as **technology, science, engineering, research, and the arts**.  
It allows global achievers to **live, work, and contribute** to Australia’s innovation sectors while including **eligible family members** in one PR application.

### Why Choose Subclass 858
- **Fast-track PR** for globally recognized professionals  
- **No job offer or points test** required  
- **Priority visa processing** under the Global Talent Program  
- **Live, work, and study** anywhere in Australia  
- **Pathway to Australian citizenship** for you and your family

### Overview
The **National Innovation visa (subclass 858)** is a permanent residency route for people with an
**internationally recognised record of exceptional and outstanding achievement** in a profession,
a sport, the arts, or academia and research. There is **no points test, no occupation list and no
age limit**.

**This visa used to be called the Global Talent visa.** It was renamed and restructured on
**6 December 2024**, when the Migration Amendment (National Innovation Visa) Regulations 2024
commenced. The old Global Talent Independent programme no longer exists, and the old Home Affairs
page now redirects to the National Innovation visa. If a consultant is still selling you a "Global
Talent visa", they are working from pre-2025 material.

### The two things that actually decide your case
- **A Form 1000 nominator.** Someone with a **national reputation in your own field** must attest
  to your record. They must be an Australian citizen, Australian permanent resident, eligible
  New Zealand citizen, or an Australian organisation. This is the real gate.
- **An invitation.** You cannot apply unprompted. You submit an expression of interest, the
  Department invites you, and you then have **60 days** to lodge.

### Why choose subclass 858
- **Permanent residency on grant** — live, work and study anywhere in Australia
- **No points test, no occupation list, no age limit**
- **Faster than most PR routes** — 6 months for half of applications, 7 months for 90%
- **Family included** with full work and study rights
- **Pathway to Australian citizenship**

### A note on the Global Talent Employer Sponsored scheme
The **GTES** still exists, but it is not a subclass 858 product. It is an employer agreement
scheme — Established Business and Startup streams, up to 20 positions a year — that lets an
employer sponsor workers under the **[Skills in Demand (subclass 482)](/skilled/australia/skills-in-demand-482)**
visa, with a permanent residency pathway after two years and age-cap concessions.
