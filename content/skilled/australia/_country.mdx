## Which Australian route fits you

### No employer? The points-tested visas
- **[Subclass 189 — Skilled Independent](/skilled/australia/skilled-independent-189)** — no sponsor at all. 61 days for half of applications, 8 months for 90%. But cut-offs reached 80-100 in 2026.
- **[Subclass 190 — Skilled Nominated](/skilled/australia/skilled-nominated-190)** — a state nominates you, worth 5 points. Invited by the states, not in departmental rounds.
- **[Subclass 491 — Skilled Work Regional](/skilled/australia/skilled-work-regional-491)** — 15 points for state nomination or family sponsorship. 5-year visa leading to PR.

### Have an employer? These skip the points queue entirely
- **[Subclass 482 — Skills in Demand](/skilled/australia/skills-in-demand-482)** — replaced the TSS visa in December 2024. Only 1 year of experience needed. The Specialist Skills stream is decided in **37 days** for half of applications.
- **[Subclass 186 — Employer Nomination Scheme](/skilled/australia/employer-nomination-scheme-186)** — permanent residency directly, but slow: 21 to 25 months.
- **[Subclass 494 — Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional](/skilled/australia/skilled-employer-sponsored-regional-494)** — a regional employer nominates you; 5 years, then PR.

### Exceptional record in your field?
- **[Subclass 858 — National Innovation visa](/skilled/australia/national-innovation-visa-858)** — no points test, no occupation list, no age limit. A Form 1000 nominator with a national reputation in your field is mandatory, and so is a government invitation.

### Already on a regional visa?
- **[Subclass 191 — Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional)](/skilled/australia/permanent-residence-skilled-regional-191)** — the permanent visa for 491 and 494 holders after 3 years. From **AUD 630**.

### Closed
- **[Subclass 187 — Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme](/skilled/australia/regional-sponsored-migration-187)** — closed to new applications on 16 November 2019. Subclass 494 replaced it.
- **Business Innovation and Investment Program (188 and 888)** — closed permanently on 31 July 2024.
- **Subclass 132 — Business Talent** — closed on 1 July 2021 and now on Home Affairs' repealed-visa list.
- **TSS 482** — applications closed 6 December 2024; the Skills in Demand visa replaced it under the same subclass number.
