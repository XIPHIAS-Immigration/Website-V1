### Overview
The **Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS) Visa – Subclass 186** is an **Australia skilled migration program** that allows **qualified professionals** to gain **permanent residency** through **employer sponsorship**.  
This visa provides a **direct PR pathway** for those with a **valid job offer from an approved Australian employer**, allowing them to **live, work, and include family members** in their application.  
It supports **Australia’s critical skill needs** and helps employers fill **long-term skilled positions** across industries.

### Why Choose Subclass 186
- Employer nomination to secure permanent residency in Australia  
- No points test; Direct Entry, Temporary Residence Transition and Labour Agreement streams  
- Supports long-term skilled roles with genuine job offers  
- Include eligible family members for PR and Medicare access  
- Stable career pathway with national mobility after approval  
- Recognises Australian work experience and qualifications

### Visa application charge
The Home Affairs visa application charge for the primary applicant is **AUD 6,140** (as displayed
on 29 September 2026). Additional charges apply for each family member included in the
application.
