### Overview
The **Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 494)** lets an approved
Australian employer in a **designated regional area** sponsor you for a **5-year visa**, with
permanent residency available through **[subclass 191](/skilled/australia/permanent-residence-skilled-regional-191)**
after three years.

This is the visa that **replaced [subclass 187](/skilled/australia/regional-sponsored-migration-187)**,
which closed to new applications on 16 November 2019. If you were researching the 187 for regional
employer sponsorship, the 494 is what you actually want.

### Why it is often the better bet
There is **no points test**. In 2026 the points-tested subclass 189 invited most professional
occupations at **80 to 100 points**, and issued no invitations at all in the first three months of
the 2026-27 programme year. The 494 sidesteps that queue entirely — if you have a regional employer
willing to nominate you, your score is irrelevant.

It also draws on a **wider occupation list**: MLTSSL, STSOL and ROL, where subclass 189 uses MLTSSL
alone.

### The catch worth understanding before you sign
The permanent stage is **not automatic**. Subclass 191 requires you to have held the 494 for three
years **and** to have had a taxable income at or above a set threshold for at least three years
while holding it. If your regional salary sits near that threshold, the permanent residency you are
working towards is at risk. Ask an adviser to check the current threshold against the specific
offer before you accept it.
