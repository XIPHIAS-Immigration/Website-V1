### Overview
The **Permanent Residence (Skilled Regional) visa (subclass 191)** is the permanent visa that
holders of **[subclass 491](/skilled/australia/skilled-work-regional-491)** and
**[subclass 494](/skilled/australia/skilled-employer-sponsored-regional-494)** convert to after
three years in regional Australia.

Home Affairs' own description: it allows workers who hold an eligible visa and meet an income
requirement to live and work in Australia permanently. **No sponsor and no nominator are required**,
and the Regional provisional stream starts from **AUD 630** — a fraction of what Australia's other
permanent skilled visas cost, because you already paid the full charge on your provisional visa.

### The two streams

**Regional provisional stream** — for people who have lived and worked in designated regional areas
on an eligible visa. You must have held that visa for **at least 3 years**, complied with its
conditions, and had a **taxable income at or above a set threshold for at least 3 years** while
holding it. From **AUD 630**.

**Hong Kong stream** — for Hong Kong or British National (Overseas) passport holders who have
demonstrated commitment to Australia and meet certain visa and residence requirements. From
**AUD 6,135**.

### The income test decides the case
The requirement that decides your case is the **taxable income threshold, met for at least three
years**. Home Affairs does not publish the amount on the visa page, so XIPHIAS confirms the current
threshold for your case before you rely on it.

What this means practically: if you are about to accept a regional job offer on a 491 or 494, the
salary is not just about living costs. It determines whether the permanent residency you are working
towards is achievable at all. Get the current threshold checked against the specific offer
**before** you sign, not three years later.

Keep every notice of assessment. They are the evidence.
