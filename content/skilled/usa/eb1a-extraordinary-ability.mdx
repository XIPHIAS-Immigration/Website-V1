### Overview
The **EB-1A visa** is a U.S. employment-based immigration pathway for individuals with **extraordinary ability** in **science, arts, education, business, or athletics**. It offers a **direct route to permanent residency** through **self-petition**, without the need for employer sponsorship or labor certification.

### Eligibility Overview
Applicants must demonstrate that they are among the **top professionals in their field** and meet at least **three of ten USCIS criteria**, or hold a **major internationally recognized award** such as a **Nobel Prize, Olympic medal, or Academy Award**.

### Why Choose EB-1A
- **No employer or sponsor required**  
- **Faster processing** under the first-preference category  
- **Direct Green Card eligibility**  
- **Global recognition** of professional excellence

## Where India actually stands, October 2026

A petition approval is not a green card. After approval you wait for your priority date, and for
India that wait is the whole story. From the October 2026 Visa Bulletin:

| Category | India final action date | Roughly how far behind | Worldwide |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EB-1 | 01 Feb 2023 | 3 years 8 months | Current |
| EB-2 | 01 Nov 2013 | **12 years 11 months** | 01 Jan 2025 |
| EB-3 | 01 Jan 2014 | **12 years 9 months** | 15 May 2024 |
| EB-5 unreserved | 01 Dec 2023 | 2 years 10 months | Current |
| **EB-5 rural, high-unemployment, infrastructure** | **Current** | **No wait** | Current |

The Visa Bulletin also warns that some worldwide categories have **retrogressed** to hold issuance
within FY2027 limits. Dates move backwards as well as forwards.

## USCIS filing fees

| Item | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Form I-140 immigrant petition | USD 715 |
| Asylum Program Fee, paid with the I-140 | USD 600 (USD 300 small employer, nil nonprofit) |
| Form I-485 adjustment of status, applicants aged 14 and over | USD 1,440 |
| Premium processing on the I-140 (optional) | USD 2,965, response within 15 business days |

Fees follow the USCIS fee schedule at 8 CFR 106.2; the premium processing fee took effect on
1 March 2026. Applicants who complete the process at a US consulate pay the State Department's
immigrant visa fees at that stage instead of the Form I-485 fee.
