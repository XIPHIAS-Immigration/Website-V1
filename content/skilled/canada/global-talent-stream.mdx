## Overview  
The **Global Talent Stream (GTS)** allows **Canadian employers** to hire **highly skilled foreign workers** in **tech and in-demand roles**.  
It offers a **two-week work permit processing standard** under **Category A (referral)** or **Category B (occupation list)**. Permanent residence is a separate application, usually through **Express Entry** or a **Provincial Nominee Program**.  

## Why Choose the Global Talent Stream  
- **Two-week work permit processing standard**, met 80% of the time  
- **Top Canadian employer opportunities**  
- **Canadian work experience** that can support a later PR application through Express Entry or a PNP  
- **Spouse and dependents included**
