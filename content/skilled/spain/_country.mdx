  Spain's Digital Nomad Visa, the visa for international telework created by Ley 28/2022, lets non-EU professionals live in Spain while working remotely: employees for companies located outside Spain, and self-employed professionals mainly for clients outside Spain, with Spanish clients allowed up to 20% of their activity.

  The income test is 200% of the SMI, which for 2026 is €1,221 a month in 14 payments (€17,094 a year). The consular visa runs for one year, followed by a three-year residence authorisation from inside Spain, renewable for two more.
