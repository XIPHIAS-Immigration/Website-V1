### Overview

**Green List - Tier 1** is connected to New Zealand's **Straight to Residence Visa** pathway. It is for applicants who have a current job or job offer with an accredited New Zealand employer in a Tier 1 Green List role.

### Who this pathway suits

This pathway suits skilled workers whose occupation is listed in Tier 1 of the Green List and who meet the role's specific qualification, registration, wage, professional membership, or experience requirements.

### Key requirements

Applicants must be 55 or younger, have a qualifying Tier 1 Green List job or offer, meet Green List role requirements, speak and understand English, and meet health and character requirements.

### Documents required

Prepare identity documents, English evidence, medicals, police certificates, employment evidence, wage evidence, and any qualification, registration, professional membership, or experience proof required for the Green List role.

### Process

Confirm the occupation and Tier 1 requirements first, then prepare the employment and personal evidence before submitting the Straight to Residence application online.
