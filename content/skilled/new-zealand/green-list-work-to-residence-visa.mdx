### Overview

**Green List - Tier 2** is connected to New Zealand's **Work to Residence Visa** pathway. It is for applicants who have completed qualifying New Zealand work in a Tier 2 Green List role and have current employment or a job offer with an accredited New Zealand employer.

### Who this pathway suits

This pathway suits skilled workers already working in New Zealand in an eligible Tier 2 Green List occupation.

### Key requirements

Applicants must be 55 or younger, meet the 24-month qualifying work requirement, have a current Tier 2 Green List job or job offer, meet role-specific requirements, and satisfy English, health, and character rules.

### Documents required

Prepare identity, health, police, English, current job, previous work experience, wage, and Green List role-specific documents.

### Process

First confirm Tier 2 eligibility, then verify that 24 months of qualifying New Zealand work can be counted before preparing and submitting the residence application.
