### Overview
France runs its skilled migration through the multi-year **talent card**. For senior salaried professionals the relevant version is the **EU Blue Card** — a permit that follows your contract rather than the calendar, and that brings your family with you without a separate reunification process.

### Why France for skilled professionals
- Europe's second-largest economy, inside the single market and the Schengen area
- A card valid for the length of the contract, up to four years — not annual renewals
- Family included from the outset on talent-famille cards
- Portable across the EU: a year on a Blue Card elsewhere opens the French route
- Five years of residence leads to naturalisation, with French at B2 and a civics exam
