## Visa Categories to Migrate to Germany

- **EU Blue Card** for highly qualified professionals
- **Skilled Worker Visa** for qualified professionals with a job offer
- **Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)** for job seekers, which replaced the job seeker visa on 1 June 2024
- **Residence Permit for Self-Employment** for entrepreneurs and investors
- **Settlement Permit** (permanent residency)


## Benefits of Having a Germany Visa

- High-quality lifestyle and infrastructure
- Access to free education
- Outstanding medical facilities
- Strong investment and business environment
- Travel freedom across the **Schengen Area** (29 countries)


## Pathway to Citizenship and Permanent Residency

- Eligible for **German citizenship** after 5 years of residence (since June 2024)
- Entrepreneur Visa holders receive a residence permit of up to **3 years**, convertible to **Permanent Residency** after 3 years if the business succeeds
- Spouses and children (under 18) can usually join you through **family reunification**
