## Citizenship by Investment Programs

But what **is** the **Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** program? In the **CBI** program, individuals must **contribute to the host country’s economy** (through various **investment** options available). The **CBI** program is an efficient way to obtain a **second citizenship** in a different country. Individuals get to be a part of a country’s **economic development** while enjoying lucrative **business opportunities**.

But how are **Citizenship-by-Investment** programs beneficial to investors?

- The **processing time** for the **CBI** program is only a few months, making the process streamlined and hassle-free.
- Most **CBI programs** offer **visa-free** and **visa-on-arrival** travel opportunities to numerous countries, opening up several **business** opportunities.
- **CBI programs** offer **stability** and **legal protection** to the investors in the new country they’re moving to.
- Participating in **CBI programs** lets **business owners** reap the benefits of **citizenship** of multiple countries, opening up new pathways to find **resources**, **markets**, and **cultures**.

For Indian citizens, one point comes first: India does not allow dual citizenship. Under Section 9 of the Citizenship Act 1955, Indian citizenship ends automatically when another citizenship is voluntarily acquired, and the Overseas Citizenship of India (OCI) card available afterwards is not dual citizenship.

While the **Citizenship-by-Investment** program mostly caters to **high-net-worth individuals**, several **budget-friendly** options for a smooth **business** establishment are also available. Let us delve into those.

## 1. Antigua and Barbuda

[**Antigua and Barbuda**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/antigua-and-barbuda-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html) is the perfect immigration destination with a high standard of living and fun water sports for a leisurely break! This tropical island paradise is well-renowned for its breathtaking scenery and profitable **citizenship** options for **investors** and **business owners**.

A few advantages of **Antigua and Barbuda citizenship** are:

- **Visa-free** travel to many destinations, including **Europe’s Schengen Area** and **Hong Kong**, and to the **UK** with an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA).
- Reside in a **tax-friendly** jurisdiction with attractive **business** prospects.
- Passing **citizenship** on to **future generations** provides a legacy of **security** and **opportunity**.

## 2. Dominica

This mountainous Caribbean island greets non-habitants with open arms and is home to various rare flora. [**Dominica**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/dominica-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html) is famed for its rugged terrain with dense rainforests, rivers, waterfalls, and hot springs. While it is a small island nation, **Dominica** offers several **business** and **entrepreneurship** possibilities in **tourism**, **agriculture**, and **eco-friendly industries**.

A few of the reasons why **Dominica** is the immigration destination for you are:

- **Visa-free** travel to many destinations, such as **Hong Kong** and **Singapore**. A visa has been required for the **UK** since 19 July 2023.
- **Dominica** is a culturally and ethnically diverse nation that includes indigenous **Carib** people with a mix of **European** and other **Caribbean** influences.
- **Dominica** has a highly **stable political environment**, meaning power transitions have been smooth sailing over the past years.

### Investment options include

| --- | --- |
| **USD 200,000** | **Real Estate** properties, plus a government fee from USD 75,000 |

## 3. St. Kitts and Nevis

Another nation that boasts gorgeous natural scenery is **Saint Kitts and Nevis**. It is a twin-island nation situated between the **Atlantic Ocean** and the **Caribbean Sea**. **St. Kitts and Nevis** has witnessed a significant positive **economic** shift in recent years.

Why **St. Kitts and Nevis citizenship** is beneficial from an **investor** point of view:

- **Visa-free** travel to many destinations, such as the **Schengen** region, **Hong Kong** and **Singapore**, and to the **United Kingdom** with an ETA.
- The **real estate** market in **St. Kitts and Nevis** offers gainful opportunities to **investors** such as luxury properties and resort developments.
- Second **citizenship** in **St. Kitts and Nevis** lets **business owners** **diversify** their assets, which saves them from major risks during **economic** downturns in their home country.

## 4. Grenada

The Caribbean island country is known for its spices and serene wilderness—often called the **“Island of Spice.”** But **Grenada’s** proximity to nature does not make it any less technologically and economically up to the minute.

So, what advantages does the **Grenada CBI** program offer?

- **Visa-free** travel to many destinations, such as **Greece**, **Brazil** and **Spain**, and to the **UK** with an ETA.
- **Grenada** has an **E-2 treaty** with the **USA** (in force since 1989), so Grenadian citizens can apply for an E-2 visa to **invest** in and **run a business** in the **US**; each case is decided by US consular officers. It is the only one of the five Eastern Caribbean CBI countries with this treaty.
- The democratic **Grenadian** government actively executes policies to ensure a **stable economic** and **political** environment.

### Investment options include

| **USD 150,000** | **National Transformation Fund** (single applicant) |
| --- | --- |
| **USD 220,000** | **Real Estate** in projects approved at that option (USD 350,000 otherwise), plus a USD 50,000 government fee |
