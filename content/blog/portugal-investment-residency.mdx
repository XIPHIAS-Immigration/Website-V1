## Residency and Citizenship in Portugal

- Residency permit granted to **investor, spouse, and children under 18**
- Only **7 days in the first year** and **14 days in each later two-year period** required to maintain residency
- Processing currently takes around **15–18 months**
- Permanent residence after **5 years**; citizenship after **10 years** of legal residence for Indian nationals

Portugal’s **hospitality, manufacturing, and technology sectors** are growing rapidly, boosting real estate and investment opportunities.


## Portugal Investment Programs

### 1. Portugal Golden Visa – Investment Funds
- Invest **€500,000** in qualifying investment funds that capitalise Portuguese companies
- Obtain residency through the **Golden Visa Program**
- Real estate no longer qualifies: Portugal removed it from the Golden Visa in October 2023

### 2. Business Investment
- Invest in a **running enterprise** or **start a new business**
- Must create **at least 10 local jobs**
- Qualifies for residency through Golden Visa

### 3. Cultural Donation
- Donate **€250,000** to organizations supporting **cultural heritage and arts**
- Qualifies for residency under Golden Visa

### 4. Scientific Research
- Fund **research activities** in Portugal's scientific and technological system
- Minimum investment of **€500,000** (**€400,000** in low-density areas)
- Qualifies for residency under Golden Visa

### 5. Company Capitalisation
- Invest **€500,000** to set up or increase the capital of a Portuguese company
- Must create or keep at least **5 permanent jobs** for three years

## FAQs

**1. What are the main investment options for residency in Portugal?**
- Investment funds, scientific research, cultural donations, company capitalisation, and job creation. Real estate no longer qualifies.

**2. Can family members obtain residency too?**
- Yes, spouse and children under 18 are included in the residency permit.

**3. How long until I can apply for Portuguese citizenship?**
- Indian nationals need **10 years** of legal residence (Lei Orgânica 1/2026, in force since 19 May 2026); permanent residence is available after **5 years**.

**4. Does Portugal allow dual citizenship?**
- Portugal does, but India does not: an Indian citizen who acquires Portuguese citizenship ceases to be an Indian citizen under Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955.
