## UK Innovator Visa Program (now Innovator Founder)

The UK Innovator Visa Program was for experienced businesspersons looking to invest in the UK and required £50,000 of investment funds. It closed to new applicants on 13 April 2023 and was replaced by the Innovator Founder Visa. Innovator Founder applicants implement an innovative business idea in the UK; the business should be viable and scalable in the long run. There is no minimum investment, but the applicant needs an endorsement from an approved endorsing body. Through this visa, the founder and immediate family can get to live in the UK for good—first as permanent residents, later as citizens.

The applicant will have to submit a business plan proving the viability and scalability of the business. The plan should be genuine and demonstrate that it meets new and existing market needs with a competitive edge.

- Validity: 3 years
- Settlement: Eligible to apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (UK Settlement) after 3 years if the business meets the endorsement criteria

## UK Start-up Visa

The UK Start-up Visa replaced the Tier 1 Graduate Entrepreneur Visa and was used by high-potential businesspeople who wanted to start a new business in the UK. It closed to new applicants on 13 April 2023, when the Innovator Founder Visa replaced it.

- No initial investment was required (unlike the old Innovator route)
- Applicants had to be 18+, with a business plan/idea endorsed by an approved body
- Applicants had to prove English language competence and financial ability to support themselves and dependents
- Validity: 2 years (not renewable)

## UK Sole Representative of an Overseas Business Visa

The UK Sole Representative of an Overseas Business Visa helped senior managers or management representatives of an already running overseas business to open a branch in the UK. It closed to new applicants in April 2022 and was replaced by the **UK Expansion Worker Visa**, part of the Global Business Mobility routes.

- The Expansion Worker route is for a senior manager or specialist sent to set up a UK branch of an overseas business that is not yet trading in the UK
- Stay: a maximum of 2 years in total
- It does not lead to settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain)
- Family (immediate) can accompany

## FAQs

**Q1. What is the difference between the UK Innovator Visa and the UK Start-up Visa?**
A. The Innovator Visa required £50,000 of investment and an endorsed, scalable business, with a route to settlement; the Start-up Visa required no initial investment and was valid for 2 years. Both closed on 13 April 2023 and were replaced by the Innovator Founder Visa, which needs an endorsement but no minimum investment.

**Q2. Do I need an endorsement for these UK business routes?**
A. Yes. The Innovator Founder route requires endorsement by an approved body based on innovation, viability, and scalability. The Expansion Worker route, which replaced the Sole Representative visa, instead requires the overseas business to hold a UK sponsor licence.

**Q3. Can my family accompany me to the UK on these visas?**
A. Yes. Dependents (spouse/partner and eligible children) can usually accompany under the Innovator Founder and Expansion Worker routes, subject to meeting maintenance and documentation requirements.

**Q4. When can I apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)?**
A. Under the Innovator Founder route, you may be eligible for ILR after 3 years if the business meets the endorsement criteria. The Expansion Worker route does not lead to ILR.
