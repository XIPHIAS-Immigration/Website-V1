## Portugal – Golden Visa Program

This visa program is also known as the **Portugal Golden Visa**. Since October 2023, **real estate** no longer qualifies. The main routes today are **€500,000** in qualifying **investment funds**, **€500,000** in **research activities** (**€400,000** in low-density areas), **€250,000** in **arts or cultural heritage** (**€200,000** in low-density areas), and business investment that creates jobs.

To maintain the Golden Visa, the investor must spend a minimum of **7 days in the first year** of investment followed by **14 days every 2 years**. **Portugal** grants **permanent residence after 5 years**; **citizenship** now requires **10 years** of legal residence for Indian nationals (Lei Orgânica 1/2026, in force since 19 May 2026), together with language proficiency. India does not allow dual citizenship, so acquiring Portuguese citizenship would end Indian citizenship.

## Cyprus – Residency by Investment

To obtain **Cyprus residency**, a high-net-worth individual must spend a minimum of **€300,000** in **Cyprus real estate**. Such property should be new and approved by the Government. Unlike many programs, **Cyprus** allows the property to be owned by the applicant, their spouse, or an entity belonging to them. Residency holders are required to visit **Cyprus** at least once every two years.

Investors become eligible to apply for **Cyprus citizenship** after **8 years of legal residence** within the previous 11, including **12 months of continuous stay** immediately before applying. Cyprus ended its citizenship by investment programme on **1 November 2020**, so there is no longer a direct citizenship route. Note also that **Cyprus is not part of the Schengen Area**.

## Greece – Golden Visa Program

**Greece** requires investors to invest **€800,000** in **real estate** in Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Thira (Santorini) and islands with more than 3,100 residents, or **€400,000** elsewhere; **€250,000** applies only to converting commercial buildings to residential use or restoring listed buildings. The threshold must be met with a **single property** of at least 120 m², so several smaller properties cannot be combined. The **Greece Residency Program** (Golden Visa) does not require investors to live in Greece. However, to obtain **Greece citizenship**, the investor must live in Greece for seven continuous years and learn the **Greek language**.

## Latvia – European Union Residency Program

**Latvia** abolished its **real estate** residency route on **15 September 2026**, when its new Immigration Law took effect. Investors can now qualify through **business investment**: share capital of **€50,000** in a small Latvian company (**€100,000** for a larger company or group), plus a **€10,000** state fee, for a **2-year** residence permit.

After **5 years of residence**, investors can apply for **Latvian citizenship**, provided they meet residence requirements and pass a **Latvian language test**. Latvia does not allow dual citizenship with India, so Indian applicants would have to give up Indian citizenship.

## Malta – Permanent Residence Programme

**Malta** is a beautiful island nation that welcomes **foreign direct investors**. Through the **Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)**, investors buy a property of at least **€375,000** or lease one for at least **€14,000 a year**, pay a **€60,000** administrative fee and a **€37,000** government contribution, and make a **€2,000** donation to a registered NGO. Malta's citizenship by investment programme was repealed in 2025 after a ruling by the Court of Justice of the EU.

## France – Talent Passport Visa

**France** is one of the most advanced economies in Europe and offers a **multi-year residency permit** under the **Talent Passport visa program**. This visa is renewable every **four years** and is ideal for **highly skilled professionals** or **high-net-worth individuals** who wish to **start or manage a business in France**.
