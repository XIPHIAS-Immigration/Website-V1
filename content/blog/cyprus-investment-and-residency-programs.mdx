## Overview

European countries are known for their beauty and lively living. They offer many beautiful beaches and rich histories. To increase foreign investment, several European countries, including **Cyprus**, have introduced **investment immigration programs**. Cyprus now offers **residency**; its citizenship-by-investment program ended on **1 November 2020**.

## Why Cyprus?

- Part of the **European Union** since 2004 and the **Euro Zone** since 2008.
- Stunning **Mediterranean beaches** and crystal-clear waters.
- Strong **tourism, agriculture, and modern industries**.
- Low pollution and low **crime rate** with world-class amenities.
- Cost-free education and globally recognized universities.
- Favorable **tax regime** with no inheritance tax and a competitive corporate tax rate.
- Cyprus is in the **EU** but not in the **Schengen Area**, so a Cyprus residence permit does not give Schengen travel rights.

## Cyprus Investment Pathways

### 1. Cyprus Permanent Residency Program

**Purpose:** Boost foreign direct investment.

**Eligibility & Requirements:**
- Applicants must be **non-Europeans**.
- **Investment in real estate:** a minimum of **€300,000** in new property is the commonly applied threshold.
- Proof of a **secure annual income** from abroad, rising with family size.

**Process:**
- Processed by the **Civil Registry and Migration Department**.
- The spouse and dependent children can be included.
- The Migration Department updates its criteria from time to time, so we confirm the current requirements in writing for each application.

### 2. Cyprus Citizenship by Investment Program (Closed)

The Cyprus Investment Program, which granted citizenship for an investment of about **€2 million**, was terminated on **1 November 2020** by a decision of the Council of Ministers. No new applications are accepted. Naturalisation now requires years of legal residence: for most non-EU nationals, **8 years** within the previous 11, including the 12 months immediately before applying.

## FAQs

**1) What is the Cyprus Investment Program?**
Cyprus offers **permanent residency** to investors who make qualifying investments, mainly in real estate. Its citizenship-by-investment program ended on **1 November 2020**.

**2) How much investment is required for Cyprus Permanent Residency?**
A minimum of **€300,000** in approved real estate is the commonly applied threshold, alongside proof of a secure annual income from abroad. We confirm the current criteria in writing for each application.

**3) Can I still get Cyprus Citizenship by Investment?**
No. The program ended on **1 November 2020**. Citizenship now comes only through naturalisation after years of legal residence.

**4) Can my family be included in the Cyprus investment application?**
Yes, the **spouse and dependent children** can be included; age limits apply to children.
