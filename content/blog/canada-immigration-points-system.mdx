## Canada Immigration Points System (CRS) Overview

**Canada Immigration** is primarily a **point-based** process, and the **Express Entry Program** is the preferred route for many applicants. The **Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)** assigns points based on the information provided in your application.

## What is Canada’s Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)?

Candidates are ranked for **immigration to Canada** using the **CRS**. Points are assigned for:

- **Age**  
- **Education**  
- **Language fluency**  
- **Canadian work experience**


## CRS Factors — Without Spouse/Partner

- **Age** — *Max 110 points.* Highest points between **20–29**; 0 points at 17 or under, or at 45 and over.  
- **Education** — *Max 150 points.* Highest for **doctoral-level degrees**.  
- **Language Fluency**  
  - Official language: **reading, writing, speaking, listening** (*max 34 points per ability*; CLB 10+ highest).  
  - Second official language: same four abilities (*max 6 points per ability, 24 in total*).  
- **Canadian Work Experience** — *Max 80 points* (maximum for 5+ years).  


## CRS Factors — With Spouse/Partner

- **Your own factors** are capped slightly lower: age *max 100*, education *max 140*, first official language *max 128*, Canadian work experience *max 70*.  
- **Education (Partner)** — Points range 0–10; **Master’s/Doctorate** receive 10.  
- **Language Fluency (Partner)** — Each first official language ability can give up to 5 points (*total 20*).  
- **Canadian Work Experience (Partner)** — *Max 10 points* for 5+ years.  


## Skill Transferability Factors (Max 100)

Points awarded for combinations of **education**, **foreign work experience**, **language ability** and **Canadian work experience**, plus a **certificate of qualification** in a trade:

- **Education** combined with strong language ability or Canadian work experience — up to **50** points.  
- **Foreign work experience** combined with strong language ability or Canadian work experience — up to **50** points.  
- **Trade certificate of qualification** combined with language ability — up to **50** points.  

The total for skill transferability is capped at **100** points.


## Additional Points (Max 600)

- **Provincial nomination** — **600** points.  
- **French-language ability** — up to **50** points.  
- **Post-secondary education in Canada** — **15** or **30** points, depending on the length of the program.  
- **Brother or sister in Canada** who is a citizen or permanent resident — **15** points.  

Since March 2025, a job offer no longer adds CRS points. The maximum possible score is **1,200**.


## Improving Your CRS Score

Higher language test results, an Educational Credential Assessment for every qualification, additional work experience and a provincial nomination are the most common ways to raise your score. Invitations depend on each draw’s cut-off, and many recent rounds have been category-based.
