## Why Canada for Investment Immigration

Canada offers political stability, strong GDP growth, and multiple **provincial pathways** for entrepreneurs who can **create jobs**, invest in **businesses/farms**, and meet compliance requirements.

Canada currently has no open federal investor or entrepreneur programme: the Start-Up Visa stopped accepting new applications on 31 December 2025 and was paused on 30 June 2026, and the Self-Employed Persons Program has been paused since 30 April 2024. Business immigration now runs through the provincial and territorial nominee streams.

## Canada Start-Up Visa (SUV)

**Closed to new applicants.** New applications stopped on **31 December 2025**, holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate had until **30 June 2026** to file, and IRCC paused the programme on that date. IRCC continues to process applications accepted before the cut-off.


## Manitoba — Entrepreneur / Farm Investment

**Highlights:**
- **Pathways:** Entrepreneur or Farm Investment.
- **Personal net worth:** **CAD $500,000**.
- **Initial investment:** set individually in each applicant's Business Performance Agreement; CAD $250,000 in the Capital Region and CAD $150,000 elsewhere are the usual reference points.
- **No deposit** is required for the Entrepreneur Pathway.
- Conduct thorough **business research**.


## Prince Edward Island (PEI) — Work Permit Route

**Highlights:**
1. Own at least **33.33%** of a PEI business.
2. Obtain a **temporary work permit**; may need **exploratory visit**.
3. Prove **net worth:** CAD $600,000.
4. **Minimum business investment:** CAD $150,000.
5. Submit **EOI** via official portal.

## Saskatchewan — SINP Entrepreneur & Farm (Closed)

Saskatchewan **permanently closed** its SINP Entrepreneur category and Farm Owner/Operator pathway on **27 March 2025**. No new applications, expressions of interest or invitations are being issued.

---

## British Columbia (BC) — Entrepreneur Immigration

**Highlights:**
1. **Net worth:** CAD $600,000 (Base stream) or CAD $300,000 (Regional stream).
2. **Minimum investment:** CAD $200,000 (Base stream) or CAD $100,000 (Regional stream).
3. Own at least **33.3%** of the business.
4. Create **at least one job** for a Canadian/PR.
5. **XIPHIAS** assists with documentation and compliance.

---

## New Brunswick (NB) — Business Immigration Stream

**Highlights:**
1. **Net worth:** CAD $500,000 (CAD $300,000 for a farming business).
2. **Investment:** CAD $150,000.
3. An **exploratory visit** and an **approved business plan** are required before you apply.
4. This stream replaced New Brunswick's closed Entrepreneurial Stream.

---

## Ontario (OINP) — Entrepreneur Stream (Closed)

Ontario has closed the **OINP Entrepreneur Stream**.

## General Eligibility

- **Experience:** 3+ years in last 5 years (management/ownership).
- **Language:** Meet **CLB** in English/French.
- **Background:** Clean criminal record; medical clearance.
- **Adaptability:** Ability to **settle in Canada**.
- Exact criteria differ by province; check the specific stream before applying.

---

## Benefits

- Obtain **Canadian PR** and eventual **citizenship** (after 3 years of physical presence within 5 years).
- Family inclusion; children get **public education**.
- Access **Medicare** and high living standards.
- Proximity to **USA** for expanded business opportunities.
- Eligibility for **Canadian universities** on PR.

## FAQs

**1) Is the Start-Up Visa still an option?**  
- No. It closed to new applicants on 31 December 2025 and was paused on 30 June 2026.  
- **Entrepreneur PNP:** Buy/start a local business meeting **net worth**, **investment**, and **job creation** targets. This is now the main route.

**2) Can my family get PR with me?**  
Yes. Eligible **spouses/partners** and **dependent children** are usually included.

**3) Do I always need an LMIA?**  
Not always. Some **owner-operator/entrepreneur** scenarios have exemptions if you **actively manage** the business.

**4) What documents matter most?**  
Proof of **net worth/source of funds**, **business ownership/management**, **business plan**, **investment**, **police and medical clearance**, plus **language** and **education** verification.
