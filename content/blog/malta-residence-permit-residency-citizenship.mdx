## Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP)

Through this program, **non-European investors** can obtain **permanent residence** in Malta, an EU and Schengen country. To be eligible, the investor has to **buy a qualifying property** worth at least **Euro 375,000** or **lease** one for at least **Euro 14,000 per annum**, anywhere in **Malta** or **Gozo**, and keep it for **5 years**. Along with this, the investor pays a **non-refundable administrative fee of Euro 60,000** (plus **Euro 7,500** for each dependant), a **government contribution of Euro 37,000** and a **Euro 2,000 donation** to a registered NGO, and must hold capital of **Euro 500,000** (of which Euro 150,000 in financial assets) or **Euro 650,000** (of which Euro 75,000 in financial assets).

With the [**MPRP**](/residency/malta), investors can **live indefinitely in Malta**. The **residence card** is valid for **5 years** and is **renewable**, while the qualifying property must be kept for the first **5 years**. Also, this program **doesn’t need** the investor to **live in Malta** at any given point in time. This **VISA** is **extendable to family members** as well.

## Malta Citizenship by Investment

**Malta’s Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** route, which began as the **Malta Individual Investor Program**, has been **repealed**. On 29 April 2025 the Court of Justice of the European Union ruled that the scheme breached EU law, and Malta discontinued it by Act XXI of 2025. **Malta has no citizenship-by-investment program today.**

## FAQs

**Is the Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) truly passive—do I need to live in Malta?**
The **residency** is valid even without continuous residence; investors **do not need to live in Malta** to maintain status.

**What are the key financial components of the MPRP?**
A **property purchase (Euro 375,000)** or **lease (Euro 14,000 per year)**, a **Euro 60,000 administrative fee**, a **Euro 37,000 government contribution** and a **Euro 2,000 NGO donation**. Malta's citizenship-by-investment route has been repealed.

**Can my family be included under these Malta programs?**
Yes, the **MPRP** allows **family inclusion**, with a **Euro 7,500** administrative fee for each dependant.
