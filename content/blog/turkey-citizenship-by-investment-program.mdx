## Program Overview: Turkey Citizenship by Investment (CBI)

The transcontinental country between Europe and Asia, Turkey, is offering its Citizenship by Investment program for interested investors who want to explore investment options in Turkey. This program was introduced in 2016. Qualifying investments include real estate, bank deposits, government debt instruments, investment fund shares, a private pension contribution, fixed capital investment and job creation. For Indian nationals, acquiring Turkish citizenship ends Indian citizenship automatically under Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955; OCI is available afterwards but is not dual citizenship.

## About Turkey: Strategic Location & Economic Profile

Turkey is a transcontinental hub bridging Europe and Asia, and a key financial center. The country’s culture reflects influences of Greece, Persia, Rome, Byzantine, and Ottoman empires. Ankara is the capital and Istanbul is renowned for its architecture. Considered one of the world’s large economies, Turkey is driven by shipping and automobile industries. To encourage foreign direct investment (FDI), Turkey launched the Turkey Citizenship by Investment program.

The main applicant's spouse and children under 18, or dependent children, can obtain Turkish citizenship in the same application; parents cannot be included. Turkey is a member of the United Nations, NATO, IMF, World Bank, and G-20.

## Investment Routes & Requirements

### Real Estate Investment Route (Minimum USD $400,000; Hold 3 Years)

An investor purchasing authorised real estate in Türkiye must invest a minimum of **USD $400,000** and hold the property for at least 3 years. The threshold rose from USD $250,000 to USD $400,000 on 13 June 2022 under Presidential Decision 5554 (Official Gazette No. 31834). Since 12 December 2023 the property must also have condominium ownership or construction servitude established, or be arsa-classified land with a building on it — bare and agricultural land no longer qualify. After the three years the property can be sold or retained.

### Bank Deposit Route (USD $500,000; Hold 3 Years)

Alternatively, an investor can place a bank deposit of USD $500,000 in a Turkish bank for a minimum of 3 years. The investor may withdraw or use the amount in Turkey after this period. Annual interest is typically added by the bank.

### Fixed Capital Investment (USD $500,000) and Job Creation (50 Employees)

Investors may make a fixed capital investment of at least USD $500,000 in Turkey, certified by the Ministry of Industry and Technology; the regulation states no holding period for this route. Job creation is a separate route: employing at least 50 people qualifies, with no capital minimum.

### Government Debt, Investment Funds and Private Pension (USD $500,000; Hold 3 Years)

Investors may also place at least USD $500,000 in government debt instruments, investment fund shares or a Turkish private pension, each held for 3 years.

## Processing Time & Presence Requirements

Türkiye publishes no official processing time; in XIPHIAS's case files, investors and their families have typically received passports in around 6 months. The program does not require the investor to be physically present in Turkey at any time.

## US E-2 Visa Advantage via Turkey Citizenship

A significant advantage of holding Turkish citizenship is potential eligibility for the US E-2 Visa (a treaty investor visa). Türkiye has been a US E-2 treaty country since 18 May 1990, so a Turkish citizen may apply for an E-2 visa to invest in and run a business in the USA, renewable as long as the business operates; eligibility is decided by US consular officers. Grenada is the other citizenship-by-investment country commonly used for this purpose. India is not an E-2 treaty country, so Indian passport holders cannot obtain an E-2 visa on Indian nationality.

**Q4. Can Turkey citizenship help with access to the US market?**
