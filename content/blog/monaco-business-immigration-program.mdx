## Monaco Permanent Residency Visa: Eligibility & Investment

**Monaco** is not a member of the European Union, so a **Monaco residence permit** does not give **EU permanent residence**. The process to obtain a **Monaco residence permit** typically takes around **6 months**. The **high net-worth individual** who intends to **invest in Monaco** will be **16 years and above** and needs **accommodation in Monaco** large enough for the household. The investor will need a **bank attestation** from a **Monaco bank** confirming sufficient means to reside in Monaco. Monaco sets no legal minimum: the sum a bank regards as sufficient is set by that bank, and figures around **Euro 500,000** reflect typical private-banking practice. **Alternatively**, the investor can choose to **invest in a business in Monaco**, either **start-up** or a **running business**.

## Residency Stages & Timeline

There will be **3 stages** in obtaining the **residency permit**. In the **first stage**, the investor gets a **temporary residency permit** for **12 months** time period. It would be necessary for the investor to **live in Monaco** for a period of at least **3 months** in the **12 months’** time. The investor will be **renewing** the **temporary residency permit** for **3 years** in total. Only after which, the investor will be granted a proper **ordinary residency card**. This is the **second stage** of the visa process. This **residency card** would be valid for **3 years**, post which the card should be **renewed**. This card can be renewed as many times as possible.

## Privileged Residency (10-Year Card)

Once the investor has lived in **Monaco** for **10 years**, holding **temporary** and then **ordinary residency cards**, he/she becomes eligible to apply for **privileged residency permit** which allows the investor **residency for 10 years** in **Monaco**. However, to **maintain** this **privileged residency permit**, the investor will have to **reside in Monaco** for a period **no less than 183 days every year**.

## Monaco Citizenship

**Monaco** has no **Citizenship by Investment program**. Naturalisation requires at least **10 years** of ordinary residence in Monaco after the age of 18, is entirely at the discretion of the **Sovereign Prince**, and requires applicants to give up their previous nationality.

## FAQs

**How long does it take to obtain Monaco PR?**
The **Monaco residence permit** process typically takes around **6 months**, subject to meeting **eligibility** and **documentation** requirements.

**What is the minimum investment or deposit required?**
Monaco sets **no legal minimum**. You need a **bank attestation** from a **Monaco bank** confirming sufficient means; banks commonly look for around **Euro 500,000**, but each bank sets its own figure. Alternatively, you can **invest in a business in Monaco** (start-up or running business).

**What are the stages of Monaco residency?**
**Temporary residency (12 months, renewable up to 3 years)** → **Ordinary residency card (3 years, renewable)** → **Privileged residency (10-year card)**, available after 10 years in Monaco, with **183 days/year** residence to maintain.

**Is there a Citizenship by Investment route in Monaco?**
No. **Monaco** has no **Citizenship by Investment** route; naturalisation requires at least **10 years** of residence and is at the discretion of the **Sovereign Prince**.
