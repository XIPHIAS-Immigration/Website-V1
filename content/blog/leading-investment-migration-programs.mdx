## Popular Investment Migration Programs

Migration for investment has shaped societies and economies globally. Investors contribute to the prosperity of their adopted countries while benefiting from **residency and citizenship advantages**. Studies show migrants form 3% of the global population but contribute **9% to global GDP**, making investment migration a win-win proposition.


### Investment Options

- **Investment in New Business** – Investors create employment and contribute to local economies. Popular destinations: UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand.
- **Investment in Startups** – Entrepreneurial investors can launch innovative businesses. Example: the UK Innovator Founder visa (Canada's Start-Up Visa stopped accepting new applications on 31 December 2025).
- **Investment in Real Estate** – Investors acquire properties in countries like Turkey, Greece, Malta, or Caribbean nations (Dominica, St. Lucia, St. Kitts and Nevis, Grenada, Antigua & Barbuda). Portugal and Spain no longer offer residence through property purchase.

## Leading Investment Migration Programs by Country

### Canada
- **Provincial entrepreneur streams** – Work permit first, then permanent residency once the business meets the conditions of a performance agreement. Canada's federal Immigrant Investor Program closed in 2014.
- **Canada Start-Up Visa** – Stopped accepting new applications on 31 December 2025 and was paused on 30 June 2026.
- **Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program** – Saskatchewan permanently closed its Entrepreneur and Farm Owner/Operator streams on 27 March 2025.
- **Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program** – Ontario has closed its Entrepreneur Stream.

### USA
- **EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program** – Invest in a business to create jobs and obtain a **Green Card**.
- Investment minimum: USD 1,050,000 (Non-Targeted Areas) or USD 800,000 (Targeted Areas).
- EB-5 Integrity Fund fee: USD 1,000.

### Australia
- **Subclass 188 Business Innovation & Investment Visa** – Closed to new applications on 31 July 2024. Australia no longer runs a business or investor migration program; the **National Innovation visa (subclass 858)** is the main route for people with exceptional talent.

### Germany
- **Residence permit for self-employment** – Set up or invest in a business that serves an economic interest in Germany; permit for up to 3 years.
- Settlement permit (permanent residency) possible after 3 years if the business succeeds. Citizenship eligibility after 5 years of residence (since June 2024).
- Benefits include **high living standards, free education, healthcare, and excellent infrastructure**.

## FAQs

**1. What are the main investment migration programs worldwide?**
- EB-5 USA, Canadian provincial entrepreneur streams, Germany's self-employment residence permit, and residence-by-investment programmes such as Greece's Golden Visa. Canada's Start-Up Visa and Australia's Subclass 188 are closed.

**2. Who is eligible for investment migration programs?**
- High-net-worth individuals willing to invest in business, startups, or real estate, and meet the country-specific criteria.

**3. How long does it take to get permanent residency?**
- Varies by program: Germany's self-employment permit can lead to permanent residency after 3 years, Canadian provincial entrepreneur streams lead to permanent residency once the business meets its performance agreement, and EB-5 USA leads to a conditional Green Card once the investor petition is approved.

**4. Can family members benefit from investment migration?**
- Yes, most programs extend residency and future citizenship benefits to spouses and dependent children.
