## Overview

Canada welcomes **foreign direct investment** through multiple **investment immigration programs**. Eligible investors and their families can gain **Permanent Residency (PR)** while contributing economically through **start-ups, existing businesses, or agricultural ventures**.

Canada currently has no open federal investor or entrepreneur programme: the Start-Up Visa stopped accepting new applications on 31 December 2025 and was paused on 30 June 2026, and the Self-Employed Persons Program has been paused since 30 April 2024. Business immigration now runs through the provincial and territorial nominee streams.

**Typical investor profile:**
- High-net-worth individual with **3+ years** business or senior management experience.
- Eligible investments include **Canadian businesses** and **start-ups**; buying real estate does not qualify for any Canadian immigration program.
- Provincial programs provide diverse **entrepreneur/PNP** routes tailored to local economic needs.

### Benefits of Canada Investment Immigration
- **PR** for the principal applicant and family.
- Access to **public schooling** for children.
- **Government-sponsored health insurance**.
- Stable business environment and world-class infrastructure.


## Canada Start-Up Visa (SUV) Program — Closed

The **Start-Up Visa** supported entrepreneurs with **innovative business ideas** backed by a **designated organization** (angel investor group, venture capital fund or business incubator). New applications stopped on **31 December 2025**, holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate had until **30 June 2026** to file, and IRCC paused the programme on that date. IRCC continues to process applications accepted before the cut-off and has said a targeted pilot for immigrant entrepreneurs will follow; as at 29 September 2026, none had launched.


## Owner-Operator Investor Program (Work Permit → PR)

A **two-stage route**:

1. **Stage 1 — Work Authorization:** Acquire or establish a **Canadian business** and seek a **work permit** (LMIA may apply; exemptions exist for qualifying owner-operators who **control** and **actively manage** the business).  
2. **Stage 2 — PR Pathway:** Transition to **PR** via **Express Entry** or suitable **PNP** once operational and meeting criteria.

> LMIA traditionally requires advertising a position, but **owner-operator** variations may differ if the applicant manages the business directly.


## Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) — Entrepreneur Streams

In several provinces and territories, investors can **purchase, start, or expand businesses** and receive **provincial nomination** toward **PR**.

### Manitoba — Business Investor Stream (BIS)
- **Entrepreneur Pathway:** Invest in or start a Manitoba business; criteria include **net worth** and **minimum investment**.  
- **Farm Investor Pathway:** For applicants with **farming/agri-business** experience.

### Prince Edward Island (PEI) — Work Permit Stream
Invest at least **CAD 150,000** for at least **33.33%** ownership of a **PEI business** and manage it on a work permit, with a verified net worth of **CAD 600,000**.

### Saskatchewan — SINP Entrepreneur & Farm (Closed)
Saskatchewan **permanently closed** its Entrepreneur category and Farm Owner/Operator pathway on **27 March 2025**.

### Ontario — OINP Entrepreneur (Closed)
Ontario has closed its **OINP Entrepreneur Stream**.

### British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration
Operate a business (often on a **work permit**), meet performance conditions, then apply for **nomination** and **PR**.

### New Brunswick — Business Immigration Stream
Establish, acquire or operate a business in **NB** with a minimum investment of **CAD 150,000** and a net worth of **CAD 500,000** (**CAD 300,000** for a farming business), leading to **provincial nomination** and a PR application. This stream replaced New Brunswick's closed Entrepreneurial Stream.

### Other Open Streams
Nova Scotia, the Northwest Territories, Newfoundland & Labrador, Alberta and Yukon also run business streams that lead to provincial nomination.

## FAQs

**1) Is the Start-Up Visa still an option?**  
No. New applications stopped on 31 December 2025 and the programme was paused on 30 June 2026. **PNP entrepreneur** streams, which focus on **owning/operating** local businesses with **investment**, **job creation**, and **performance conditions**, are the main open route.

**2) Can my family get PR with me?**  
Yes. Eligible **spouses/partners** and **dependent children** are typically included.

**3) Do I need an LMIA for owner-operator?**  
LMIA rules apply in many cases, but **exemptions/variations** exist where the applicant **controls and manages** the business.

**4) How long until I receive PR?**  
- **PNP Entrepreneur:** Allow time for business setup, the performance period and nomination before the PR application itself.
