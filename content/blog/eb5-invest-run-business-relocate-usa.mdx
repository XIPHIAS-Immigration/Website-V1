## What is the EB-5 Program?

The **EB-5 program**, started in 1990, allows foreign investors to **invest in a U.S. business** and obtain **permanent residency** for themselves and their families. The investment must create a **minimum of 10 full-time jobs** for U.S. workers.

### Eligibility Requirements:

- Minimum investment of **USD $800,000** in a **Targeted Employment Area (TEA)** or infrastructure project, or **USD $1,050,000** elsewhere.
- Proof of a **good personal and financial background**, including the lawful source of the invested funds.

### Where Can You Invest?

- **Real estate development projects** in the USA, usually through regional centers
- **Running or Starting a Business** in the USA

## Direct Investment Opportunities

Many U.S. businesses and projects seek **EB-5 capital**. Every project needs careful due diligence on its job-creation plan and on the risk to your capital, so it is highly recommended to work with a **trusted immigration and investment expert**.

### Benefits of Investing in EB-5 Projects:

1. **U.S. Green Card** for you and your family
2. Contribute to **job creation**
3. **Relocate to the USA** with family
4. Pathway to **U.S. Citizenship**
5. Children born in the U.S. **automatically receive citizenship**
6. No requirement to prove **English proficiency**

In the October 2026 Visa Bulletin, the reserved EB-5 categories (rural, high-unemployment and infrastructure) are current for India, while the unreserved category has a final action date of 1 December 2023. Once the **I-526 petition** is approved and a visa is available, investors can proceed with **Green Card applications**.

**Q1: What is the minimum investment required for EB-5?**
A: $800,000 in a TEA or infrastructure project, or $1,050,000 elsewhere.

**Q2: Can my family relocate with me under EB-5?**
A: Yes, spouses and children under 21 are included.

**Q3: How long does it take to get a Green Card through EB-5?**
