## Before you read any further: two things this passport does not do

**It does not reach the Schengen area.** São Tomé and Príncipe is listed in **Annex I** of Regulation
(EU) 2018/1806 — the list of third countries whose nationals must hold a visa. **It does not reach the
United Kingdom** either: São Tomé and Príncipe is item 85 on the UK's visa national list.

So where you read "60+ countries" below, read it as **58 to 61 destinations** (Henley Passport Index
2026: 58, ranking the passport around 74th; Arton's Passport Index: 61 plus one visa-on-arrival). What
it genuinely reaches includes Singapore, Hong Kong, South Africa, Türkiye, the UAE, Qatar and most
Portuguese-speaking countries. Rankings are commercial indices, not government data.

**And if you hold an Indian passport, this replaces it.** Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955
ends Indian citizenship automatically on the voluntary acquisition of another nationality. There is no
appeal and no way to defer it. You would surrender your Indian passport, obtain a Surrender Certificate,
and apply for Overseas Citizen of India status — which the Ministry of External Affairs states expressly
is *not* dual citizenship and carries no political rights, no vote, and no right to buy agricultural
land in India.

## What the programme actually is, in law

The statutory chain is **Lei n.º 07/2022, Article 10(2)** (nationality for investment or donation),
implemented by **Decreto-Lei n.º 07/2025**, published in the *Diário da República*, I Série, n.º 33, of
1 August 2025. Applications opened 1 September 2025. It is administered by the Citizenship Investment
Unit under a ten-year exclusive concession.

## How long it really takes

The Citizenship Investment Unit's own first-year report, published 1 September 2026, records a fastest
approval of **26 days**, an **average approval of about two months**, and an **average of about four
months to passport in hand** — with **29 passports issued against 548 applications** in the first year.
Anyone quoting you six weeks to a passport is quoting a marketing target.

## Two limits that catch people

**Article 11(1) of Lei 07/2022 bars naturalisation to anyone already holding more than two foreign
nationalities**, and a Citizenship Investment Unit memorandum of 10 April 2026 put applications from
holders of three or more on hold pending an amendment that has not been enacted.

**Adult dependent children.** Programme materials describe children under 30, but Article 10(4) of the
nationality law contemplates only minor children, and the same April 2026 memorandum placed **passport
issuance for adult dependent children aged 18 and over on hold**. We will tell you the position on the
day you file rather than sell you something that cannot currently be delivered.

There is also a **USD 5,000 non-refundable submission fee** on top of the contribution, and the
contribution is USD 95,000 for a family of two to four, plus USD 5,000 per additional dependant.

## Sao Tome CBI Requirements

To apply for **citizenship** in **Sao Tome and Principe** through **investment**, you need to meet a few key requirements:

- **Minimum investment:** **US$90,000** for a single applicant (US$95,000 for a family of two to four, plus US$5,000 per additional dependant), as a non-refundable contribution to the fund named in the decree — plus a **US$5,000 non-refundable submission fee**. The programme's own channel styles the payment a *National Development Contribution*.
- **Family inclusion:** **Spouse** (including a recognised de facto partner), **dependent children**, and **dependent parents over 55**. Programme materials describe children up to 30, but passport issuance for adult dependent children aged 18 and over is currently **on hold** pending a revised dependency law. Grandparents and siblings are **not** included.
- **No residency requirement:** The whole process can be done **remotely** without visiting the country.
- **Clean record:** All applicants must provide **verified documents** and pass **background checks**.

Getting a **Sao Tome passport** is simple and clear. The steps are:

1. **Submit your application:** Apply through the **Citizenship Investment Unit (CIU)** and pay the required fee.

3. **Make your investment:** Complete the chosen **donation** or **approved investment** option.
4. **Receive your documents:** Get your **passport**, **national ID**, and **Certificate of Registration**.

## Investment Options in Sao Tome for Citizenship

Applicants can choose from two main **investment routes**:

- **Donation:** A non-refundable contribution to the **National Transformation Fund**, which supports national development projects.
- **Sectors the fund supports:** energy, port and airport infrastructure, urban development, housing, education, health, industry and tourism. ## FAQs

**1) What is the minimum investment for Sao Tome & Principe Citizenship by Investment?**
**Answer:** **US$90,000** for a single applicant, **US$95,000** for a family of two to four, plus **US$5,000** for each additional dependant — and a separate **US$5,000 non-refundable submission fee**. We have not been able to verify a distinct "approved public projects" investment route with its own published threshold, so we do not quote one.

**2) Do I need to visit Sao Tome and Principe during the CBI process?**
**Answer:** **No.** Article 10(3) of Lei 07/2022 allows the residence requirement to be waived, there is no minimum stay, and the process is handled through the Citizenship Investment Unit without a required visit.
