Australia offers multiple pathways for skilled migration through the **Australia Immigration programs**.

### Australia PR Pathways

**Sub-Class 189 (Skilled Independent Visa):**
Applicants without a job offer in Australia can apply for **permanent residency** under the Sub-Class 189 visa. This visa allows living, working, and studying anywhere in Australia.

**Sub-Class 190 (Skilled Nominated Visa):**
Skilled professionals nominated by an Australian state or territory government can apply under the Sub-Class 190 visa, a **permanent residency** visa. Nominees are expected to live and work in the nominating state or territory.

**Sub-Class 491 (Skilled Regional Provisional Visa):**
This pathway targets professionals wishing to live and work in regional Australia, nominated by a state or territory government or sponsored by an eligible relative. After three years of living and working in a designated regional area and meeting the income requirement, holders can apply for **permanent residency** through the Subclass 191 visa.

### SkillSelect Portal and Eligibility

Applicants create a profile on the **SkillSelect portal**, valid for up to 2 years, and can update it multiple times. Eligibility is assessed based on:

- **Age**
- **Education**
- **Work experience**
- **Language proficiency**
- **Adaptability**

The points-test pass mark is 65 for **Sub-Class 189**, **Sub-Class 190** and **Sub-Class 491**, but invitations go to higher scores: in the June 2026 round, professional occupations needed 80 to 100 points.

## FAQs

1. **What is the minimum score required for Australia PR?**
A. The pass mark is 65 points for **Sub-Class 189**, **190** and **491**, though invitations usually need higher scores.

2. **Do I need a job offer to apply for Australia PR?**
A. Not for **Sub-Class 189**. **Sub-Class 190** requires nomination by a state or territory government, and **491** requires state or territory nomination or sponsorship by an eligible relative in a designated regional area.

3. **What documents are required to support my application?**
A. Essential documents include **education certificates, work experience letters, language test results (IELTS/PTE), and identity documents**.

4. **What is SkillSelect?**
A. **SkillSelect** is the Australian immigration portal where applicants submit their profiles, update information, and express interest in visas.

5. **Why work with MARA-registered consultants?**
