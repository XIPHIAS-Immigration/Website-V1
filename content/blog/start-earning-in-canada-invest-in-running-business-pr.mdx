## FAQs

**1) What’s the minimum investment to buy a running business in Canada for PR?**
**Answer:** Provincial entrepreneur streams start from **CAD 100,000** of investment outside major centres (for example British Columbia's Regional stream and Nova Scotia outside Halifax), with higher amounts in larger cities. We provide **pre-screened businesses** and **full immigration support** to align with **PR** pathways.

**2) Do I need prior business experience or a specific age to qualify?**
**Answer:** Each provincial stream sets its own requirements, including **business ownership or senior management experience**, **net worth** and **language**. Each case is assessed against them.

**3) How soon can I start earning after investing?**
**Answer:** An **established business** already has revenue, so it can produce income from the start, but profits depend on the business and are not guaranteed. Review its financials as part of **due diligence**.
