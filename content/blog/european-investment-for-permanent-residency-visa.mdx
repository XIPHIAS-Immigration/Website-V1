## Introduction

**The European Investment for Permanent Residency VISA** Programs cover every variety of investment plan for cross-border investors, meeting the requirements of **High Net Worth Individuals**. **Investment VISA** in Europe provides numerous benefits beyond the return on the investment itself.

Europe is one of the most **united continents**, with countries cooperating through initiatives like the European Union. To encourage **foreign direct investment**, European countries have created programs offering **Permanent Residency** to investors in real estate, business, or government-approved projects.

Investors gain **visa-free travel** across the **European Union and Schengen Area**, along with the ability to **work, study, and expand business opportunities**. Immediate family members can also obtain residency with similar benefits. Children below age limits defined by each country can join as **Permanent Residents**, enjoying similar rights as citizens.

Most European countries are developed, offering **high living standards, industrialized and tech-driven economies, and investor-friendly tax regimes**. Residency programs are often renewable and, in some cases, lead to **citizenship**.

## Popular European Investment Residency Programs

### Portugal Investment Programs
Portugal's main investment route is the **Portugal Golden VISA**:

- **Investment funds or research** – Residency through a qualifying investment fund or research activities, each from **€500,000**.
- **Culture and business** – Support for artistic production or cultural heritage from **€250,000**, or a company investment from **€500,000**.

Real estate purchases have not qualified for the Golden Visa since October 2023.

---

### France Investor Program
Known as the **France Talent Passport Program**, it allows a direct investment of at least **€300,000** in **commercial or industrial assets**, made personally or through a company in which you hold at least 30%, and provides a **multi-year residence permit of up to 4 years**, renewable.

---

### Germany Investment Program
Germany allows investors to set up or invest in a **business**; buying real estate does not qualify on its own.

- **Residence permit for self-employment**, usually issued for up to 3 years
- **Settlement permit** (permanent residency) possible after 3 years if the business succeeds
- Citizenship eligibility after 5 years of residence (since June 2024)

---

### Greece Golden VISA Program
Investors can obtain residency through **real estate investment**.

- Property thresholds from **€400,000** (**€800,000** in the most popular areas), with **beautiful landscapes and rich history**
- Tourism-driven economy provides additional business opportunities

---

### Ireland Investment Visa Program
Ireland closed its **Immigrant Investor Programme** to new applications on 15 February 2023, so this route is no longer available.

---

### Other European Countries Offering Residency Programs
Hungary, Malta, Cyprus, Latvia, Monaco, Switzerland and others offer **residence** routes for investors, each on its own terms; Malta and Cyprus grant permanent residence, while the others start with temporary permits. Spain ended its Golden Visa on 3 April 2025.
