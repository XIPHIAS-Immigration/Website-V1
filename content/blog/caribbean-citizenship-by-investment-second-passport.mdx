## Dominica

**Dominica’s Citizenship by Investment** program started in **1993**. Qualifying **investments**:

1. **Donation to the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF)** — *(**Dominica Citizenship by Donation Program**)*
   - **USD 200,000**: **Main applicant alone**
   - **USD 250,000**: **Main applicant + up to three dependants**
   - **+ USD 25,000** for **each additional dependant under 18**; **+ USD 40,000** for each aged 18 or over
2. **Government-approved real estate**
   - Minimum **USD 200,000**, plus a **government fee** from **USD 75,000**; **hold** for **3 years** (5 years if the next buyer is also a CBI applicant)

Learn more: [**Dominica Citizenship by Investment**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/dominica-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html)

## Saint Lucia

**Saint Lucia** is a tropical **Eastern Caribbean** island known for **safety** and stunning landscapes. Typical **processing time**: ~**3 months**. Qualifying **investments** (choose one):

1. **National Economic Fund (NEF)**
   - **USD 240,000**: **Applicant alone or with up to three qualifying dependants**
2. **Approved real estate**
   - **USD 300,000** minimum in **high-end resort/hotel/boutique** projects, plus administrative fees from **USD 30,000**
3. **Enterprise projects** *(eligible sectors)*
   - **Specialty restaurants**, **cruise ports & marinas**, **agro-processing**, **pharmaceuticals**, **ports/bridges/roads/highways**, **research facilities**, **offshore universities**

Guide: [**St. Lucia Citizenship by Investment**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/saint-lucia-citizenship-by-investment.html)

## Grenada

**Grenada**, the **“Island of Spice,”** is the only one of these five countries with a **US E-2 treaty**: Grenadian citizens may apply for the **USA E-2 Treaty Investor Visa**, with eligibility decided by US consular officers.

**Investment options**:

1. **Approved real estate**
   - **USD 220,000** in a project approved at that option, or **USD 350,000** in other approved projects
   - Plus a **government fee** of **USD 50,000** for a single applicant, couple or family of up to four
   - **+ USD 25,000** per **additional dependant** beyond four
2. **National Transformation Fund (NTF)** *(donation)*
   - **USD 150,000**: **Single applicant**
   - **USD 200,000**: **Family of 4**
   - **+ USD 25,000** per **additional dependant**

Details: [**Grenada Citizenship by Investment**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html)

## St. Kitts & Nevis

**St. Kitts & Nevis** launched **CBI** in **1984** to attract **foreign direct investment**.

**Investment routes**:

1. **Approved real estate** — from **USD 325,000** in an approved development, held for **7 years**
2. **Sustainable Island State Contribution (SISC)** *(donation)*
   - **USD 250,000**: **Main applicant or family of up to four**
   - **+ USD 25,000**: **Each additional dependant under 18**
   - **+ USD 50,000**: **Each additional dependant aged 18 or over**
3. **Public Benefit Option** — from **USD 250,000** in an approved public benefit project

Program info: [**St. Kitts & Nevis Citizenship by Investment**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/saint-kitts-and-nevis-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html)

## Antigua & Barbuda

To qualify for **Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship**:

1. **National Development Fund (NDF)** — **USD 230,000** (same amount for any family size)
2. **Real estate** — from **USD 300,000**, held for **5 years**
3. **Business investment** — **USD 1.5 million** as a sole investor, or **USD 5 million** jointly (at least **USD 400,000** each)
4. **University of the West Indies Fund** — **USD 260,000** for a family of six or more

Antigua & Barbuda also asks new citizens to spend at least **5 days** in the country during their first **5 years**.

Overview: [**Antigua & Barbuda Citizenship by Investment**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/antigua-and-barbuda-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html)

For Indian citizens the new passport replaces the Indian one. India does not allow dual citizenship: under Section 9 of the Citizenship Act 1955, Indian citizenship ends automatically when another citizenship is voluntarily acquired, and the OCI card available afterwards is not dual citizenship. ## FAQs

**Which Caribbean CBI program is the fastest to process?**
Most **Caribbean CBI** routes complete within a **few months**: **Saint Lucia** states that most applications are processed within **90 days**, and **Dominica** quotes **3–4 months** to approval in principle, subject to **due diligence** and documentation.

**Do I need to live in the Caribbean to keep my second passport?**
Generally, **no residency** is required for **Caribbean CBI**. The exception to note is **Antigua & Barbuda**, which asks new citizens to spend at least 5 days there in their first 5 years.

**Can parents be included as dependents?**
Yes, many **CBI** programs allow **parents** (and sometimes **grandparents**) as **dependents**, subject to **fees** and **dependency** rules.

**Is real estate mandatory, or can I donate instead?**
Most **Caribbean CBI** programs offer **two core options**: a **government fund donation** or **approved real estate investment**; choose based on **family size**, **hold period**, and **exit** goals.
