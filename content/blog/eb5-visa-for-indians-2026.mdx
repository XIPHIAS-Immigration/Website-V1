## Why EB5 Is Back in Focus

The global conversation around investment migration has changed significantly over the last few years.

Investors are no longer looking only at tax benefits or business opportunities.

They are thinking about:

- Education access
- Geographic diversification
- Family mobility
- Long-term residency security
- Opportunities for future generations

This shift is one reason why the **EB5 Investor Visa** continues attracting strong interest from Indian investors.

According to [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/permanent-workers/employment-based-immigration-fifth-preference-eb-5), the EB-5 program was created by Congress to stimulate the U.S. economy through capital investment and job creation — and it remains one of the most direct government-backed pathways to US permanent residency.

For families with long-term plans involving the United States, the program offers a structured pathway toward permanent residency through qualifying investments.

The appeal is not simply the Green Card.
