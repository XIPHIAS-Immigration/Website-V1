The **Caribbean** offers several **Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** routes that enable qualified investors to obtain a **second passport** through **government contributions** or **approved real estate**. Beyond lifestyle and tax advantages, these passports typically provide **visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 100+ countries**. **XIPHIAS Immigration** guides investors end-to-end. For Indian citizens, the new passport replaces the Indian one. India does not allow dual citizenship: under Section 9 of the Citizenship Act 1955, Indian citizenship ends automatically when another citizenship is voluntarily acquired, and the OCI card available afterwards is not dual citizenship.

## Why the Caribbean CBI Route?

- Potential **visa-free travel** to 100+ destinations
- Options to invest in **approved real estate** or make a **national fund contribution**
- Family eligibility (spouse, children; sometimes parents)
- Business-friendly environments and **investor-friendly tax** regimes


## Dominica — Citizenship by Investment Program

**Dominica (Commonwealth of Dominica)** welcomes **high-net-worth** investors to participate via:
- **Economic Diversification Fund** **contribution** (from **USD 200,000**), or
- **Approved real estate** investment (from **USD 200,000**, plus government fees)

**Key notes:** Applications are submitted **only through authorized agents**. Typical documentation includes **medical** and **police clearance**, and every applicant aged **16 or over** must attend a **mandatory interview**, held virtually. **Travel note:** Dominica nationals have needed a **UK visa** since 19 July 2023, and the US partially suspended visa issuance to Dominica nationals from 1 January 2026.


## Saint Lucia — Citizenship by Investment Program

Launched in **2015**, **Saint Lucia** offers citizenship via:
- **National Economic Fund** **donation** (from **USD 240,000**), or
- **Approved real estate** investment (from **USD 300,000**)

**Highlights:** Growing, resilient economy; renowned natural beauty (volcanic beaches, reef diving). Clean **background** and **medical** certificates are required. Processing can complete in **about 3 months**. **Travel note:** Saint Lucia nationals have needed a **UK visa** since 5 March 2026.


## Grenada — Citizenship by Investment Program

**Grenada**, the “**Spice Island**,” grants citizenship through:
- **National Transformation Fund** **donation** (from **USD 150,000** for a single applicant), or
- **Approved real estate** (tourism/agri/manufacturing), from **USD 220,000**

**Standout benefit:** Access to pursue the **U.S. E-2 Investor Visa** via **Grenadian** citizenship (subject to U.S. treaty policy). Applicants need **police clearance**, **medical**, and must apply through a **registered international agent**. Grenada remains visa-free to the **UK** with an ETA.


## St. Kitts & Nevis — Citizenship by Investment Program

A dual-island nation known for mountains and beaches, **St. Kitts & Nevis** offers:
- **Sustainable Island State Contribution** (from **USD 250,000**), or
- **Pre-approved real estate** projects (from **USD 325,000**)

**Highlights:** broad **visa-free** access, including the **UK** with an ETA; **no physical presence** required to obtain citizenship. The Citizenship by Investment Unit quotes **120–180 days** to approval in principle, and main applicants must attend an interview.


## Antigua & Barbuda — Citizenship by Investment Program

An independent **Commonwealth** state famed for **365 beaches**, offering:
- **National Development Fund** **donation** (**USD 230,000**), or
- **Approved real estate** (from **USD 300,000**) / **business investment** (from **USD 1.5 million**)

**Benefits:** broad **visa-free** access, including the **UK** with an ETA; membership in the **UN**, **Commonwealth**, and **OAS**. Applicants must apply via a **registered agent** and be **18+**. New citizens must spend at least **5 days** in Antigua & Barbuda during their first **5 years**, and the US partially suspended visa issuance to Antigua & Barbuda nationals from 1 January 2026.

## FAQs

**1) Donation vs. Real Estate—what’s better?**
**Donation** usually has **lower entry amounts** and simpler exits, while **real estate** can offer **asset holding** and potential **returns** (subject to **minimum hold periods** and project performance).

**2) Can I include family members?**
Most **CBI** programs allow adding **spouses** and **dependent children**; some also include **parents**/**grandparents** for an additional fee.

**3) Do I need to visit or live in the country?**
Many Caribbean **CBI** programs **do not require** an initial **visit** or **residency** to obtain citizenship (ongoing obligations may vary by country).

**4) How long does it take?**
Timelines vary. For example, **Saint Lucia** often completes in **~3 months**, while **St. Kitts & Nevis** quotes **120–180 days** to approval in principle. Actual times depend on **due diligence** and **file completeness**.
