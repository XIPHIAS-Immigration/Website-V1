Australia is a prosperous, multicultural country, and for many years it ran dedicated visas for business owners, **investors** and **entrepreneurs**. Those visas are now closed: the Business Talent visa (subclass 132) closed on 1 July 2021, and the Business Innovation and Investment Program (subclasses 188 and 888) closed permanently to new applicants on 31 July 2024. There is currently no open federal business or investor visa, so the categories below are described for reference only.

## Australia's Former Business Investment Visa Categories

For the routes that remain open, such as employer-sponsored visas and the National Innovation visa (subclass 858), see our [Australia visa guidance](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/skilled/australia).

### 1. Business Talent Visa, Subclass 132 (Permanent) — closed 1 July 2021

**Venture Capital Entrepreneur stream (132B):**  
A permanent visa to start a new business or develop an existing one. It required **AUD 1 million** in venture capital funding from a member of the Australian Investment Council (AIC).

### 2. Subclass 188 Business Innovation and Investment Visa (Provisional) — closed 31 July 2024

This provisional visa let business owners and entrepreneurs own and manage a business in Australia. It required an **EOI submission**, **state or territory nomination** and an **invitation to apply**.

### 3. Significant Investor Stream (Subclass 188C)

Required an **AUD 5 million** complying investment and nomination by a state or territory government or **Austrade**. It was a stream of the Subclass 188 visa for high-value investors.

### 4. Subclass 888 Business Innovation & Investment Visa (Permanent)

The permanent stage for Subclass 188 holders who met their business or investment requirements. Family members could be included.

### 5. Significant Investor Extension Stream

Holders of Subclass 188 in the Significant Investor stream could extend their provisional visa with a further nomination.

### 6. Premium Investor Stream

For investors able to invest **AUD 15 million** in Australia, with nomination by **Austrade**. This stream is also closed.

### 7. Entrepreneur Stream (Subclass 188E)

A provisional visa for entrepreneurs with a complying **funding agreement** (at least AUD 200,000 under the original rules) to run a business in Australia. It closed with the rest of the program on 31 July 2024.

## FAQs

**Q1. What is the difference between Subclass 188 (provisional) and Subclass 888 (permanent)?**  
A. Subclass 188 was the provisional visa; Subclass 888 was the permanent stage after meeting nomination and business or investment criteria. The program closed to new applicants on 31 July 2024.

**Q2. How much did the Significant Investor stream (Subclass 188C) require?**  
A. AUD 5 million in complying investments under the Business Innovation and Investment Program (BIIP), plus nomination. The stream closed with the rest of the program on 31 July 2024.

**Q3. Can family members be included?**  
A. Yes. Most streams allowed eligible family members (spouse/partner and dependent children).

**Q4. Is any Australian business visa open now?**  
A. No federal business or investor visa is open. Founders and investors with an exceptional record may qualify for the National Innovation visa (subclass 858), and employer-sponsored visas such as the Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482) remain open.
