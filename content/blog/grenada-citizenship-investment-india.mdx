Are you an **Indian investor** looking for better global mobility, a secure backup plan for your family, or a smoother way to expand your business internationally? The **[Grenada Citizenship by Investment (CBI)](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html)** program offers a fast, flexible, and reliable pathway to **second citizenship**.

This small Caribbean island provides one of the **world’s most attractive citizenship options**. Here’s why Indian nationals are increasingly choosing Grenada.

## What is Grenada Citizenship by Investment?

Since 2013, [**Grenada’s CBI program**](https://www.xiphiasimmigration.com/grenada-citizenship-by-investment-immigration.html) has allowed **global investors** to support Grenada’s growth and, in return, gain **full citizenship**.

- **Visa-Free Travel:** Including the Schengen Area, the UK (with an ETA) and China
- **No Residency Requirement**
- **Citizenship for Life**, passed to future generations

## Why Indians Are Choosing Grenada

Key reasons for the rising demand among Indian investors:

- The Indian passport offers limited visa-free access, restricting global mobility
- A **US E-2 treaty**, the basis for the **E-2 Investor Visa**
- No education or management-experience requirement

Note that India does not allow dual citizenship: acquiring Grenadian citizenship ends Indian citizenship under Section 9 of the Citizenship Act, 1955, and OCI status is not dual citizenship.

## Top Benefits of Grenada Citizenship

### 1. US Access via E-2 Visa
Grenada is the only one of the five Eastern Caribbean citizenship-by-investment countries with an **E-2 Investor Treaty** with the U.S. (in force since 3 March 1989), a route for citizens to **live and work in the U.S.** by setting up a business. Whether an individual qualifies for an E-2 visa is decided by US consular officers.

### 2. Visa-Free Travel
Citizens can travel freely to:

- UK (with an ETA)
- Schengen countries
- China
- Singapore

Perfect for frequent travelers, business owners, and families seeking international education.

### 3. Family Inclusion
Covers spouse, dependent children and dependent parents; age limits are confirmed with Grenada's Citizenship by Investment Committee for each file.

## Investment Options for Grenada Citizenship

To qualify, choose one of these paths:

### A) Government Donation
- **Amount:** $150,000 (single applicant); $200,000 for a couple or family of four
- Non-refundable contribution to the **National Transformation Fund**

### B) Real Estate Investment
- **Amount:** Minimum $220,000 in a project approved at that price ($350,000 for other approved projects), plus a $50,000 government fee
- Suitable for ROI through rentals or resale

## Grenada Citizenship for Indian Nationals
Advantages over other Caribbean/EU programs:

- **US E-2 treaty** (unique among the five Eastern Caribbean programmes)
- Fast route to global mobility and U.S. business access
- Alternative to EB-5 or H-1B visa programs

## FAQs

**Who can apply for Grenada CBI as an Indian national?**
Indian investors with required net worth, good character, and willingness to make a qualifying investment.

**What is the minimum investment for Indians?**
$150,000 for the government donation (single applicant) or $220,000 for real estate in a project approved at that price, plus government fees.

**How long does it take to obtain Grenada citizenship?**
Timelines vary by case; we confirm the current processing time with Grenada's Citizenship by Investment Committee before filing.

**Can families be included?**
Yes, spouse, dependent children and dependent parents can be included.
