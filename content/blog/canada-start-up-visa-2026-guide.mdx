Canada’s **Start-Up Visa (SUV)** program has undergone major changes, leaving many entrepreneurs, founders, and investors wondering what’s next. Instead of relying on rumours, let’s look at what is officially known, what continues, what has paused, and — most importantly — how you can still plan a successful pathway toward Canada or other strong destinations.

## What Actually Happened to the Canada Start-Up Visa?

As of **June 30, 2026**, Canada's Start-Up Visa program is **paused**. Intake for new applications ended on December 31, 2025; applicants holding a valid 2025 commitment certificate had until June 30, 2026 to apply. Applications received before the deadline remain in processing, but timelines may remain longer than usual. The optional Start-Up Visa work permit stopped being accepted on December 19, 2025, except extensions for people already in Canada on one.

IRCC has also said that a **new, targeted pilot program for immigrant entrepreneurs** will follow. As of September 29, 2026, no replacement has launched on canada.ca, and no structure, criteria or opening date has been published.

**Key takeaways:**
- **SUV is paused and is not accepting new applications**
- **Existing applicants remain in process**  
- **IRCC has promised a targeted entrepreneur pilot, but none has launched yet**
- **Federal business admissions are set at 500 a year under the 2026–2028 Immigration Levels Plan**

## Why Did This Happen?

The clearest explanation is in the numbers. The 2026–2028 Immigration Levels Plan sets the **Federal Business category at 500 admissions a year**, down from 2,000 in 2025. Canada's other federal business route, the Self-Employed Persons Program, has been paused since April 30, 2024, and IRCC extended that pause until further notice on December 19, 2025. At the same time, the federal government raised Provincial Nominee Program admissions to **91,500** for 2026, so business immigration now runs mainly through the provinces.

## Who Is Safe and Who Is Not?

You’re generally **still on track** if:

- IRCC accepted your SUV permanent residence application before the cut-off  
- Your case genuinely meets eligibility requirements  

These applicants continue under the previous structure, but patience is necessary.

You need to **rethink your strategy** if:

- You were planning to apply but had not filed by the deadline  
- You are waiting purely for the SUV to reopen  
- You are assuming the promised entrepreneur pilot will offer the same terms  

Waiting without a strategy is the worst mistake right now. **Immigration should be planned, not left to uncertainty.**

## So What Should You Actually Do?

If Canada remains your goal, there are still **credible and active pathways available**.

### 1.Provincial Entrepreneur Programs (PNP)

Several provinces continue to welcome genuine business investors, founders, and experienced entrepreneurs. These programs allow you to:

- **Start a new business** (typical Entrepreneur stream)  
- **Purchase and actively operate an existing business** (Owner-Operator route)  
- **Actively manage the business day-to-day**  
- **Transition toward permanent residency** through provincial nomination  

For serious business owners, these remain some of the strongest routes to Canada. Provincial business streams still accepting applications include [British Columbia](/residency/canada/british-columbia-entrepreneur-base) (Base and Regional streams), [Prince Edward Island](/residency/canada/pei-work-permit), [Newfoundland & Labrador](/residency/canada/newfoundland-international-entrepreneur), [Nova Scotia](/residency/canada/nova-scotia-entrepreneur-stream), [Northwest Territories](/residency/canada/northwest-territories-business), [New Brunswick](/residency/canada/new-brunswick-business-immigration), [Manitoba](/residency/canada/manitoba-entrepreneur), [Alberta](/residency/canada/alberta-self-employed-farmer) and [Yukon](/residency/canada/yukon-business). Ontario has closed its Entrepreneur Stream, and Saskatchewan permanently closed its Entrepreneur and Farm Owner/Operator pathways on March 27, 2025.

### 2.Intra-Company Transfer (ICT)

If you already own or manage a business abroad, ICT allows you to:

- Open a **Canadian branch**  
- Move as an **executive, manager, or key professional**  
- Build presence and later explore **PR pathways**  

When structured properly, ICT is one of the most strategic business expansion options.

### 3.Skilled & Business-Focused Work Permits

Depending on your expertise and goals, there are legitimate ways to:

- **Enter Canada**  
- **Work or lead operations**  
- **Progress strategically toward long-term status**  

This keeps your journey active instead of paused.

### 4.Exploring Other Strong Immigration Destinations

If your ultimate goal is:

- **Residency**  
- **Business expansion**  
- **Better lifestyle**  
- **Family security**  

Limiting yourself to a single program is an unnecessary risk. Several countries offer powerful entrepreneur residency opportunities, and smart applicants keep options open.

## What About the Promised Entrepreneur Pilot?

IRCC has said a targeted pilot for immigrant entrepreneurs will follow — but here’s what you must understand:

- **Nothing has launched as of September 29, 2026, and no official structure has been announced**  
- **It may have stricter eligibility**  
- **It may prioritize proven founders and impactful businesses**  
- **It may include caps, quotas, or performance criteria**  

**Translation:** do not sit idle waiting. Use this time to strengthen your profile, build business traction, and prepare strategically.

## FAQs – Quick Answers You Actually Need

**Is the Canada Start-Up Visa coming back?**  
IRCC has promised a targeted pilot for immigrant entrepreneurs, but none has launched as of September 29, 2026, and the old SUV model is unlikely to return in the same format.

**Can new applicants apply for SUV now?**  
No. New applications stopped on December 31, 2025, and the final window for holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate closed on June 30, 2026.

**What happens to existing SUV applications?**  
They continue processing, but timelines may remain extended.

**What is the best alternative now?**  
Entrepreneur PNP programs, ICT, and selected work permit strategies — depending on your profile.
