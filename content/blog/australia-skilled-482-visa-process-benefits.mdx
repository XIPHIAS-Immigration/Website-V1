## Introduction

The **Australia Skilled 482 Visa** is now the **Skills in Demand visa**, which replaced the **Temporary Skill Shortage (TSS) visa** on 7 December 2024 and kept the same subclass number. It enables skilled professionals to **work in Australia** through three streams — **Specialist Skills**, **Core Skills**, and **Labour Agreement** — designed to fill skill gaps in the Australian workforce.

The visa is **valid for up to 4 years** and requires **sponsorship from an approved Australian business**. Holders can later move to **Australian permanent residency**, usually through the Employer Nomination Scheme (Subclass 186).

---

## Categories / Streams

### Specialist Skills Stream
- For any ANZSCO 2022 occupation except Major Groups 3, 7 and 8, with a salary above the **Specialist Skills Income Threshold**.  
- Valid stay: **up to 4 years**.  
- The fastest stream: half of applications are decided in **37 days**.

### Core Skills Stream
- For occupations on the **Core Skills Occupation List (CSOL)**, with a salary above the **Core Skills Income Threshold**.  
- Valid stay: **up to 4 years**.  
- Provides a pathway to **permanent residency**.

### Labour Agreement Stream
- For skilled workers nominated by **employers under a labour agreement** with the Australian Government.  
- Valid stay: **up to 4 years**.  

### Family Members
- **Partners and dependent children** can be included, with work and study rights.  
- Valid stay: as per the **primary visa holder’s** validity.  

**Processing Time:** From **37 days** for half of Specialist Skills applications to about **9 months** for 90% of Core Skills and Labour Agreement applications, according to Home Affairs.

## Requirements for Australia Skilled 482 Visa

Applicants must:

- Be **nominated by an approved sponsor** for a skilled position.  
- Have the **required skills and qualifications** for the role, and at least **1 year** of relevant work experience.  
- Earn at least the **income threshold** for your stream.  
- **Demonstrate English language proficiency** through approved testing.

## Benefits of Australia Skilled 482 Visa

1. Only **1 year** of relevant work experience is required.  
2. **Fast processing** in the Specialist Skills stream (half of applications decided in 37 days).  
3. **Partners and dependent children** can be included in the application.  
4. Access to **Australia’s world-class healthcare system**.  
5. Career opportunities in **dynamic industries**.  
6. Exposure to **global business networks**.  
7. **Safe and secure** living environment.  
8. A **diverse and multicultural society**.  
9. **High standard of living**.  
10. Access to **Australia’s scenic and popular destinations**.

## FAQs

**1. Who can apply for the Australia Skilled 482 Visa?**  
Skilled workers nominated by an **approved Australian employer**.

**2. What are the streams under the 482 Visa?**  
The Skills in Demand visa has three streams: **Specialist Skills**, **Core Skills** and **Labour Agreement**. The TSS visa and its Short-Term and Medium-Term streams closed on 6 December 2024.

**3. Can my family accompany me to Australia on this visa?**  
Yes, your **partner and dependent children** can be included as **dependents**.
