## What is a European Golden Visa?

A **European Golden Visa** is a **residency-by-investment program**. By investing in real estate, business, or government-approved funds, you gain **residency rights** and may eventually apply for **citizenship**.

**Benefits include:**

- Long-term residency in Europe
- Visa-free travel across the **Schengen Zone**
- Access to **quality education, healthcare, and lifestyle**
- Opportunities to invest or start **businesses in Europe**

## Golden Visa Programs by Country

### 1. Portugal Golden Visa
The [Portugal Golden Visa](/residency/portugal) allows residency through **investment funds, business, or other capital investment**; real estate purchases have not qualified since October 2023.

**Why Portugal?**

- Pleasant climate and vibrant cities
- Growing tech and startup ecosystem
- Safe and stable environment for families

**Visa Details:**

- Residency permit after investment approval
- **Permanent residence** after five years; citizenship after **10 years** of legal residence for most non-EU nationals, including Indians (since 19 May 2026)

---

### 2. Greece Golden Visa
The [Greek Golden Visa](/residency/greece) provides residency through **real estate or business investment**.

**Highlights:**

- Strategic location connecting Europe, Asia, and Africa
- Relaxed lifestyle and rich cultural heritage
- Suitable for families or individuals

**Visa Details:**

- Residency permit for applicant and family
- Freedom to travel within the **European Union**

---

### 3. Malta Golden Visa
The [Malta Golden Visa](/residency/malta), officially the Malta Permanent Residence Programme, grants permanent residence; Malta's citizenship-by-investment route was repealed in 2025.

**Advantages:**

- Investor-friendly policies
- Gateway to the **European Union**
- Access to work, travel, and education in EU

**Visa Details:**

- Qualify through a property purchase (from €375,000) or lease (from €14,000 a year), plus a €37,000 government contribution and a €60,000 administrative fee
- Includes immediate family members

---

### 4. Cyprus Golden Visa
The [Cyprus Golden Visa](/residency/cyprus) provides **fast-track residency** through property or business investment.

**Why Cyprus?**

- Strategic location for business and travel
- Attractive tax policies for entrepreneurs
- Opportunity to grow ventures internationally

**Visa Details:**

- Obtain residency for applicant and family
- Access to high-quality lifestyle, education, and healthcare
- Cyprus is an EU member but not part of the Schengen area

---

### 5. Latvia Golden Visa
The [Latvia Golden Visa](/residency/latvia/latvia-business-investment) now offers residency through **business investment** only: Latvia abolished its real-estate route on 15 September 2026. Share capital of €50,000 in a Latvian company (€100,000 for a larger company) plus a €10,000 state fee qualifies, and the permit is issued for two years.

**Benefits:**

- Growing economy and investor-friendly environment
- Peaceful lifestyle and strategic European location

**Visa Details:**

- Obtain residency after qualifying investment
- Freedom to travel across **Schengen Area**
