Investments across the globe are an important proposition. They create profits for the investor and generate jobs and growth for the destination country. Some countries grant **residency by investment** while a few offer **citizenship by investment (CBI)**. The status you receive depends on the **program** and **investment** selected.

**Europe** hosts multiple programs that invite **foreign direct investment**. No EU country now offers **citizenship by investment**, but many provide **residency** and a pathway to citizenship after meeting residence and other requirements. For Indian citizens, note that India does not allow dual citizenship: under Section 9 of the Citizenship Act 1955, Indian citizenship ends automatically when another citizenship is voluntarily acquired, and the OCI card available afterwards is not dual citizenship.

## Cyprus

Located south of Türkiye, **Cyprus** is a prosperous Mediterranean island with an economy driven by **tourism**, **transportation**, and **telecommunications**. Through its **Cyprus Investment Immigration programs**, investors have historically accessed:

- **Cyprus Permanent Residency Program**, which remains open  
- **Cyprus Citizenship Program**, which ended on 1 November 2020  

The permanent residency route is based mainly on **approved real estate** investment. Cyprus is an EU member but is not part of the **Schengen Area**.

## Malta

**Malta** is an advanced, stable economy between Sicily and North Africa. With strong **tourism**, **agriculture**, and an active **film** industry, its real estate is perennially in demand. Malta’s investment migration options include:

- **Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP):** a qualifying property purchase from **€375,000** or a lease from **€14,000** a year, plus a **€60,000** administrative fee and a **€37,000** government contribution  
- **Malta citizenship by investment** was repealed in 2025, after the EU Court of Justice ruled against it on 29 April 2025, and is no longer available

## Portugal — Portugal Golden Visa

**Portugal** offers several routes to **residency by investment** via the **Portugal Golden Visa**:

- **Real estate:** no longer qualifies; Portugal removed property purchase as a Golden Visa route in October 2023  
- **Job creation:** Create **10** jobs in a **Portuguese** business  
- **Culture/heritage support:** **€250,000**  
- **Research activities:** **€500,000**  
- **Investment or venture capital funds:** **€500,000**  
- **Company capitalisation:** **€500,000**  

The **residency** is renewable and has **minimal stay** needs: 7 days in the first year and 14 days in each later two-year period. Permanent residence is available after **5 years**; since 19 May 2026, Indian nationals need **10 years** of residence to qualify for Portuguese citizenship.

## France — France Talent Passport

**France** supports investors and entrepreneurs through the **France Talent Passport** with multiple pathways:

- Invest in or start a **French business** (startup or operating company)  
- **Economic investment:** a direct investment of at least **€300,000** in the fixed assets of a French company  
- Support for **innovative projects** aligned with national priorities

## Greece — Greece Golden Visa

The **Greece Permanent Residency** program grants residency to investors who purchase **Greek real estate**:

- **Minimum investment:** **€800,000** in Attica, the Thessaloniki regional unit, Mykonos, Thira (Santorini) and islands of over 3,100 people; **€400,000** elsewhere; **€250,000** only for converting commercial property to residential use or restoring a listed building  
- **Property rules:** one property must meet the threshold, with at least **120 m²** of main living space (except on the €250,000 routes)  
- **Processing time:** the statutory period is **50 days**  
- **Visit requirement:** short in-person visit (e.g., **3–4 days**) to complete formalities; after issuance, ongoing presence requirements are limited

## Latvia — Latvian Residency Program

Launched in **2010**, **Latvia’s** investor residency enables **Schengen** travel, but a new Immigration Law in force from **15 September 2026** abolished the real estate and bank routes. The route that remains:

- **Company share capital:** **€50,000** (or **€100,000** for a larger company), plus a **€10,000** state fee, for a **2-year** permit  

Naturalisation requires **5 years** of residence and language and civics exams, and Latvia allows dual citizenship only with EU, EFTA and NATO countries, Australia, New Zealand and Brazil.

## Spain — Spain Golden Visa

Spain abolished its **Golden Visa** on **3 April 2025**, so the former **€500,000** real estate route is no longer available. Remote workers can still apply for Spain’s **digital nomad visa**.

## Monaco — Residency & Path to Citizenship

Long a hub for the global elite, **Monaco** grants residency to people with accommodation in Monaco (owned or rented) and **sufficient financial means**, attested by a Monegasque bank; the law sets no fixed investment amount. To naturalize, a **long-term residence** period (e.g., **~10 years**) is generally required.

## Moldova — Citizenship by Investment

**Moldova** introduced a **Citizenship by Investment** program in **2018**, but it is no longer open to new applicants.

## Montenegro — Citizenship by Investment

Launched in **2020**, **Montenegro’s CBI** accepted applications until the end of **2022** and is now closed.

## How to Choose the Right Program

- **Status goal:** **Citizenship by Investment (CBI)** vs **Golden Visa**/**Residency by Investment**  
- **Budget & asset mix:** **Donation vs real estate vs business**  
- **Family coverage:** Spouse, children, and (where allowed) parents/grandparents  
- **Timeline & presence:** Processing speed and **physical stay** rules  
- **Mobility:** Target **visa-free** destinations and regional access (e.g., **Schengen**)  
- **Exit/ROI:** Real estate **hold period**, resale potential, and returns

## FAQs

**1) What’s the difference between a *Golden Visa* and *Citizenship by Investment (CBI)*?**  
A **Golden Visa** grants **residency** with potential **citizenship** later (subject to residence and other rules). **CBI** grants **citizenship** directly after due diligence and investment.

**2) Can I include my family members?**  
Most programs allow **spouses** and **dependent children**; some also include **parents/grandparents** for an additional fee.

**3) Do these programs require me to live in the country full-time?**  
**Residency** programs often have **minimal stay** requirements; some **CBI** programs have **no mandatory residence**. Always check each program’s specific rules.

**4) Is real estate the only route?**  
No. Many programs offer **government contributions**, **fund/share investments**, **job creation**, or **business formation** as alternatives to **real estate**.

**5) How long does approval take?**  
Timelines vary by country and due diligence. Some **CBI** routes conclude in **a few months**, while **golden visas** may take **several weeks to a few months** for initial residency, with citizenship eligibility after the statutory period.
