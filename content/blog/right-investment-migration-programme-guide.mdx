## Investment Migration Options

### Investment in New Business
Countries like the UK, USA, Canada, and New Zealand welcome individuals who invest in new businesses and create employment. These programs provide residency in exchange for economic contributions.

### Investment in Startups
With the rise of entrepreneurship, countries have introduced visas for innovative business founders. Examples:
- **UK Innovator Founder Visa**, which replaced the UK Innovator and Start-up visas in April 2023
- **Canada Start-Up Visa**, now paused: its intake ended on 31 December 2025

### Investment in Real Estate
Many countries offer residency through real estate investments. Benefits include returns on investment, freedom to reside and travel, and visa-free access to multiple countries. Top destinations: Turkey, Greece, Malta and the Caribbean citizenship programmes (Dominica, St. Lucia, St. Kitts & Nevis, Grenada, Antigua & Barbuda). Portugal removed real estate from its Golden Visa in October 2023, and Spain ended its Golden Visa on 3 April 2025.

## Country-Specific Programs

### Canada
- **Provincial entrepreneur streams:** Invest in and run a business in provinces such as British Columbia, Manitoba, New Brunswick or Nova Scotia to be nominated for Permanent Residency. Citizenship eligibility after 3 years of physical presence in the last 5.
- **Closed routes:** the federal Immigrant Investor Program closed in 2014, the Start-Up Visa is paused (its intake ended on 31 December 2025), Saskatchewan closed its entrepreneur category on 27 March 2025, and Ontario has closed its entrepreneur stream.

### USA
- **EB-5 Investor Visa:** Invest $800,000–$1,050,000 depending on area designation, create jobs, and obtain Green Card. Includes Integrity Fund fee of $1,000. Program valid through 2027.

### Australia
- **Business Innovation and Investment Visa Subclass 188:** Closed permanently on 31 July 2024, together with the subclass 888 permanent visa. Australia now has no open federal business or investor migration route; the National Innovation visa (subclass 858) is for people with an internationally recognised record of exceptional achievement.

## FAQs

**What are the main types of investment migration programs?**
Investment in new businesses, startups, and real estate are the primary pathways.

**Which countries offer investment migration programs?**
Canada, the USA, the UK, Portugal, Turkey, Greece, Malta, and select Caribbean nations.

**What is the minimum investment for the US EB-5 program?**
$800,000 in Targeted Employment Areas and $1,050,000 in Non-Targeted Employment Areas.

**Can investors gain citizenship through these programs?**
Yes, most programs lead to permanent residency first, with citizenship eligibility after meeting residence and other requirements.
