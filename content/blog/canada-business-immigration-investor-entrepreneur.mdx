## Canada Business Immigration Overview

**Canada** remains one of the most sought-after destinations for business investors and entrepreneurs. With expanding cross-border **investment opportunities**, many foreign investors are growing their **net worth** while contributing to Canada’s economic development.

**XIPHIAS Immigration** provides strategic guidance for investors seeking to establish or expand their ventures in Canada. Most of **Canada’s business immigration programs** can lead to **Permanent Residency (PR)** after meeting specific requirements.

Canada currently has no open federal investor or entrepreneur programme: the Start-Up Visa stopped accepting new applications on 31 December 2025 and was paused on 30 June 2026, and the Self-Employed Persons Program has been paused since 30 April 2024. Business immigration now runs through the provincial and territorial nominee streams.

---

## Start-Up Visa Program (Closed)

Canada introduced the **Start-Up Visa** in 2013 for entrepreneurs with **viable business ideas** backed by a **designated organization**. New applications stopped on **31 December 2025**, holders of a valid 2025 commitment certificate had until **30 June 2026** to file, and IRCC paused the programme on that date. IRCC continues to process applications accepted before the cut-off and has said a targeted pilot for immigrant entrepreneurs will follow; as at 29 September 2026, none had launched.

## Provincial Business Immigration Pathways

### Manitoba Business Investor Stream (BIS)
**Manitoba’s BIS** offers two major options:

- **Entrepreneur Pathway:**  
  Invest in or start a business in Manitoba. This stream replaces the former **MPNP-B** and evaluates applicants based on **personal net worth**, **investment capacity**, and **business experience**.

- **Farm Investor Pathway:**  
  For those interested in **agricultural or farm business investments** in Manitoba. Applicants must demonstrate **farming and agri-business management experience**.

---

### Prince Edward Island (PEI) Work Permit Stream
PEI's business route is the **Work Permit Stream**: invest at least **CAD 150,000** for at least **33.33%** ownership of a PEI business, show a verified **net worth of CAD 600,000**, and actively manage the business on a work permit.  
Qualified investors who meet the conditions can receive a **provincial nomination**, paving the way for **Permanent Residency** with **IRCC**.

---

### Saskatchewan SINP — Entrepreneur & Farm Categories (Closed)
Saskatchewan **permanently closed** its SINP Entrepreneur category and Farm Owner/Operator pathway on **27 March 2025**. No new applications, expressions of interest or invitations are being issued.

---

### British Columbia Entrepreneur Immigration
The **BC Entrepreneur Program** is one of the most popular provincial options.  
Investors typically arrive on a **work permit** to conduct research, prepare a **business plan**, and fulfill provincial requirements.  
Once performance conditions are met, BC may issue a **provincial nomination**, allowing the applicant to apply for **PR** with **IRCC**.

> A related option is the **New Brunswick Business Immigration Stream**, which replaced New Brunswick's closed Entrepreneurial Stream: a minimum investment of **CAD 150,000** and a net worth of **CAD 500,000** (**CAD 300,000** for a farming business).

---

### Ontario OINP — Entrepreneur Stream (Closed)
Ontario has closed the **Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP) Entrepreneur Stream**.

### Other Open Streams
Nova Scotia, the Northwest Territories, Newfoundland & Labrador, Alberta and Yukon also run business streams that lead to provincial nomination.

---

## How These Programs Work

Most provincial entrepreneur and investor pathways follow this structure:

1. **Apply for a work permit** to establish or manage the business in Canada.  
2. **Meet performance or investment milestones** during the conditional period.  
3. **Receive a provincial nomination** upon successful completion.  
4. **Apply for Permanent Residency (PR)** with **IRCC** using your nomination certificate.  

Unlike Express Entry, these streams do not require an **Express Entry profile**, although many set a minimum language level; Manitoba's Entrepreneur Pathway, for example, requires CLB 5.

## FAQs

**1. What are the main Canada business immigration options?**  
The open options are the **provincial entrepreneur/investor streams** under the **PNP**, such as **BC Entrepreneur Immigration**, **Manitoba BIS**, the **PEI Work Permit Stream** and the **New Brunswick Business Immigration Stream**. The federal **Start-Up Visa**, the **Saskatchewan SINP** entrepreneur and farm categories and the **OINP Entrepreneur Stream** are closed.

**2. Do I need a language test for investor or entrepreneur streams?**  
Often, yes. **PNP entrepreneur/investor** streams emphasize **net worth**, **investment value**, and **business experience**, but many also set a minimum language level; Manitoba's Entrepreneur Pathway, for example, requires **CLB 5**. Always check the **specific program requirements**.

**3. Can I still apply for the Start-Up Visa?**  
No. New applications stopped on **31 December 2025** and the programme was paused on **30 June 2026**. IRCC continues to process applications accepted before the cut-off.

**4. How do provincial entrepreneur programs lead to PR?**  
Most applicants begin with a **work permit**, fulfill **performance conditions**, then receive a **provincial nomination**. The nomination allows you to apply for **PR** through **IRCC**.
