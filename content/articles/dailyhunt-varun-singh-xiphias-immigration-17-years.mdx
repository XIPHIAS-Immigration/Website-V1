# Dailyhunt Coverage: How Varun Singh Built XIPHIAS Immigration Over 17 Years

**Publication:** Loktej English, syndicated on Dailyhunt
**Published:** June 25, 2026
**Feature:** Varun Singh, Founder and Managing Director, XIPHIAS Immigration
**Source:** [Dailyhunt](https://m.dailyhunt.in/news/india/english/loktej%2Benglish-epaper-loktejen/how%2Bvarun%2Bsingh%2Bbuilt%2Bindias%2Bmost%2Btrusted%2Bimmigration%2Badvisory%2Bfirm%2Bover%2B17%2Byears-newsid-n717342369) / [Loktej English](https://english.loktej.com/article/28185/how-varun-singh-built-india%E2%80%99s-most-trusted-immigration-advisory-firm-over-17-years)

Dailyhunt and Loktej English featured **Varun Singh**, Founder and Managing Director of **XIPHIAS Immigration**, in an article highlighting the company's evolution over 17 years and its role in India's fast-changing global mobility landscape.

The article traces XIPHIAS Immigration's journey from its Bengaluru beginnings in 2009 to becoming a structured advisory firm serving professionals, entrepreneurs, investors, families, and corporate clients across multiple global mobility categories.

---

## Building on credibility and structured guidance

When XIPHIAS Immigration began, India's immigration consulting sector was still developing in terms of structure, credibility, and client awareness. Applicants often faced fragmented information, unrealistic promises, and uncertainty around documentation and eligibility.

The coverage notes that Varun Singh focused on building a model centered on **compliance, process discipline, realistic guidance, and client trust**. Instead of a volume-driven approach, XIPHIAS positioned itself around written consultations, transparent communication, and careful documentation practices.

This advisory-led approach helped the company support applicants who needed clarity before making major career, financial, and family decisions connected to international mobility.

---

## Responding to India's changing mobility mindset

The article also highlights how Indian mobility has changed over the last decade. Earlier, overseas movement was often connected mainly to jobs or education. Today, business owners, investors, entrepreneurs, and globally mobile families increasingly view immigration planning as part of a wider life and business strategy.

As these needs evolved, XIPHIAS Immigration expanded its advisory scope beyond traditional skilled migration to include:

- **Residency by Investment and Golden Visa pathways**
- **Citizenship by Investment advisory**
- **Corporate and business mobility**
- **Skilled migration for professionals**
- **Multi-country global mobility planning**

This broader approach allows XIPHIAS to guide clients based on their destination goals, investment priorities, family security needs, and long-term international plans.

---

## Technology-led immigration advisory

A key theme in the feature is XIPHIAS Immigration's early use of technology. According to the article, the company introduced an in-house CRM system and a dedicated global mobile application as early as 2011 to improve communication, documentation tracking, and case visibility.

For immigration clients, technology can make the process more transparent and organized. At the same time, the article emphasizes that immigration remains a deeply personal decision, requiring expert guidance, human judgment, and careful planning.

XIPHIAS Immigration continues to combine digital systems with personalized advisory support to improve efficiency without compromising clarity, accuracy, or client confidence.

---

## Trust in a sensitive industry

Immigration decisions involve significant financial, professional, and family commitments. A documentation error, misinformation, or poor planning can create serious consequences for applicants.

The Dailyhunt and Loktej English coverage presents trust as a central pillar of XIPHIAS Immigration's growth. Under Varun Singh's leadership, the firm has emphasized transparent communication, structured documentation, compliance awareness, and realistic guidance.

As global immigration rules continue to change across countries, adaptability and credibility remain essential for advisory firms working with skilled professionals, investors, entrepreneurs, and corporate clients.

---

## The rise of strategic global mobility

The article reflects a larger shift in how Indian professionals and entrepreneurs approach global mobility. Many clients are no longer looking only for relocation. They are planning around global market access, business continuity, family security, education opportunities, lifestyle diversification, and long-term residency stability.

This makes the role of immigration advisors more strategic. Clients expect guidance that considers eligibility, compliance, documentation, investment structure, business goals, and long-term outcomes.

XIPHIAS Immigration's 17-year journey mirrors this shift in the market, moving from traditional immigration support to broader global mobility advisory.

---

## XIPHIAS Immigration perspective

For XIPHIAS Immigration, this feature reinforces the importance of ethical, structured, and client-focused advisory in a fast-growing mobility sector.

As Indian demand for skilled migration, investor residency, business expansion, and family mobility continues to grow, applicants need guidance that is transparent, practical, and aligned with changing global regulations.

XIPHIAS Immigration remains committed to helping clients make informed mobility decisions through compliance-first advisory, technology-enabled processes, and personalized support.

---

## Key takeaways

- Varun Singh founded XIPHIAS Immigration in Bengaluru in 2009.
- The firm built its approach around compliance, structured documentation, and client trust.
- XIPHIAS expanded from skilled migration into investor mobility, residency, citizenship, and corporate mobility advisory.
- Technology adoption, including CRM-led case management, has supported transparency and client communication.
- India's mobility mindset is shifting from simple relocation to strategic global planning.
