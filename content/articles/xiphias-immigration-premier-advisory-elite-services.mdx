# XIPHIAS Immigration: Advisory Firm with Elite Services

**September 11, 2018**

XIPHIAS Immigration was founded in 2009 by Mr. Varun Singh. They started with a small office in Banashankari with 5 people and hundreds of clients. The industry was very new and needed organized players like them, with everything having a legal binding. Over the years, XIPHIAS has grown from Banashankari to Koramangala, from 5 to over 200 employees and from hundreds to thousands of happy clients.

XIPHIAS Immigration is an Indian firm with a strong focus on Canadian immigration. The company has helped many clients from around the globe to obtain landed immigrant (permanent resident) status and temporary status by representing them effectively before the Canadian immigration authorities. XIPHIAS complies with high standards of ethics and professionalism. Prior to accepting a case, the company thoroughly reviews the credentials to ensure that it stands a reasonable chance of being accepted by the Government of Canada. In the event that an application does not meet the criteria for success, they inform their prospective client in an honest manner to avoid clients’ wasting their time and hard-earned money. Varun Singh founded XIPHIAS with the vision and mission to provide opportunity for those who have dreams to work and settle abroad.

---

## Vision and Services

XIPHIAS Immigration aims to be a widely trusted advisory firm in the immigration industry and empowers the clients and their families in the entire process of being a global citizen. Their bouquet of services include Skilled Migration Services, Study Abroad Services, Corporate Migration Services and Investment Immigration Services. And, the services don’t just end it here. They also offer Post Landing Assistance, which smoothes the settling down period in a foreign land.

---

## Immigration Services

XIPHIAS Immigration provides immigration, residency, citizenship and global resettlement services. Its work for skilled workers, businesses, high-net-worth individuals and families has brought it into close contact with government officials, immigration lawyers and solicitors, experienced counsellors and consultants, investors and property developers worldwide, and financial institutions for trade and investment.

XIPHIAS is a full service immigration firm. Its main areas of work include Business Immigration, Investor- Entrepreneur Immigration, Federal Skilled Worker Program, Quebec Program, Study Permit, Sponsorship.

The company has represented thousands of applications to date and prepares every application carefully to give it the best chance of approval.

Their unconventional approach for handling cases for skill, investment and business migration, manifests clear understanding, evaluation and dynamic roadmaps to work towards each milestone within government-defined timelines and expectations. To help individual, family and business, our services suffice the needs and requirements through professionals, collaborations and strong way to digitally connect with our clients undefined by location. Indigenous service delivery and perpetual support for making migration journey smooth, is our aim anywhere across the globe.

XIPHIAS Immigration has offices in Bengaluru, Gurugram, Dubai, Melbourne and Waterloo.

## Mastermind Behind the XIPHIAS

XIPHIAS, the Group Company, was founded by Mr. Rohit Kumar in the year 2002 with the company XIPHIAS Software Technologies Pvt. Ltd. which got registered in 2005. Seeing the potential and need for a good Consultancy in the Immigration sector, XIPHIAS Immigration was formed in the year 2009 by Mr. Varun Singh. Over the years, they have moved from a 5 member team to more than 200 across the globe. And, they are still expanding!

The company has a proven record of supporting clients through landing and settlement. Its Canada work is handled through a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (licence R516194) licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants (CICC), and its Australia work through a registered migration agent (MARA 1680615). XIPHIAS is the only immigration consultancy in Bangalore with a CICC-licensed RCIC. Their true assistance and proactive measures helped thousands of people reach heights of success.
