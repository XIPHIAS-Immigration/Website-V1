## Current Qualified Investor thresholds

| Route | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate | **USD 500,000** |
| Securities (five-year hold) | **USD 500,000** |
| Fixed deposit (five-year term) | **USD 750,000** |

The USD 300,000 real-estate figure still advertised widely was a transitional rate that has either
lapsed already or lapses on 13 October 2026. Budget the higher figure.

Note also that **Panama does not permit dual citizenship** — its own naturalisation form requires an
express, irrevocable renunciation. Residence is safe; naturalising is not.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.
