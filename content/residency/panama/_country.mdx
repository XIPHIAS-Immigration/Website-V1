## Current Qualified Investor thresholds

| Route | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate | **USD 500,000** |
| Securities (five-year hold) | **USD 500,000** |
| Fixed deposit (five-year term) | **USD 750,000** |

The **USD 300,000** figure still advertised across this industry was a transitional rate. On one
reading of the decree it lapsed in October 2024; on the other it lapses on 13 October 2026. Plan for
USD 500,000.

**Friendly Nations** no longer grants permanent residence immediately — it now gives two years
provisional residence first, from USD 200,000. We cannot confirm whether India is on the
friendly-nations list, because the official decrees are image-only scans, so we ask Migración per case.

**Panama does not permit dual citizenship.** Naturalisation requires an express and irrevocable
renunciation of your previous nationality, and Article 13 treats acquiring another nationality as
tacit renunciation.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.
