## The real estate threshold is USD 500,000

Panama's Qualified Investor Visa is widely advertised at **USD 300,000**. That was a transitional rate,
and it has either already expired or is about to:

| Route | Current minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate | **USD 500,000** |
| Securities (five-year hold) | **USD 500,000** |
| Fixed deposit (five-year term) | **USD 750,000** |

The transitional USD 300,000 sits in an amended article whose wording is genuinely ambiguous about
which decree starts the clock. On one reading it lapsed in **October 2024**; on the other it lapses on
**13 October 2026**. Both readings point the same way for anyone planning today: **budget USD 500,000.**

## Friendly Nations has changed too
It no longer grants permanent residence immediately. It now gives **two years provisional** residence
first. Eligibility is limited to the nationalities listed in Executive Decree 226 of 2021, so XIPHIAS
confirms whether **India is on the current list** with Migración before advising on this route.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.
