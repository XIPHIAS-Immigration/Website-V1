## The Active Investor Plus visa was rebuilt on 1 April 2025

The old weighted-investment system is gone, and so is the English-language requirement. There are now
two categories:

| Category | Investment | Minimum hold | Days required in NZ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Growth** | **NZ$5 million** | 36 months | **21 days** |
| **Balanced** | **NZ$10 million** | 60 months | **105 days** |

The Growth category is the more interesting of the two for most applicants: half the capital, a
shorter hold, and only three weeks in the country across the whole period.

Growth accepts direct investments, managed funds and philanthropy (philanthropy capped at 20% of the
total). Balanced adds listed equities, bonds and property developments. Funds must be invested within
six months of approval in principle, with a possible extension. A partner and dependent children aged
24 or younger can be included, Immigration New Zealand states no maximum age for the principal
applicant, and it reports an average processing time of 36 working days once documentation is complete.

## There is now a far cheaper route

The **Business Investor Work Visa** opened on **24 November 2025**, replacing the closed Entrepreneur
Work Visa:

- **NZ$1 million** → a three-year work-to-residence pathway
- **NZ$2 million** → fast-track to residence after **12 months**

It needs at least **25% ownership** of an established business with a purchase price of at least
NZ$1 million, and **three years' business experience** in self-employment or senior management. The
visa runs up to four years — a 12-month establishment stage and a three-year operating stage — and
both pathways lead to the Business Investor Resident Visa; on the NZ$2 million pathway the business
must still be run for three years in total across both visas. The application fee is **NZ$12,380**.
Updated on **6 July 2026** to allow franchises, purchase through a New Zealand resident entity, and
gifted capital.

If NZ$5 million is out of reach, this is the route to look at. The Entrepreneur Work Visa it replaced
is closed to new applications.

## New Zealand citizenship and your Indian passport

New Zealand **does** permit dual citizenship. **India does not.** So the constraint here is entirely
on the Indian side.

New Zealand citizenship becomes possible after **1,350 days over five years**, with at least 240 days
in each of those years. Taking it would end your Indian citizenship automatically under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, regardless of New Zealand's own generosity on the point.

Residence and permanent residence carry no such consequence. Most Indian families stop at permanent
residence for exactly that reason, and there is nothing wrong with doing so.
