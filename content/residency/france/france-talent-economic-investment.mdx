## Overview

The **direct economic investment** talent card is the closest France comes to an investor route, and it is deliberately not a golden visa. You invest at least **€300,000** in tangible or intangible fixed assets in France — plant, premises, equipment, qualifying intangibles — either in your own name or through a company in which you hold **at least 30% of the capital**. Alongside the investment you undertake to create or preserve employment.

What you receive is a **carte de séjour pluriannuelle "talent"** valid for up to four years, carrying the right to work and automatic accompanying-family rights for a spouse and minor children.

## How this differs from a golden visa

| | France, direct investment | A typical golden visa |
|---|---|---|
| **What the money buys** | Fixed assets of a business you control | Property, a fund unit, or a contribution |
| **Who holds the risk** | You, as an equity investor | You, but often with a promoter's buy-back promise |
| **Job commitment** | Required | Rarely required |
| **Return** | Whatever the business earns | Frequently marketed as fixed |
| **Right to work** | Included | Often excluded |
| **Card length** | Up to 4 years, renewable | Commonly 1–2 years, renewable |

## Who this suits

Someone who intends to own and run something in France, and for whom residence is the consequence of that rather than the purpose of it. If your goal is a European base with no operating involvement, this is the wrong route and Portugal or Greece will fit better — we will tell you that at the assessment stage rather than after a fee.
