## Overview

The **innovative economic project** talent card is the French startup route. Unlike almost every other European option marketed to founders, it specifies **no minimum investment**. What it asks instead is that the project be **recognised as innovative by the ministry responsible for the economy**, and that you can support yourself at around **€22,404.20** a year while you build it.

The card runs up to four years, carries the right to work, and brings a spouse and minor children in on talent-famille cards.

## The gate is recognition, not money

This is the point most applicants get wrong. Having capital does not help here, and lacking it does not hurt. The decision rests on whether the ministry accepts the project as innovative — which turns on novelty, the strength of the team, and whether the venture adds something the French market does not already have.

That has two consequences worth planning around. First, the preparation effort goes into the project case rather than into proving funds. Second, the timeline is set by an assessment you do not control, so it is less predictable than a route with a cash threshold.

## The salaried alternative

If you would join an innovative French company rather than found one, a separate talent route covers exactly that: employment by a **jeune entreprise innovante** or a company recognised as innovative by the economy ministry, at gross annual pay of **at least €39,582**. With an offer in hand this is the faster path by a wide margin, and it reaches the same multi-year talent card.
