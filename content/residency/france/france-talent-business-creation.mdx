## Overview

The **business creation** talent card is for founders who intend to build and run something in France. It asks for three things together: a **master's-level qualification or five years of professional experience at a comparable level**, a **real and serious business project**, and **at least €30,000 of financing** behind that project. You also need personal resources at least equal to the annual gross SMIC, currently stated as **€22,404.20** — that is what you live on, separate from what the business runs on.

The card is issued for up to four years, carries the right to work, and brings a spouse and minor children in on talent-famille cards without the ordinary family reunification procedure.

## The number is not the test

Thirty thousand euros is a low bar by European standards, and it is tempting to read it as the whole requirement. It is not. The decisive words in the criteria are **real and serious**, and that is an assessment of the plan: does the market exist, do the numbers work, does the applicant have the background to execute it, and is the capital proportionate to what is being attempted.

We have seen files clear €30,000 comfortably and fail on viability. Build the plan for the business, then check it clears the floor — not the other way round.

## Choosing between this and the innovative project route

| | Business creation | Innovative economic project |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum financing** | €30,000 | None specified |
| **External approval** | Not required | Must be recognised by the economy ministry |
| **Best for** | Trading businesses, services, acquisitions | Technology and research-led ventures |
| **Main risk** | Viability assessment | Obtaining ministry recognition |
| **Card length** | Up to 4 years | Up to 4 years |
| **Family rights** | Talent-famille | Talent-famille |
