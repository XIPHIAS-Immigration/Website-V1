## Overview
The UAE Golden Visa through Real Estate Investment offers long-term residency by purchasing property with a minimum value of **AED 2,000,000**. This route provides a renewable 10-year residency, family inclusion (spouse, unmarried children of any age, and parents), and access to the UAE's world-class business and lifestyle ecosystem. No physical presence is required to maintain your residency.

There is also a **2-year property investor visa** for owners of any completed property in Dubai — as of April 2026, Dubai removed the minimum property value threshold for this shorter-term visa.

## Investment Tiers
| Visa | Duration | Minimum Property Value | Property Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Property Investor Visa | 2 years (renewable) | No minimum (Dubai, April 2026) | Completed only |
| Senior Investor Visa | 5 years (renewable) | AED 1,000,000 | Age 55+ investors only |
| Golden Visa | 10 years (renewable) | AED 2,000,000 | Completed or off-plan (RERA-registered) |
