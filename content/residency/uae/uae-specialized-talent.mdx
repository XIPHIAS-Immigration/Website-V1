## Overview
The UAE Specialized Talent / Influencer / Student route grants long-term residency to individuals who can **evidence national or international recognition** in their field. Family members (spouse and children under **25**) can be included. The pathway is **achievement-based** — **no monetary investment** is required.

### Eligibility Overview
Applicants qualify by presenting a robust **evidence dossier** aligned to their category:
- **Professionals (scientists, doctors, academics):** publications, patents, licenses, body memberships, awards.  
- **Creatives & artists:** notable exhibitions, awards, press coverage, industry endorsements.  
- **Influencers/creators:** verified audience metrics, brand collaborations, platform credentials, compliance.  
- **Outstanding students:** high rankings, competition wins, scholarships, or exceptional transcripts.
