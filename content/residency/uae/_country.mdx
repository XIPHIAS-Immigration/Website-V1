## What the UAE golden visa actually requires

The governing instrument is the **Annex to Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022**. Golden residence is a
**10-year renewable permit** — the UAE has no permanent residency at all.

| Route | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Property | **AED 2,000,000** (a local-bank mortgage is acceptable) |
| Public investment — deposit, company capital or partnership share | **AED 2,000,000** |
| Annual tax paid | **AED 250,000** |
| Entrepreneur — pilot project revenue | **AED 1,000,000** a year |
| Entrepreneur — prior venture sold | **AED 7,000,000** |
| Skilled worker | from **AED 30,000** a month |

**One thing to get right on off-plan property.** The federal rule permits buying off-plan from an
approved local developer. But the **Dubai Land Department requires an e-Certificate of Title** in
practice, so the federal rule and Dubai's practice are not the same thing. Anyone telling you off-plan
is straightforwardly fine is describing the regulation, not the counter.

## Why this suits Indian families particularly well

The UAE is the **only major destination where the outcome can never cost you your Indian
citizenship** — because there is nothing to acquire. Golden residence is a renewable permit, there is
no permanent residency, and UAE citizenship exists only by government nomination through the Rulers'
Courts and Cabinet. An investor cannot apply for it, and ten years of residence does not lead to it.

For a family that wants a stable base, schooling, banking and regional mobility without ever facing
the Section 9(1) decision, that structure is the point rather than a limitation.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.

## A warning about the "lifetime golden visa" you may have read about

In mid-2025 a story circulated widely in the Indian press about a new **nomination-based UAE golden
visa** available from outside the UAE, on relaxed conditions, through consultancies, for a one-off fee.

**The UAE's own federal authority denied it.** On **8 July 2025** the Federal Authority for Identity,
Citizenship, Customs and Port Security confirmed the rumours were untrue, and said in terms that:

- **all golden residency applications are managed exclusively through official government channels
  inside the UAE**, and no advisory entity, inside or outside the country, is an approved party in the
  process
- the reports had come from **a consultancy office in another country**, and described conditions with
  **no basis in law**
- **legal action would be taken** against those spreading the rumours in order to obtain money
  unlawfully
- applicants should **not pay any sum or hand over personal documents** to anyone claiming to provide
  that service

A nomination route to golden residence does exist. It is run by the government, **it is free of
charge**, and it is not restricted by nationality. If anyone offers to sell you a UAE lifetime golden
visa, that is the moment to stop.
