## Overview
The **USA EB-5 TEA Investment route** provides a path to U.S. residency and Green Card through a lower investment of **$800,000** in a Targeted Employment Area. This route allows location-specific projects, often via Regional Centers, and promotes job creation in rural or high-unemployment regions.

## Investment Overview
- **Minimum Investment:** $800,000  
- **Type:** Investment in a TEA project (Regional Center or Direct)  
- **Requirement:** Create at least **10 full-time jobs**  
- **Risk:** Investment must remain "at risk" with no guaranteed returns  

## Comparison Between Investment Options
| Feature                  | TEA Investment ($800,000) | Non-TEA Investment ($1,050,000) |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Minimum Investment        | **$800,000**              | **$1,050,000**                  |
| Eligible Locations        | Rural, high-unemployment or infrastructure project | Any U.S. location |
| Risk Level                | Moderate, location-limited| Higher capital exposure         |
| Job Creation Requirement  | 10 full-time jobs         | 10 full-time jobs               |
| Involvement               | Regional Center or Direct | Regional Center or Direct       |

## Why EB-5 is different for Indian applicants right now

Every other employment-based green card puts an Indian national in a queue. EB-5 set-asides do not.

From the **October 2026 Visa Bulletin**:

| Category | India final action date | Wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EB-5 rural (20% set-aside) | **Current** | **None** |
| EB-5 high unemployment (10% set-aside) | **Current** | **None** |
| EB-5 infrastructure (2% set-aside) | **Current** | **None** |
| EB-5 unreserved | 01 Dec 2023 | 2 years 10 months |
| EB-2 (for comparison) | 01 Nov 2013 | **12 years 11 months** |
| EB-3 (for comparison) | 01 Jan 2014 | **12 years 9 months** |

Because a visa is immediately available in the set-aside categories, Indian applicants already
lawfully in the United States can **file Form I-485 concurrently** with the I-526E, under INA
section 245(n) as amended by the Reform and Integrity Act. That is the single strongest feature of
this route and it exists only while the set-aside stays current.

## The two investment levels

| Route | Minimum investment | Jobs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Targeted Employment Area, rural, or infrastructure project | **USD 800,000** | 10 full-time |
| Standard (non-TEA) | **USD 1,050,000** | 10 full-time |

The codified regulation at 8 CFR 204.6 still shows USD 1,800,000 and USD 900,000. Those are the
**vacated 2019 figures**; the regulation was not updated after the 2022 Act, and the operative
amounts are the statutory ones above.

## Filing fees drop at the end of November 2026

A final rule published **30 September 2026** takes effect about **29 November 2026**:

| Form | Now | From about 29 Nov 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I-526E initial | USD 11,160 | **USD 7,850** |
| I-526 | USD 11,160 | **USD 7,615** |
| I-829 | USD 9,525 | **USD 5,000** |

An EB-5 Integrity Fund fee of **USD 1,000 per I-526E** applies on top. Premium processing is **not
available** for any EB-5 form.

If your filing is not urgent, waiting until after 29 November 2026 saves roughly USD 3,300 on the
I-526E. We will tell you when that trade-off is worth it and when it is not.

## What to watch
- The 2022 Act provides for **periodic inflation adjustment** of the USD 800,000 and USD 1,050,000
  thresholds. An increase would be material, so XIPHIAS confirms the threshold in force before you
  invest.
- A DHS **proposed rule of 2 July 2026** would add promoter registration, a revocable living trust
  requirement, remove repaid bridge financing as job-creation evidence, and create a penalties and
  debarment regime. It is **not yet in force**.
- Set-aside categories are current **today**. They are a quota, and quotas fill.
