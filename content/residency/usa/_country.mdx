## One thing to be clear about: the E-2 visa

**India is not a US treaty country.** It appears nowhere in the State Department's treaty countries
table, for either E-1 or E-2. An Indian passport holder **cannot obtain an E-2 treaty investor visa
on Indian nationality** — at least half the US enterprise must be owned by nationals of a treaty
country.

Some Indian families do reach E-2 by first acquiring a qualifying second citizenship, for example
[Grenada](/citizenship/grenada) or [Turkey](/citizenship/turkey). That is a legitimate two-step
plan with its own cost and timeline, and we are happy to map it out. An E-2 application on Indian
nationality alone is not possible.
