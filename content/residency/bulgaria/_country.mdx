## Two things about Bulgaria that most pages get wrong

**There is no Bulgarian citizenship by investment.** It was repealed by an amending law published in
the State Gazette, issue 26 of **1 April 2022**, which struck out articles 12a and 14a of the
Citizenship Act. Pages still offering a Bulgarian passport for an investment are selling something
that stopped existing four years ago.

**But Bulgaria joined Schengen in full on 1 January 2025**, and adopted the **euro on 1 January
2026**. Both are genuinely good news for a residence applicant, and both post-date most of the
marketing material in circulation — including, until this review, some of ours.

One consequence of the euro changeover: every figure previously quoted in leva has been
redenominated, and the official sources we could reach still carry undated lev amounts that disagree
with each other. Rather than publish a euro figure we cannot source, we confirm the current amount
per file. Ask us and we will get it in writing before you commit anything.

## One thing to settle before you start

These are **residency** programmes, not citizenship. That distinction matters more for an Indian
passport holder than for most, because **India does not permit dual citizenship**: under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, voluntarily acquiring another citizenship ends your Indian citizenship
automatically.

Holding European **residency** does not affect your Indian citizenship at all. Only naturalising
would. So the residence permit is the safe part; the decision point comes years later, if and when
you choose to naturalise.
