## Two options, and one that people wrongly assume exists

Hungary's **Guest Investor Programme** opened on **1 July 2024**. There are exactly two qualifying
investments:

| Option | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Units in a real estate investment fund registered with the Hungarian National Bank | **€250,000** |
| Donation to a Hungarian higher education institution maintained by a public trust | **€1,000,000** |

**Buying a house or flat directly does not qualify.** It was discussed while the programme was being
designed and is still repeated on plenty of pages, but it is not on the official list.

The fund must hold at least **40% of its net asset value in Hungarian residential property**, and the
units must be held for **five years**.

The permit itself is unusually long: **10 years, extendable once by another 10**.

## One thing to settle before you start

These are **residency** programmes, not citizenship. That distinction matters more for an Indian
passport holder than for most, because **India does not permit dual citizenship**: under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, voluntarily acquiring another citizenship ends your Indian citizenship
automatically.

Holding European **residency** does not affect your Indian citizenship at all. Only naturalising
would. So the residence permit is the safe part; the decision point comes years later, if and when
you choose to naturalise.
