## What the Global Investor Programme actually requires

| Option | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **A** — business | **S$10 million** into a new or existing Singapore business |
| **B** — fund | **S$25 million** into a select GIP fund |
| **C** — family office | Singapore Single Family Office with **at least S$200 million AUM**, of which **at least S$50 million deployed and maintained in Singapore** within 12 months |

Option C is S$200 million under management with a S$50 million Singapore-deployment condition. The
S$50 million must be deployed within 12 months of Final Approval and kept in Singapore for as long as
the Re-Entry Permit runs. The deployment condition was introduced on **15 March 2023**, when Options A
and B were also raised from S$2.5 million; the S$200 million figure itself did not change.

## Who qualifies, and what it costs to apply

| Applicant profile | Track record and financials | Options open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Established business owner | At least 3 years' entrepreneurial track record; company turnover of at least **S$200 million** in the last year and on average over the last 3 years; at least 30% shareholding; approved industry | A, B or C |
| Next-generation business owner | Immediate family's company turnover of at least **S$500 million** in the last year and on average over 3 years; family holds at least 30% or is the largest shareholder; applicant on the C-suite or Board | C only |
| Founder of a fast-growth company | Founder and one of the largest individual shareholders; company valued at **S$500 million** or more, backed by reputable venture capital or private equity investors; approved industry | A, B or C |
| Family office principal | At least 5 years' entrepreneurial, investment or management track record; net investible assets of at least **S$200 million**, real estate excluded | C only |

EDB charges a non-refundable application fee of **S$10,000**, and processing takes around 12 months.
Approval-in-principle is valid for six months to make the investment, and you then have 12 months from
Final Approval to formalise PR. The Re-Entry Permit runs **five years**. It is renewed for five more
where the investment, employment and residence conditions for your option are all met — residence
meaning you or all your dependants live in Singapore more than half the time — or for three years on a
softer test.

## The salary routes, and a date to plan around

- **ONE Pass** — S$30,000 a month, five years, renewable for five. A new **AI and Tech Track** opens
  in **January 2027**, replacing Tech.Pass, where the S$30,000 can include at least S$22,500 fixed
  salary plus vested non-cash.
- **Employment Pass** — S$5,600 general, S$6,200 financial services, rising with age to S$10,700 and
  S$11,800 at 45 and over. **COMPASS** requires 40 points.

**Plan around 1 January 2027.** The Employment Pass thresholds rise to **S$6,000** general and
**S$6,600** financial services, with renewals following on 1 January 2028. If your timeline crosses
that date, budget for the higher number.

## Citizenship, and what it would cost you
Singapore citizenship becomes possible **two years after permanent residency** — but **Singapore does
not permit dual citizenship**, and India does not either. Taking Singaporean citizenship would end
your Indian citizenship automatically under Section 9(1) of the Citizenship Act 1955. Permanent
residency has no such effect, which is why most Indian families stop there.
