## The current MPRP figures

Malta's Permanent Residence Programme was repriced twice, by Legal Notice 310/2024 and Legal Notice
146/2025. As the consolidated law stands today:

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Administrative fee | **€60,000** |
| Government contribution | **€37,000** |
| Qualifying property — purchase | **€375,000** |
| Qualifying property — lease | **€14,000 per year** |
| Donation | €2,000 |

**The Gozo and South Malta discount has been abolished** — there is a single national threshold now.

Two cautions. Our own pages carried a **€40,000** administrative fee until this review. And
**Residency Malta's own January 2025 brochure and agents' handbook still show superseded figures** —
if a consultant quotes you from those, they are quoting a repealed schedule.

## One thing to settle before you start

These are **residency** programmes, not citizenship. That distinction matters more for an Indian
passport holder than for most, because **India does not permit dual citizenship**: under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, voluntarily acquiring another citizenship ends your Indian citizenship
automatically.

Holding European **residency** does not affect your Indian citizenship at all. Only naturalising
would. So the residence permit is the safe part; the decision point comes years later, if and when
you choose to naturalise.

## About Maltese citizenship by investment

On **29 April 2025** the Grand Chamber of the Court of Justice of the European Union ruled against
Malta in Case C-181/23, holding that its investor naturalisation scheme breached Article 20 TFEU and
Article 4(3) TEU — the Court described it as the commercialisation of the grant of Member State
nationality.

Malta responded with **Act XXI of 2025**, published 24 July 2025. The Government's own statement is
that the amendments **discontinue the programme entirely**, and that all references to the
programme, the transaction and the agents involved have been removed from the law.

A route called "citizenship by merit" exists under older legislation, but the Maltese agency
responsible states expressly that it does **not permit any marketing or promotion** of it, and that
it is *neither a programme nor a scheme, pathway, continuation, or alternative*.

**So there is no Maltese citizenship-by-investment product, and we will not market one.** If you have
been offered Maltese citizenship for an investment by anyone in 2026, that is a reason to walk away.
The MPRP described on this page is a **residence** programme and is unaffected by the ruling.
