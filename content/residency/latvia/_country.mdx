## Latvia's investment routes changed on 15 September 2026

A **new Immigration Law** replaced the 2002 Act and abolished two of its three remaining investment
routes. The government bonds route had already been removed.

| Route | Status |
| --- | --- |
| [Real estate purchase](/residency/latvia/latvia-real-estate-investment) | **Abolished** 15 Sep 2026 |
| [Bank subordinated liabilities](/residency/latvia/latvia-bank-deposit) | **Abolished** 15 Sep 2026 |
| [Government bonds](/residency/latvia/latvia-government-bonds) | **Closed** — removed before the 2026 law |
| [Company share capital](/residency/latvia/latvia-business-investment) | **Open** — €50,000 / €100,000 + €10,000 state fee |
| Alternative investment fund €150,000 | Provided for in law, **fund not yet established** |

The residence permit granted under the new law runs **two years**, reduced from five.

**A warning about sources.** Latvia's own PMLP investment pages are still dated **April 2022** and
still describe the abolished €280,000 route. The governing statement is PMLP's news notice of
15 September 2026, linked below.

## One thing to settle before you start

These are **residency** programmes, not citizenship. That distinction matters more for an Indian
passport holder than for most, because **India does not permit dual citizenship**: under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, voluntarily acquiring another citizenship ends your Indian citizenship
automatically.

Holding European **residency** does not affect your Indian citizenship at all. Only naturalising
would. So the residence permit is the safe part; the decision point comes years later, if and when
you choose to naturalise.

## What is still available in Latvia

The **[company share capital route](/residency/latvia/latvia-business-investment)** survives: €50,000
into a small Latvian company, or €100,000 into a larger one, plus a **€10,000 state fee**. Note that
the permit now runs **two years**, not five.

A **€150,000 alternative investment fund** route exists in the new law, but the fund itself has not
been established, so nothing can be invested in it yet.

If Latvia was on your list for the property route, the closest live equivalents in Europe are
**[Greece](/residency/greece)** and **[Hungary](/residency/hungary)**.
