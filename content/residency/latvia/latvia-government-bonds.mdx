## This route is closed

The government securities route had already been removed from Latvia's Immigration Law before the
**new Immigration Law**, adopted on 20 August 2026 and **in force since 15 September 2026**, replaced
the 2002 Act. The new law does not provide for it.

The new law also ended the real estate and bank routes. The Office of Citizenship and Migration
Affairs states it directly: the law no longer provides for temporary residence permits to be granted
to purchasers of real estate, or to investors in subordinated liabilities of credit institutions.

**No new application can be made on this route.** Permits already granted under it remain valid
until expiry and are then renewed under the transitional rules of the new law.

A caution worth having: Latvia's own PMLP investment pages are still dated **April 2022** and still
describe the abolished routes. They are not current; PMLP's notice of 15 September 2026 governs.

## One thing to settle before you start

These are **residency** programmes, not citizenship. That distinction matters more for an Indian
passport holder than for most, because **India does not permit dual citizenship**: under Section 9(1)
of the Citizenship Act 1955, voluntarily acquiring another citizenship ends your Indian citizenship
automatically.

Holding European **residency** does not affect your Indian citizenship at all. Only naturalising
would. So the residence permit is the safe part; the decision point comes years later, if and when
you choose to naturalise.

## What is still available in Latvia

The **[company share capital route](/residency/latvia/latvia-business-investment)** survives: €50,000
into a small Latvian company, or €100,000 into a larger one, plus a **€10,000 state fee**. Note that
the permit now runs **two years**, not five.

A **€150,000 alternative investment fund** route exists in the new law, but the fund itself has not
been established, so nothing can be invested in it yet.

If Latvia was on your list, the closest live investor-residence routes in Europe are
**[Greece](/residency/greece)** and **[Hungary](/residency/hungary)**.
