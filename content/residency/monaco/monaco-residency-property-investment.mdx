## No fixed sum is prescribed

Monaco law prescribes no minimum deposit or investment for residence. The Government states the
financial condition simply as *sufficient financial resources*, evidenced by an attestation from a
Monaco bank confirming that you have the means to reside in Monaco. **The sum regarded as sufficient
is set by the Monaco bank that provides the attestation.** Figures of around €500,000 reflect typical
private-banking practice rather than a legal threshold, and they vary from bank to bank.

The Government also accepts *any other evidence* of sufficient resources, subject to approval by the
Section des Résidents.

## What is required
- **Accommodation** in Monaco, large enough for the people living in it — owned, held through a
  company, rented, or shared with a relative, spouse or partner
- **A bank attestation** from a Monaco establishment, less than one month old
- **A criminal record extract** less than three months old, from every country you have lived in over
  the past five years

Applies to everyone **16 and over** staying more than three months a year.

## On Monegasque citizenship
Ten years of residence, granted by **individual Sovereign Ordinance at the Prince's discretion**, and
**renunciation of your existing nationality is required**. In practice Monaco is a residency, not a
route to a second passport, and it should be chosen on that basis.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.
