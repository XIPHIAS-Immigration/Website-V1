## There is no official minimum — and that matters

Almost every page about Monaco quotes **€500,000** as the required deposit. **It is not a legal
requirement.** Monaco's government page states the condition as simply *sufficient financial
resources*, and says in terms that **the sum judged sufficient depends on the Monaco bank that
provides your attestation**.

So €500,000 is one bank's policy, quoted as though it were the law. Different banks set different
thresholds, and the government also accepts *any other evidence* of sufficient resources, subject to
approval by the Section des Résidents.

We have corrected our own pages, which carried the €500,000 figure as if it were prescribed.

## What is actually required
- **Accommodation** in Monaco, large enough for the people living in it
- **A bank attestation** from a Monaco establishment, less than one month old
- **A criminal record extract** less than three months old, from every country you have lived in over
  the past five years

Applies to everyone **16 and over** staying more than three months a year.

| Card | Duration | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporaire | 1 year | First application |
| Ordinaire | 3 years | After 3 years' residence |
| Privilégiée | 10 years | After 10 years |

## On Monegasque citizenship
Ten years of residence, granted by **individual Sovereign Ordinance at the Prince's discretion**, and
**renunciation of your previous nationality is required**. In practice Monaco is a residency, not a
route to a second passport, and it should be chosen on that basis.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.
