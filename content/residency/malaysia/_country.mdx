## MM2H has four categories, not three

| Category | Fixed deposit | Property purchase | Pass | Participation fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Platinum** | **USD 1,000,000** | RM2,000,000 or above | 20 years | RM200,000 |
| **Gold** | **USD 500,000** | RM1,000,000 or above | 15 years | RM3,000 |
| **Silver** | **USD 150,000** | RM600,000 or above | 5 years | RM1,000 |
| **SEZ / SFZ** — Forest City, Johor | **USD 65,000** (ages 21–49) or **USD 32,000** (50+) | In Forest City, at the price set for SEZ developments | 10 years | RM1,000 |

**Property purchase is mandatory in every federal tier**, and the property cannot be sold for 10 years
(upgrading to a higher-value property is allowed). Up to 50% of the fixed deposit can be withdrawn after
approval to buy a residence, or for education, medical and tourism spending in Malaysia.

The minimum age is 25 (21 for SEZ/SFZ). Every tier requires 90 days a year in Malaysia, cumulatively —
applicants aged 25 to 49 may meet this between principal, spouse and dependants, and there is no
minimum stay from age 50. Only **Platinum** permits work and business. A medical check-up is
mandatory, and pass validity is capped by passport expiry.

The Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture states the Platinum, Gold and Silver terms are accurate as of
22 July 2024; the SEZ/SFZ category was added later, and the Ministry's category pages were last
updated on 10 February 2026.

## Sarawak-MM2H is a different programme, and often a better one

Sarawak runs its own scheme with its own criteria, revised from **1 January 2025**:

- minimum age **30**
- fixed deposit **RM500,000**
- offshore income **RM10,000 a month**, or RM15,000 with a dependant
- a **5 + 5 year** pass
- **30 days a year** physically in Sarawak
- **property purchase optional** — RM600,000 in Kuching, RM500,000 elsewhere

That last point is the real differentiator: federal MM2H makes property compulsory, Sarawak does not.
From 16 May 2025, personal sponsors for Sarawak-MM2H are limited to immediate family.

## What MM2H is not

It is a **long-stay visit pass**. It does not lead to permanent residency and it does not lead to
citizenship. Malaysia does not permit dual citizenship, and Malaysian citizenship under Article 19
needs ten of the previous twelve years' residence plus adequate Malay, granted at discretion.

Your Indian citizenship is entirely safe on MM2H — precisely because MM2H never becomes a
citizenship. It is not a route to a Malaysian passport.

## On the Premium Visa Programme

The Immigration Department's published **PVIP** criteria are an offshore income of RM40,000 a month,
a RM1,000,000 fixed deposit, a pass of up to 20 years with no age limit, and permission to work and run
a legal business. Applications go through authorised PVIP agencies. The Department does not publish
PVIP's current operating status and the programme does not appear in its main visa index, so XIPHIAS
confirms the position with an authorised agency before any application.
