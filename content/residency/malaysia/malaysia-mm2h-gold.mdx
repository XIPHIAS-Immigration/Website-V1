## MM2H has four categories, not three

| Category | Note |
| --- | --- |
| **Platinum** | Highest tier |
| **Gold** | |
| **Silver** | Entry tier |
| **SEZ / SFZ** — Forest City, Johor | Fixed deposit **USD 65,000** (ages 21–49) or **USD 32,000** (50+), **10-year pass** |

The Special Economic Zone category is the one most pages miss, including ours until this review. The
Ministry states the Platinum, Gold and Silver terms are accurate as of 22 July 2024; the SEZ/SFZ
category is absent from that document, so we do not publish an effective date for it.

**Property purchase is mandatory in every federal tier.**

## Sarawak-MM2H is a different programme, and often a better one

Sarawak runs its own scheme with its own criteria, revised from **1 January 2025**:

- minimum age **30**
- fixed deposit **RM500,000**
- offshore income **RM10,000 a month**, or RM15,000 with a dependant
- a **5 + 5 year** pass
- **30 days a year** physically in Sarawak
- **property purchase optional** — RM600,000 in Kuching, RM500,000 elsewhere

That last point is the real differentiator: federal MM2H makes property compulsory, Sarawak does not.
From 16 May 2025, personal sponsors for Sarawak-MM2H are limited to immediate family.

## What MM2H is not

It is a **long-stay visit pass**. It does not lead to permanent residency and it does not lead to
citizenship. Malaysia does not permit dual citizenship, and Malaysian citizenship under Article 19
needs ten of the previous twelve years' residence plus adequate Malay, granted at discretion.

Your Indian citizenship is entirely safe on MM2H — precisely because MM2H never becomes a
citizenship. If someone has told you it is a route to a Malaysian passport, they are wrong.

## On the Premium Visa Programme

We cannot currently tell you whether **PVIP** is open. Its FAQ and authorised-agency list are live on
the Immigration e-services portal but carry no date, and PVIP does not appear in the Department's main
visa index. Rather than guess, we ask the Department directly for each enquiry.
