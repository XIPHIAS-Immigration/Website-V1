## There is no investment threshold for Uruguayan residency

This is the most common misunderstanding about Uruguay.

**Permanent legal residency requires only sufficient means of living.** Ley 18.250 and Decreto 394/009
state **no investment figure at all**.

The figures commonly quoted are **tax residency** thresholds — a different thing entirely, set in
**unidades indexadas**:

| Tax residency route | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Real estate | above **UI 15,000,000** |
| Promoted project | above **UI 45,000,000** |
| Real estate acquired on or after 1 July 2020, plus 60 days' presence | at least **UI 3,500,000** |
| Business from 1 July 2020, plus 15 new full-time jobs | at least **UI 15,000,000** |

The **unidad indexada was 6.6511 pesos on 30 September 2026**. Its value moves daily, so we quote the
thresholds in UI and confirm the dollar equivalent for each file.

## A caveat on citizenship
After three years of habitual residence with a family constituted in Uruguay, or five years without,
Uruguay grants **ciudadanía legal** rather than nationality, and Article 81 says Uruguayan nationality
is not lost even by naturalising elsewhere. Whether India treats *ciudadanía legal* as the voluntary
acquisition of a foreign citizenship under Section 9(1) is **unsettled**, so we ask clients to take advice
from Indian citizenship-law counsel before applying for it.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.
