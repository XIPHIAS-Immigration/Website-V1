## The Occupation Permit criteria changed

| Route | Current EDB criteria |
| --- | --- |
| **Investor** | **USD 100,000** initial, then MUR 5m turnover from year 3 and MUR 8m from year 5 |
| **Professional** | **MUR 50,000 a month**, standardised |
| **Self-employed** | MUR 2m from year 3, MUR 3m from year 5 |

These replace the older USD 50,000, MUR 30,000 and USD 35,000 figures still in wide circulation.

**An honest note on their legal status.** We publish these as the Economic Development Board's
**current published criteria** — because that is what they are. The 2025 Act does not amend the
Immigration Act, and the instrument that does is still a **Bill**. So we will not tell you these have
been law since a particular date, only that they are what the EDB currently applies.

## Property: USD 375,000, and one trap

**USD 375,000** under the IRS, RES, PDS or Smart City schemes buys a residence permit valid for as
long as you hold the property, with spouse and children under 24 included.

The trap: there is a **separate USD 500,000 non-citizen property route, and it expressly does not give
you a residence permit**. The guideline says so in terms. If someone is selling you USD 500,000
Mauritian property as a residency route, they are misdescribing it.

Also, since **13 December 2024, 85% of the price must be paid in Mauritian rupees**.

## Permanent residence — we are holding our figures
Two official Mauritian sources publish **different** permanent residence criteria. Rather than pick
one, we confirm with the EDB for each file. This is the single item on Mauritius we would most want in
writing before anyone commits.

## Coming, but not here yet
The 2026 Bill contains a **Golden Visa** at an aggregate **USD 1 million** in priority sectors, and an
AI City Scheme. Neither is confirmed in force, and we are not marketing either.

## Your Indian passport is safe on a residency

Section 9(1) of India's Citizenship Act 1955 ends Indian citizenship only on the **voluntary
acquisition of another country's citizenship**. Holding a residence permit, permanent residence or a
long-term visa does none of that.

So every route on this page is safe to hold. What differs between countries is the **endpoint** — and
we will tell you plainly where a route can never become a citizenship, where it can, and what taking
that step would cost you.
