## Overview
The **New Brunswick Business Immigration Stream**, run under the New Brunswick Provincial
Nominee Program (NBPNP), is for experienced entrepreneurs ready to establish, acquire or operate
a business in the province. It offers a route to **Canadian permanent residency** for applicants
who actively manage their business and contribute to the provincial economy.

**This stream replaced the NB Entrepreneurial Stream**, which New Brunswick has closed. The
province's own wording is that the Business Immigration Stream "has replaced the Entrepreneurial
Stream which is now closed and no longer accepting applications."

## Investment overview
You need a minimum eligible investment of **CAD $150,000** in a qualifying New Brunswick business
and a verified **net worth of CAD $500,000** — or **CAD $300,000** if the business is a farming
operation. An **exploratory visit** and an **approved business plan** are required before you
apply.

If you have seen CAD $250,000 and CAD $600,000 quoted for New Brunswick, those were the closed
Entrepreneurial Stream's figures. They no longer apply.
