## This programme is closed

The **Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP) Entrepreneur Stream** is not accepting
applications. Ontario's own page for the stream now carries two statements: that the page is no
longer current and is provided for archival and research purposes, and that the Entrepreneur
Stream is not accepting applications.

The closure is part of a wider OINP redesign into a consolidated **Workforce Priority Stream**.
No reopening date has been given.

## Why it closed

In October 2024 the federal government halved Provincial Nominee Program allocations for 2025,
from 110,000 to 55,000. Provinces absorbed that cut by closing their lowest-volume, most
discretionary streams — which meant business and entrepreneur streams first, because they use up
nominations while creating few jobs per nomination. Ontario went furthest of any province,
closing the Entrepreneur Stream outright and restructuring the whole OINP.

PNP allocations were partly restored for 2026, to 91,500. Ontario has not reopened the stream.

## What it required, for reference

These figures are historical.

| Requirement | Inside the GTA | Outside the GTA, or ICT sector |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum investment | CAD $600,000 | CAD $200,000 |
| Minimum net worth | CAD $800,000 | CAD $400,000 |
| Jobs to create | 2 full-time | 1 full-time |

Applicants also needed two years of business ownership or senior management experience in the
past five years, at least 33.3% ownership, CLB 4 in English or French, and had to live in Ontario
and actively manage the business. No deposit was required — it was a performance-based stream.

**A note on the CAD $200,000 figure:** many consultancy websites still quote it as *the* Ontario
minimum. It was only ever the outside-GTA and ICT tier, and it is now historical either way.

## Routes into Canada that are still open

Canada has not closed down — the money-based federal routes closed, while the skills and
provincial routes grew. For 2026 the federal government raised Provincial Nominee Program
admissions to **91,500** (up from 55,000 in 2025) and held total permanent residence at 380,000.

**Provincial business streams still accepting applications:**

- [British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Base stream](/residency/canada/british-columbia-entrepreneur-base) — from CAD $200,000
- [British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Regional stream](/residency/canada/british-columbia-regional-pilot) — from CAD $100,000
- [Prince Edward Island — Work Permit Stream](/residency/canada/pei-work-permit) — from CAD $150,000
- [Newfoundland & Labrador — International Entrepreneur](/residency/canada/newfoundland-international-entrepreneur) — from CAD $200,000
- [Nova Scotia — Entrepreneur stream](/residency/canada/nova-scotia-entrepreneur-stream) — from CAD $100,000
- [Northwest Territories — Business Stream](/residency/canada/northwest-territories-business) — from CAD $100,000
- [New Brunswick — Business Immigration Stream](/residency/canada/new-brunswick-business-immigration) — from CAD $150,000
- [Manitoba — Business Investor Stream, Entrepreneur Pathway](/residency/canada/manitoba-entrepreneur)
- [Yukon — Business Nominee Program](/residency/canada/yukon-business) — from CAD $300,000
