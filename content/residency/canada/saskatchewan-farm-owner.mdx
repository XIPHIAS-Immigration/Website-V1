## This programme is permanently closed

The **Saskatchewan Farm Owner & Young Farmer Stream** no longer exists. Effective
**27 March 2025**, the SINP permanently closed all Entrepreneur and Farm immigration pathways.
No new applications are accepted, no new expressions of interest are considered, and no further
invitations to apply are issued.

## Why it closed

In October 2024 the federal government halved Provincial Nominee Program allocations for 2025,
from 110,000 to 55,000. Saskatchewan's share fell to **3,625 nominations — its lowest since
2009**. The province responded by permanently closing its entrepreneur and farm pathways, saying
so explicitly in its news release of 27 March 2025.

PNP allocations were partly restored for 2026, to 91,500 nationally. Saskatchewan has not
reopened these pathways, and described the closure as permanent rather than a pause.

## What it required, for reference

These figures are historical: a minimum farm investment of CAD $150,000, net worth of
CAD $500,000 for the Farm Owner route or CAD $300,000 for the Young Farmer route, and a
refundable good-faith deposit of CAD $75,000.

## Is there another farm route into Canada?

[Alberta's Farm Stream](/residency/canada/alberta-self-employed-farmer) is still technically open,
at CAD $500,000 net worth and CAD $500,000 equity. But Alberta has allocated only **60
nominations across all its entrepreneur streams for 2026, against 217 applications already in
process**. Read that page's risk note before treating it as a realistic route.

## Routes into Canada that are still open

Canada has not closed down — the money-based federal routes closed, while the skills and
provincial routes grew. For 2026 the federal government raised Provincial Nominee Program
admissions to **91,500** (up from 55,000 in 2025) and held total permanent residence at 380,000.

**Provincial business streams still accepting applications:**
