## Provincial pathways that are open

- **British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Base stream**
  Investment from CAD $200,000; net worth CAD $600,000. Drawing monthly through 2026.

- **British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Regional stream**
  Investment from CAD $100,000; net worth CAD $300,000. No longer a pilot — BC made it a
  permanent part of the BC PNP on 27 May 2024.

- **Prince Edward Island — Work Permit Stream**
  Investment from CAD $150,000; net worth CAD $600,000. PR through a work permit and a
  performance agreement.

- **Nova Scotia — Entrepreneur stream**
  Investment from CAD $150,000 inside Halifax or CAD $100,000 outside it; net worth CAD $600,000
  or CAD $400,000. A CAD $2,000 application fee applies from 1 September 2026.

- **New Brunswick — Business Immigration Stream**
  Investment from CAD $150,000; net worth CAD $500,000 (CAD $300,000 for farming). This stream
  replaced the closed Entrepreneurial Stream.

- **Newfoundland & Labrador — International Entrepreneur**
  Investment from CAD $200,000; operate the business for a year before nomination.

- **Northwest Territories — Business Stream**
  Investment from CAD $200,000 within Yellowknife or CAD $100,000 outside it; net worth
  CAD $500,000 or CAD $250,000. Exploratory visit and interview required; no deposit.

- **Manitoba — Business Investor Stream, Entrepreneur Pathway**
  Net worth CAD $500,000. Manitoba does not publish a fixed investment threshold — the amount is
  set individually in your Business Performance Agreement.

- **Alberta — Self-Employed Farmer Stream**
  Net worth and equity of CAD $500,000 in primary agriculture. Open, but only 60 nominations
  across all Alberta entrepreneur streams for 2026 against 217 applications already in process.

- **Yukon — Business Nominee Program**
  Investment from CAD $300,000; net worth CAD $500,000.

## Pathways that have closed

These are kept online so that anyone searching for them gets an accurate answer. None of them
accepts a new application.

- **Federal Start-Up Visa** — paused 30 June 2026; intake ended 31 December 2025.
- **Federal Self-Employed Persons Program** — intake closed 30 April 2024; pause extended
  indefinitely on 19 December 2025.
- **Ontario OINP Entrepreneur Stream** — closed; Ontario has archived the page.
- **Saskatchewan Entrepreneur Category** — permanently closed 27 March 2025.
- **Saskatchewan Farm Owner & Young Farmer Stream** — permanently closed 27 March 2025.

The federal Business category is set at **500 admissions a year** under the 2026-2028 Immigration
Levels Plan, which is the clearest explanation of why both federal entrepreneur routes were shut.
Provincial Nominee Program admissions, by contrast, were raised to **91,500 for 2026** after being
halved to 55,000 in 2025.
