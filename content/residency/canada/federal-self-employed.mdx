## This programme is paused

The **Federal Self-Employed Persons Program** is not accepting applications. IRCC stopped intake
on **30 April 2024** to clear its backlog, and on **19 December 2025** extended that pause
**"until further notice"**. The status banner on canada.ca reads *Paused*.

The programme has not been formally cancelled, which is why it still appears in older guides and
on other consultants' websites. With no intake and no reopening date, it cannot be applied for.

## What happened, and when

| Date | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 30 April 2024 | IRCC stopped accepting new applications, to clear the existing backlog. |
| 19 December 2025 | The pause was extended "until further notice" in IRCC's entrepreneur measures notice. |
| 2026 onwards | The 2026-2028 Levels Plan sets the Federal Business category at 500 admissions a year. No reopening date has been published. |

## What it was, for reference

The programme offered permanent residence to people with relevant experience in **cultural or
athletic activities** who intended to be self-employed in Canada and contribute to its cultural
or athletic life. It required **no monetary investment**. Eligibility rested on professional
achievement, recognition, and the intention and ability to be self-employed, plus enough funds to
support the applicant and their family after landing.

## If you already have an application in

IRCC continues to process files accepted before 30 April 2024. The pause does not cancel an
existing application. [Contact us](/contact) if you need help with a submitted file.

## Routes into Canada that are still open

Canada has not closed down — the money-based federal routes closed, while the skills and
provincial routes grew. For 2026 the federal government raised Provincial Nominee Program
admissions to **91,500** (up from 55,000 in 2025) and held total permanent residence at 380,000.
