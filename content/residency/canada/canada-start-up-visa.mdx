## This programme is closed

The **Canada Start-Up Visa (SUV)** is no longer available to new applicants. Immigration,
Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) paused the programme on **30 June 2026**. Its own
eligibility page now states that the programme was paused on that date and that IRCC will
only continue processing applications it accepted before then.

We have left this page online because people still search for the Start-Up Visa and deserve a
straight answer rather than a sales pitch for something that no longer exists.

## What happened, and when

| Date | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 19 December 2025 | IRCC published its entrepreneur measures notice. The optional SUV work permit stopped being accepted the same day, except extensions for people already in Canada on one. |
| 31 December 2025, 11:59 p.m. | Intake for new Start-Up Visa applications ended. |
| 30 June 2026 | Final deadline for applicants holding a valid 2025 commitment certificate. The programme was paused on this date. |
| 2026 | IRCC said a new, targeted pilot programme for immigrant entrepreneurs would follow. Nothing has launched on canada.ca as at 29 September 2026. |

The wider context: the 2026-2028 Immigration Levels Plan sets the **Federal Business category at
500 admissions a year** — down from 2,000 in 2025. That is the clearest explanation of why both
federal entrepreneur routes were shut.

## What it required, for reference

These figures are historical. They are published here so that anyone holding old paperwork can
check it, not as a current offer.

The minimum investment depended on which designated organisation backed the business:

| Backer | Minimum investment |
| --- | --- |
| Venture capital fund | CAD $200,000 |
| Angel investor group | CAD $75,000 |
| Business incubator | No investment required — acceptance into the programme was the requirement |

Other requirements were CLB 5 in all four language abilities, at least 10% of the voting rights
held by the applicant, and the applicant plus the designated organisation holding more than 50%
together. Settlement funds ranged from CAD $15,263 for one person to CAD $40,392 for seven,
plus CAD $4,112 for each additional family member.

## Routes into Canada that are still open

Canada has not closed down — the money-based federal routes closed, while the skills and
provincial routes grew. For 2026 the federal government raised Provincial Nominee Program
admissions to **91,500** (up from 55,000 in 2025) and set total permanent residence at 380,000.

**Provincial business streams still accepting applications:**

- [British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Base stream](/residency/canada/british-columbia-entrepreneur-base) — from CAD $200,000
- [British Columbia — Entrepreneur Immigration, Regional stream](/residency/canada/british-columbia-regional-pilot) — from CAD $100,000
- [Prince Edward Island — Work Permit Stream](/residency/canada/pei-work-permit) — from CAD $150,000
- [Newfoundland & Labrador — International Entrepreneur](/residency/canada/newfoundland-international-entrepreneur) — from CAD $200,000
- [Nova Scotia — Entrepreneur stream](/residency/canada/nova-scotia-entrepreneur-stream) — from CAD $100,000
- [Northwest Territories — Business Stream](/residency/canada/northwest-territories-business) — from CAD $100,000
- [New Brunswick — Business Immigration Stream](/residency/canada/new-brunswick-business-immigration) — from CAD $150,000
- [Manitoba — Business Investor Stream, Entrepreneur Pathway](/residency/canada/manitoba-entrepreneur)
- [Yukon — Business Nominee Program](/residency/canada/yukon-business) — from CAD $300,000

**Skilled and employment routes:**

- [Express Entry](/skilled/canada/express-entry) — four new occupation categories were added for 2026
- [Provincial Nominee Program](/skilled/canada/provincial-nominee-program)
- [Global Talent Stream](/skilled/canada/global-talent-stream) — two-week work permit processing
